# Add Charts to the Analytics View on Search (CRMLS)

**Description:** Open the Analytics view on the Search page and add Market Share, Distribution, Location, Trends, or Median charts to your results.

Use this article to open the Analytics view, add charts to it, and choose the chart category that fits your question.

## Open the Analytics view on the Search page

The Analytics view charts the listings in your current search, which means your filters decide what the charts show.

### Steps:

1. Click Search in the top navigation
2. Click Results in the upper right of the Search page
3. Click Analytics next to View, above the search results

The Analytics view opens and charts your current search results.

> Screenshot placeholder (CRMLS): Show the Results tab of the Search page with the Analytics button next to View highlighted | Alt text: The View ribbon above the search results, with the Analytics button highlighted next to the view icons.

The Analytics page in the top navigation runs separate reports. [Analytics FAQ](http://support.perchwell.com/baldwin/en/articles/15200594-analytics-faq) explains how the Analytics page differs from the Analytics view.

## Add charts to the Analytics view

Add charts to the Analytics view from the Charts window, which groups charts into five categories.

### Steps:

1. Click Charts in the upper right of the Analytics view
2. Click a category name to expand it
3. Select the checkbox next to each chart you want to add
4. Click Update Dashboard

The charts you selected are added to the Analytics view. A chart already in the view is greyed out and labeled Already added.

> Screenshot placeholder (CRMLS): Show the Charts window with the Distribution category expanded and its five charts listed | Alt text: The Charts window with Distribution expanded, listing five charts with checkboxes, and the Cancel and Update Dashboard buttons below.

The Analytics view holds up to 8 charts at a time. [Analytics FAQ](http://support.perchwell.com/baldwin/en/articles/15200594-analytics-faq) explains how to delete a chart when 8 are showing.

## Choose a chart category for the Analytics view

The Charts window groups charts for the Analytics view into five categories:

- Market Share: Chart your results by Bedrooms or Price.
- Distribution: See how your results spread across a range with DAM by Price Segment [confirm: what DAM stands for], Price, Price by Price Segment, Price per SqFt, or Square Feet.
- Location: Compare Days Active in MLS, Listing Count, or Price across areas.
- Trends: Chart Market Inventory, which is labeled Monthly.
- Median: See the median of a measure with Days Active in MLS, List Price, List Price per SqFt, Price, Price per Acre, Price per SqFt, or Sold Price.
