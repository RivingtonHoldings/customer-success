# User Settings FAQ

**Description:** Answers on updating your Perchwell contact details and profile picture, and why assistants see only the Dashboard until given access.

Use this article to update your name, email address, or phone number, change your profile picture, and get full Perchwell access as a personal or office assistant.

## How do I change my name, email address, or phone number in Perchwell?

Contact Baldwin MLS staff to change your name, email address, or phone number in Perchwell. Call (251) 270-0328 or email [mls@baldwinrealtors.com](mailto:mls@baldwinrealtors.com). Perchwell support at [support@perchwell.com](mailto:support@perchwell.com) can also help.

MLS staff manage the email address that shows on your listings, so contact them to correct it there too.

## How do I change my profile picture in Perchwell?

Change your profile picture from Edit profile on the User settings page.

1. Hover over your profile picture in the upper right

![Alt text: The top navigation in Perchwell, with the profile picture highlighted in the upper right.](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/trq7czv5/2581504680/0234bcaaa3a0af9e84813110115e/Screenshot+2026-08-02+at+10_58_16%E2%80%AFAM.png)

2. Click Settings

![Alt text: The profile menu open below the profile picture, with Settings highlighted above Report library and My brokerage.](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/trq7czv5/2581505408/e82dbe0dfe9fbe178f906f626c49/Screenshot+2026-08-02+at+10_59_21%E2%80%AFAM+1.png)

3. Click Edit profile in the upper right
4. Click your profile picture in the upper left
5. Upload an image from your computer

> Screenshot placeholder: Show the Edit profile view of the User settings page with the profile picture highlighted in the upper left | Alt text: The Edit profile view of the User settings page, with the profile picture highlighted in the upper left.

Perchwell saves your new profile picture automatically.

Members who came from a legacy platform may know the User settings page as the About Me page.

## Why do I only see the Dashboard when I log in to Perchwell as an assistant?

Personal and office assistants see only the Dashboard until their agent or broker grants them permission to log in as them. [Log In as an Agent with Assume Identity](https://support.perchwell.com/baldwin/en/articles/16053168-assume-identity) explains how an agent grants access and how you then work in their Perchwell account.
