# Client Collaboration FAQ

**Description:** Answers to common client collaboration questions about invited clients, messages, sharing listings, Tags, saved search alerts, and contacts.

Use this article to answer common client collaboration questions about invited clients and the client app, messages and emails, sharing listings, Tags, saved searches and alerts, and contacts.

## What is an invited client in Perchwell?

An invited client is a client you invited to Perchwell. Accepting the invitation gives them a client portal, their own workspace for collaborating with you. In the client portal, invited clients:

- Message you and view the listings you share in Messages
- React to each shared listing with Like, Maybe, or Reject
- Save and organize listings they like
- Track listing updates with saved searches you share or they create

[Invite Clients to Perchwell](http://support.perchwell.com/baldwin/en/articles/11373895-invite-clients-to-perchwell) explains how to send or resend an invitation.

## Do clients need a Perchwell login to view listings I share?

Clients do not need a Perchwell account or login to view listings you share by public link, email, or text message. To have a conversation with a client in Messages, invite them to Perchwell first. They create a password when they accept the invitation.

[Share Multiple Listings in Perchwell](http://support.perchwell.com/baldwin/en/articles/13903326-share-multiple-listings-in-perchwell) covers sharing listings with clients you have not invited.

## Is there a Perchwell mobile app for clients?

Yes. Invited clients view listings, receive messages, and share feedback in the Perchwell mobile app for iOS and Android. Clients accept the invitation in a web browser first, then log in to the app.

Share [Accept Your Agent's Perchwell Invitation](http://support.perchwell.com/baldwin/en/articles/15521326-accept-your-agent-s-perchwell-invitation) with a client who needs help getting started.

## Do client messages arrive in real time?

Yes. When an invited client sends you a message, it arrives in your conversation in real time. Perchwell also emails you and adds a green dot to Messages in the top navigation. With push notifications turned on, the Perchwell mobile app notifies you too.

[Message Clients in Perchwell](http://support.perchwell.com/baldwin/en/articles/12541968-message-clients-in-perchwell) explains how to find and answer new messages.

## Where do emails sent from Perchwell come from?

Emails you send from Perchwell come from [no-reply@perchwell.com](mailto:no-reply@perchwell.com), with your name as the sender. Replies go to you. When a client answers an email about a new message, their reply also appears in your conversation in Messages.

## Can I export my conversations with clients?

Conversations with clients cannot be exported from Perchwell. Review every conversation with an invited client on the Messages page.

## Can I personalize the text message when I share a listing?

Yes. Edit the message in the Text tab of the Share window before you click Send Text. Perchwell adds the listing link and "Reply STOP to opt out." to every text, and neither can be removed.

Enter up to 600 characters. A longer message may arrive as multiple texts.

[Share Multiple Listings in Perchwell](http://support.perchwell.com/baldwin/en/articles/13903326-share-multiple-listings-in-perchwell) covers texting listings step by step.

## Can the listing agent see or contact my clients?

No. The listing agent does not see who you shared their listing with and cannot contact your clients through Perchwell. When a client opens listings you shared, they see only your contact information, not the listing agent's.

## Do I need permission to share another agent's listing on social media?

Yes. Get written permission from the listing agent before you promote their listing on social media, such as Facebook or X. Perchwell does not block you from sharing another agent's listing, so getting approval is your responsibility.

## Are clients notified when I add listings to a shared Tag?

No. Clients are not notified when you add listings to a Tag you already shared. To let a client know about new listings in the Tag, find the Tag on the Tags page and click Re-Share to copy the link or send it again by email.

[How to Share a Tag](http://support.perchwell.com/baldwin/en/articles/16221617-how-to-share-a-tag) covers sharing and re-sharing a Tag.

## Will I be notified if my client changes a shared saved search?

No. Perchwell does not notify you when a client changes the filters in a saved search you shared with them. Changes the client saves also update the saved search in your account, which means you see the client's current filters when you open it.

Learn how to control what clients can edit in [Add an Invited Client to a Saved Search](http://support.perchwell.com/baldwin/en/articles/8955682-add-an-invited-client-to-a-saved-search).

## Do clients still receive saved search alerts from a legacy platform?

No. Clients stopped receiving saved search alert emails from a legacy platform once Perchwell replaced it. Turn on alerts for each Saved Search in Perchwell to keep emailing clients about matching listings.

A client receives alerts once they are added to the saved search and Recipient includes contacts. The first time you turn on alerts, Recipient is set to Only me.

[Set Up Email Alerts for Saved Searches](http://support.perchwell.com/baldwin/en/articles/11112420-set-up-email-alerts-for-saved-searches) explains how to choose recipients and how often alerts send.

## Can I add contacts from my phone?

Yes. Add contacts from the Contacts page in the Perchwell mobile app. Turn on Invite to Perchwell while you create a contact to send them an invitation at the same time.

[Contacts Page Overview- Mobile](http://support.perchwell.com/baldwin/en/articles/14785957-contacts-page-overview-mobile) explains how to add a contact in the app.

## Does Perchwell connect to my CRM?

Perchwell does not connect to a CRM (customer relationship management system) directly. [confirm: Perchwell has no direct CRM integration; the live article says only "Perchwell is not in the middle"] Your contacts belong to you, and listings belong to the MLS, so each reaches your CRM a different way:

- Export your contacts to a CSV (comma-separated values) file from the Contacts page, then import the file into your CRM. [Export Contacts to a CSV File](https://app.notion.com/p/3f48b9e01438815d890ce71b71457ba5) explains how.
- Brokers can send listing data to a CRM through a broker-only Bridge feed at no cost. Contact your MLS staff to set it up.
