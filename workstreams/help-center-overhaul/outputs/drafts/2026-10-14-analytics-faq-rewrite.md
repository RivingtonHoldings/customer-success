# Analytics FAQ

**Description:** Answers about Analytics report access for agents, the 8-chart limit in the Analytics view on Search, and saving and sharing charts.

Use this article to check which Analytics reports agents can open, add and save charts in the Analytics view on the Search page, and share them.

## Which Analytics reports can agents open?

Agents can open all six reports on the Analytics page, including the four labeled Broker. The Broker reports are Agent Ranking, Office Ranking, Firm Ranking, and Team Ranking, and the Market reports are Market Activity and Market Analytics. Members who came from a legacy platform may know Analytics as Statistical Reporting. [Analytics Page Overview](https://app.notion.com/p/3f98b9e0143881118584db40d55bf944) explains what each report shows and how to filter and export it.

## What's the difference between the Analytics page and the Analytics view on Search?

The Analytics page holds ranking and market reports for your MLS. The Analytics view on the Search page builds charts from your current search results. The charts reflect any filter you add or remove. To open the Analytics view, click Analytics next to View, above your search results.

## How many charts can I add to the Analytics view?

Add up to 8 charts at a time to the Analytics view on the Search page. To add a different chart when 8 are showing, delete one first. Click the x icon in a chart's upper right, labeled Delete and the chart name, such as Delete Price. [Analytics Tab Walkthrough](https://support.perchwell.com/baldwin/en/articles/8955889-analytics-tab-walkthrough) explains how to add a chart with + Charts and pick a chart category.

## Are Analytics charts saved when I save a search?

Yes. A saved search keeps the charts in its Analytics view and their order. To save chart changes to a saved search that is already open, click Save, then click Update Current. [Manage Your Saved Searches](https://support.perchwell.com/baldwin/en/articles/8955646-manage-your-saved-searches) explains how to save, update, and open saved searches.

## How do I share, export, or print Analytics charts?

Click Share in the Analytics view to email a report or export a PDF (Portable Document Format) file, in Portrait or Landscape. Click the Print icon to download a PDF of the charts showing, then print it from your device. [Share and Export Analytics Charts](https://support.perchwell.com/baldwin/en/articles/8955876-share-and-export-analytics-charts) walks through each sharing option.
