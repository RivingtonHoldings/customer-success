# Log In as an Agent with Assume Identity

**Description:** Log in as an agent from Manage People or the profile menu, let someone in your office log in as you, and return to your account.

Use this article to let someone in your office log in as you, log in as an agent with Assume Identity, and return to your own account. Assume Identity signs you in to an agent's Perchwell account, which means you see exactly what the agent sees. From there, work on the agent's behalf: troubleshoot their account, add listings, run searches, create reports, or update settings.

## Let someone in your office log in as you

Give an admin or another agent in your office access to your account from the Can login as me dropdown in your profile settings. The Can login as me list includes only people in your office.

### Steps:

1. Click your profile icon in the upper right

![The profile menu open in the upper right of Perchwell, with the profile icon highlighted.](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/trq7czv5/2576177813/b8cbf91b80f2d2e8b4af209b093a/Screenshot+2026-07-30+at+8_33_12%E2%80%AFAM.png)

2. Click Settings

![The profile menu with Settings highlighted above Report library and My brokerage.](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/trq7czv5/2576181023/a284dd8df9f2b610df1727f259b6/Screenshot+2026-07-30+at+8_33_59%E2%80%AFAM.png)

3. Click Edit profile in the upper right

![The profile settings page with the Edit profile button highlighted in the upper right.](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/trq7czv5/2576183678/6280ba05125d792fcd5539b1c5c1/Screenshot+2026-07-30+at+8_35_20%E2%80%AFAM.png)

4. Scroll down to the Features section
5. Click the Can login as me dropdown

![The Features section of profile settings with the Can login as me dropdown highlighted below Display My Location.](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/trq7czv5/2576186409/056955a38494e3bfc4a24a6730e5/Screenshot+2026-07-30+at+8_36_21%E2%80%AFAM.png)

6. Enter the person's name in the search field
7. Select the person

![The Can login as me dropdown open, with a name entered in the search field and the matching member listed below it.](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/trq7czv5/2576189680/2e199dec3faf7a51eac2013c2e79/Screenshot+2026-07-30+at+8_37_38%E2%80%AFAM.png)

Perchwell saves your selection automatically. The person can now assume your identity from Log in as in their profile menu. You, your broker, and the person you added each receive an email. Perchwell tracks all history while someone is logged in as you.

To give more than one person access, select each person in the Can login as me list. To remove someone's access, remove them from the Can login as me list. [confirm: how to remove a person from the Can login as me list, such as clicking their name again or clicking an x]

## Who can log in as an agent with Assume Identity

Your role decides where you start when you log in as an agent with Assume Identity.

### Log in as an agent from Manage People

Brokers, office admins, and MLS staff log in as an agent from the agent's details on the Manage People page. Brokers who oversee multiple branches see the agents in every office they oversee.

### Steps:

1. Click Manage People in the top navigation
2. Enter the agent's name in the Search all field in the upper right

![The Manage People page with the Search all field highlighted in the upper right, next to Filters and Add new.](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/trq7czv5/2561402867/497952f1a0feee2e02e2b129d2b2/image.png)

3. Click the agent's name to open their Agent details in a new tab
4. Click Assume identity

> Screenshot placeholder: Show the Agent details page in Manage People for a test agent, with the Assume identity button highlighted | Alt text: The Agent details page with the Assume identity button highlighted under the Assume identity heading.

The agent's Dashboard opens in the same tab, and you see their account exactly as they do.

### Log in as an agent from the profile menu

Admins for an agent or broker log in as an agent from Log in as in the profile menu. Before you log in as an agent this way for the first time, contact the MLS at [mls@baldwinrealtors.com](mailto:mls@baldwinrealtors.com), together with your broker, to request permission. Log in as lists only the agents who added you under Can login as me.

### Steps:

1. Click your profile icon in the upper right
2. Click Log in as

![The profile menu open with Log in as highlighted above Log out.](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/trq7czv5/2561406115/7e17e14ecdb69cd72c78ea13025d/image.png)

3. Enter the agent's name in the Search users field
4. Select the agent

> Screenshot placeholder: Show the profile menu with a test agent's name entered in the Search users field and the matching agent listed | Alt text: The profile menu with an agent's name entered in the Search users field and the matching agent listed below it.

The agent's Dashboard opens in the same tab, and you see their account exactly as they do.

## Stop assuming identity and return to your account

Click your profile icon in the upper right, then click Stop assuming identity to return to your own account. Stop assuming identity shows in the profile menu only while you are logged in as an agent.

![The profile menu while logged in as an agent, with Stop assuming identity highlighted below My brokerage.](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/trq7czv5/2576156721/d8f92bbfaa68ac5438c7367d667b/Screenshot+2026-07-30+at+8_25_43%E2%80%AFAM.png)

## Things to Know

- Changes you make while logged in as an agent with Assume Identity apply directly to the agent's account, even if the agent is logged in at the same time. Your own permission level still applies, which means you make only the changes your role allows.
- Assume Identity, Log in as, and Can login as me work in Perchwell on the web. Log in as an agent from a web browser on a computer, since the mobile app does not include them.

The [Manage People FAQ](https://support.perchwell.com/baldwin/en/articles/15201140-manage-people-faq) answers common questions about managing agent accounts and permissions.
