# Listing Detail Page FAQ

**Description:** Check HOA fees, where a One Page Report QR code leads, comparable listings, and who sees open house remarks on a Listing Detail Page.

Use this article to read a listing's HOA (homeowners association) fee as a monthly or entered amount, know what opens when someone scans your One Page Report, compare a listing with similar listings, and confirm that clients cannot read open house remarks.

## Are HOA fees on the Listing Detail Page monthly or annual?

The Listing Detail Page shows HOA fees both ways. At the top of the page, the fee is a monthly amount, such as $30/mo. In the Overview section, HOA fees holds the amount the listing agent entered, and HOA fees frequency says how often it is charged.

The two numbers look different when the HOA fee is not charged every month. For example, a fee of $355 charged once a year shows as $355 in the Overview section. The top of the page spreads that same fee across 12 months and shows it as $30/mo.

## Where does the QR code on a One Page Report take someone?

The QR code on a One Page Report, or one-pager, opens the client-facing Listing Detail Page. The agent version and the client version open the same page, which means anyone who scans the QR code sees no agent-only details. [Create a One Page Report](http://support.perchwell.com/baldwin/en/articles/12816780-create-one-page-reports) explains how to create either version.

## Where are similar listings on the Listing Detail Page?

The Listing Detail Page does not include a similar listings section. To find comparable listings, click Actions in the upper right of the Listing Detail Page, then select CMA Subject Property. [Create a CMA with a Subject Property](http://support.perchwell.com/baldwin/en/articles/15216797-create-a-cma-with-a-subject-property) explains how to adjust the comps search and create a CMA (Comparative Market Analysis).

## Can clients see open house remarks on the Listing Detail Page?

No. Open house remarks are visible to agents only, which means clients and contacts do not see them on the Listing Detail Page, in a shared listing, or on a public link.

Other agents read open house remarks in the Showings card, under Listing Agent on the Listing Detail Page. Click See All in the Showings card to open the Upcoming Showings window, which lists every scheduled open house for the listing.

[Manage Listings Page Overview](http://support.perchwell.com/baldwin/en/articles/13623016-manage-listings-page-overview) explains how to add an open house to your own listing and enter remarks for other agents.
