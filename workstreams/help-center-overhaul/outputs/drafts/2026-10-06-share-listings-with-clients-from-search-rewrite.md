# Share Listings with Clients from Search

**Description:** Share listings from Search by public link, email, or text message. Clients view them without a Perchwell account or login.

Use this article to share listings from the Search page by public link, email, or text message, and to see what your client sees when they open them.

> Video placeholder: Sharing listings from the Search page, from selecting listings through copying the public link, emailing the listings, and texting them

## Select listings and choose how to share them

Select the listings you want to share on the Search page, then open the Share window from the Actions menu. The Actions menu becomes available once at least one listing is selected.

### Steps:
1. Click Search in the top navigation
2. Select the checkbox next to each listing you want to share
3. Click Actions
4. Select Share

> Screenshot placeholder: the Search page with one listing selected and the Actions menu open, Share highlighted | Alt text: The Search page with one listing checkbox selected and the Actions menu open, showing Share.

The Share window opens, and its title shows how many listings are selected, such as Share 1 Listing. The Share window has two sections:

- via Tag: Add the listings to a Tag, which collects your client's reactions to each listing. Select a Tag, then click Next to add the listings. [How to Share a Tag](http://support.perchwell.com/baldwin/en/articles/16221617-how-to-share-a-tag) covers sending the Tag to your client.
- via Public Link: Copy a link to the selected listings, or send the link by email or text message from Perchwell.

> Screenshot placeholder: the Share 1 Listing window showing the via Tag and via Public Link sections | Alt text: The Share window with the via Tag section and the via Public Link section, which shows the link and sharing buttons.

To send listings to a client you have invited to Perchwell, select Share via Message in the Actions menu instead. [Message Clients in Perchwell](http://support.perchwell.com/en/articles/12541968-message-clients-in-perchwell) walks through sharing listings in Messages.

## Copy or post a public link to share listings

Copy the public link from the via Public Link section of the Share window to paste into your own email, a text message, or a messaging app such as WhatsApp.

### Steps:
1. Click the Copy sharable link icon, the two overlapping squares next to the link
2. Paste the link into an email, text message, or messaging app

The link copies to your clipboard. Click Close to exit the Share window.

Click the Facebook, LinkedIn, or X icon next to Email or Text to start a new post on that site with the public link. `[confirm: LinkedIn opens a new post with the link, as Facebook and X do]`

When you share multiple listings, the post previews the first listing. Clicking the preview opens the shared listings page, where every listing you shared is available.

## Email listings to a client from Perchwell

Click Email or Text in the via Public Link section to email listings from Perchwell. A window opens with Email and Text tabs.

### Steps:
1. In the via Public Link section of the Share window, click Email or Text
2. Click the Email tab
3. Select one or more recipients in To
4. *(Optional)* Select recipients in CC (carbon copy) or BCC (blind carbon copy)
5. *(Optional)* Select a template under Select a template
6. Enter a subject in Subject
7. *(Optional)* Enter a message in Message (optional)
8. Click Email

> Screenshot placeholder: the Email tab of the Share window with the To, CC, BCC, template, Subject, and Message fields | Alt text: The Email tab of the Share window with To, CC, BCC, Select a template, Subject, and Message fields.

A Listings Shared Successfully message appears at the lower right of the screen. Your recipients receive an email with a link to the listings.

To email someone who is not saved as a contact yet, click Create contact to add them first. Learn how to save reusable email text in [Create Communication Templates](http://support.perchwell.com/baldwin/en/articles/14549167-create-communication-templates).

## Text listings to a client from Perchwell

Click Email or Text in the via Public Link section, then the Text tab, to text listings from Perchwell. `[confirm: Share via Text in the Actions menu opens this same Text tab]`

### Steps:
1. In the via Public Link section of the Share window, click Email or Text
2. Click the Text tab
3. Select a contact in Select a contact
4. *(Optional)* Edit the message in the message box
5. Click Send Text

> Screenshot placeholder: the Text tab of the Share window with the Select a contact field, the message box, and the character counter | Alt text: The Text tab of the Share window with the Select a contact field, a message box, and the 600-character counter.

Your contact receives a text with a link to the listings. The first time you text a new number, Perchwell sends a confirmation request instead of the listings. The listings send automatically once the contact replies YES.

Contacts without a phone number are greyed out and cannot be selected. Add a phone number to the contact first, then return to Share. To text someone who is not saved as a contact yet, click Create Contact.

### How text messages are sent and delivered

Text messages sent from Perchwell follow these rules:

- Message length: The message is limited to 600 characters. The counter below the message box shows how many you have used. A long message may arrive on your contact's phone as multiple texts in a row.
- Link and opt-out line: The listing link and the line "Reply STOP to opt out." are added to the end of every text automatically. They cannot be edited or removed, even if you clear the message box.
- Sender number: Texts come from 332-240-3300. Contacts cannot reply to you through the text. Perchwell reads replies only for opt-in (YES) and opt-out (STOP).
- Sending hours: Texts send between about 8am and 8pm in your time zone. A text sent outside those hours goes out at about 8am the next morning.

> **Tip:** Let your client know a confirmation text is coming before you text them for the first time.

## What your client sees when they open shared listings

Shared listings open on a page with your brokerage logo and your name, photo, email, and phone number at the top. The page is the same whether your client opens a public link, an email, or a text. Your client does not need a Perchwell account or login.

- Browse: Click the arrows at the upper right to move between the shared listings. The count between them, such as 1 of 2, shows which listing is open.
- Listing details: Open each listing to review its details.
- Not shown: Listing agent information, private documents, and listing history.

The page shows only the listings you selected when you shared them.
