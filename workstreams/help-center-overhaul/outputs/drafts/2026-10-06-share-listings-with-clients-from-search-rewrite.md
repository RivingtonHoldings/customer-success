# Share Listings with Clients from Search

**Description:** Share listings from Search by public link, email, or text message. Clients can view shared listings without a Perchwell account or login.

Use this article to share listings from the Search page by public link, email, or text message and understand what clients see when they open them.

> Video placeholder: Sharing listings from the Search page, from selecting listings through copying the public link, emailing the listings, and texting them

## Select listings and choose how to share them

Select the listings you want to share from the Search page. After you select at least one listing, the Share option in the Actions menu becomes available.

### Steps:
1. Select the checkbox next to each listing you want to share
2. Click Actions
3. Select Share

> Screenshot placeholder: the Search page with one listing selected and the Actions menu open, Share highlighted | Alt text: The Search page with one listing checkbox selected and the Actions menu open, showing Share.

The Share window opens with two options:

- via Tag: Add the listings to a Tag to collect your client's reactions. [How to Share a Tag](http://support.perchwell.com/baldwin/en/articles/16221617-how-to-share-a-tag) covers sharing a Tag with your client.
- via Public Link: Share a branded public link by copying it or sending it by email or text message, or posting it to social media.

> Screenshot placeholder: the Share 1 Listing window showing the via Tag and via Public Link sections | Alt text: The Share window with the via Tag section and the via Public Link section, which shows the link and sharing buttons.

To share listings with an invited client in Messages, Perchwell's message center, select Share via Message in the Actions menu. [Message Clients in Perchwell](http://support.perchwell.com/en/articles/12541968-message-clients-in-perchwell) covers sharing listings in Messages.

## Copy or post a public link

Click the Copy sharable link icon in the Share window to copy the public link. The icon is the two overlapping squares next to the link. Paste the link into an email, text message, or messaging app.

To post the link on social media, click the Facebook, LinkedIn, or X icon. When you share multiple listings, the post previews the first listing, but the public link opens every listing you shared.

## Email listings from Perchwell

Click Email or Text in the Share window's via Public Link section to email the listings. The Email tab opens by default.

### Steps:
1. Select one or more contacts in the To field
2. *(Optional)* Select recipients in the CC (carbon copy) or BCC (blind carbon copy) field
3. *(Optional)* Select a template under Select a template
4. Enter a subject in the Subject field
5. *(Optional)* Enter a message
6. Click Email

> Screenshot placeholder: the Email tab of the Share window with the To, CC, BCC, template, Subject, and Message fields | Alt text: The Email tab of the Share window with To, CC, BCC, Select a template, Subject, and Message fields.

A Listings Shared Successfully message appears, and each recipient receives an email with a link to the listings.

To email someone who is not in your contacts, enter their email address in the To field and click Add, or click Create contact. Both options add the person to your contacts.

[Create Communication Templates](http://support.perchwell.com/baldwin/en/articles/14549167-create-communication-templates) covers saving reusable email text.

## Text listings from Perchwell

Click Email or Text in the Share window's via Public Link section. Click the Text tab to text the listings. To open the Text tab directly from Search, select Share via Text in the Actions menu.

### Steps:
1. Select a contact in the Select a contact field
2. *(Optional)* Edit the message
3. Click Send Text

> Screenshot placeholder: the Text tab of the Share window with the Select a contact field, the message box, and the character counter | Alt text: The Text tab of the Share window with the Select a contact field, a message box, and the 600-character counter.

Your contact receives a text with a link to the listings. The first time you text a new number, Perchwell sends a confirmation request instead of the listings. The listings send automatically once the contact replies YES.

Contacts without a phone number are greyed out and cannot be selected. Add a phone number to the contact first, then return to the Text tab. Click Create Contact to add someone who is not already in your contacts.

### How texting works in Perchwell

Texts sent from Perchwell follow these rules:

- Message length: Enter up to 600 characters. The counter below the message box shows how many characters you have entered. Longer messages may arrive as multiple texts.
- Listing link and opt-out: Perchwell automatically adds the listing link and "Reply STOP to opt out." to every text. Neither can be edited or removed.
- Sender number: Texts come from 332-240-3300, not your phone number. Contacts cannot reply to you at this number. Perchwell recognizes replies only for opt-in (YES) and opt-out (STOP).
- Sending hours: Texts send between about 8:00 AM and 8:00 PM in your time zone. Texts sent outside these hours go out at about 8:00 AM the next morning.

> **Tip:** Let your client know to expect a confirmation text before you text them for the first time.

## What clients see when they open shared listings

Clients do not need a Perchwell account or login to view shared listings. Whether they open a public link, email, or text message, they see the same page with your brokerage logo and your name, photo, email, and phone number.

The page shows only the listings you selected. Clients can move between listings and view their details. Listing agent information, private documents, and listing history are not shown.
