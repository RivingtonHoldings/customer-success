# Share a Saved Search with a Client

**Description:** Share a saved search with clients, control what they can see and edit, set up their alerts, and remove clients from the search.

Use this article to share a saved search with clients, manage their access and alerts, and remove them from the search.

https://app.arcade.software/share/2JEF0FWKz8HQbae6kfSR

## Share a saved search with a client

Share a saved search with one or more clients from the Edit Search window. Clients open shared searches in their client portal, their own workspace in Perchwell.

The Contacts dropdown lists only people already in your contacts. Clients must accept their invitation to Perchwell before they can open a shared saved search. [Invite Clients to Perchwell](http://support.perchwell.com/baldwin/en/articles/11373895-invite-clients-to-perchwell) explains how to invite them.

### Steps:

1. Click Saved Searches, or the name of the open saved search, in the upper left of the Search page
2. Click the pencil icon next to the saved search in the My Searches window
3. Click the Contacts dropdown in the Edit Search window
4. *(Optional)* Enter the client's name in the Search contacts field
5. Select the checkbox next to each client you want to share the search with
6. Turn on the Allow added contacts to see and edit this search toggle
7. Click Update

The client receives an email that you added them to the saved search. The email has an Open saved search button. [View Shared Saved Searches](http://support.perchwell.com/baldwin/en/articles/14743530-view-shared-saved-searches) explains how clients open shared searches.

## Control what clients can see and edit in a shared saved search

The Allow added contacts to see and edit this search toggle controls what clients can see and edit. Find it below Alerts in the Edit Search window.

- On: Clients can open the saved search in their client portal, view the results, and edit filters and charts.
- Off: The saved search does not appear in their client portal.

Changes a client saves to the shared saved search also update the saved search in your account.

In the saved search, clients do not see Tags you have added to listings, whether the Tags are for them or another client. Tags do not show on the listing detail page, and the Tag Search column shows NA.

## Send saved search alerts to a client

Turn on Alerts in the Edit Search window to email clients about matching listing activity. Select a Recipient option that sends alerts to contacts. Contacts do not need a Perchwell account to receive them.

[Set Up Email Alerts for Saved Searches](http://support.perchwell.com/baldwin/en/articles/11112420-set-up-email-alerts-for-saved-searches) explains how to choose recipients and alert frequency.

## Remove a client from a saved search

Remove a client from a shared saved search through the Contacts field in the Edit Search window.

### Steps:

1. Click Saved Searches, or the name of the open saved search, in the upper left of the Search page
2. Click the pencil icon next to the saved search in the My Searches window
3. Click the X next to the client's name in the Contacts field
4. Click Update

The saved search is removed from the client portal, and the client no longer receives its alerts.
