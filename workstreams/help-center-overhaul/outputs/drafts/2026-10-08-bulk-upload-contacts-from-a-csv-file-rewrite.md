# Bulk Upload Contacts from a CSV File

**Description:** Download the Perchwell CSV template, fill it in, and bulk upload contacts from it. Then fix any import errors and invite the contacts.

Use this article to download and fill in the Perchwell CSV (comma-separated values) template, bulk upload contacts from it, fix import errors, and invite the new contacts to Perchwell.

## When to bulk upload contacts

Bulk upload contacts when you have a list of people to add to Perchwell at once, such as:

- A contact list exported from your CRM (customer relationship management system)
- A client list from a spreadsheet or another outside source

Members who joined from a legacy platform may already have their contacts in Perchwell.

## Download and fill in the CSV template

Download the CSV template from the Upload Contacts window on the Contacts page, then add your contacts to it. Bulk upload works in a web browser, not in the Perchwell mobile app.

### Steps:

1. Click the Add contact pencil icon on the My Contacts tab
2. Click Bulk upload in the lower left of the Create New Contact window
3. Click CSV template in the Upload Contacts window
4. Open the downloaded template in a spreadsheet program
5. Enter your contacts in the template
6. Save the file in CSV format

Your CSV file is ready to upload.

### CSV template requirements for bulk upload

Fill in the CSV template to these requirements so the bulk upload finishes without errors:

- Include a First Name, Last Name, and Email Address for each contact. These fields are required.
- Add a Phone and up to five more emails in Additional Email 1 through Additional Email 5, if you have them.
- Give each contact a different email address.
- Keep the template's column headers exactly as they are. Do not rename or reorder them.

## Upload the CSV file and review the import summary

Upload your completed CSV file in the Upload Contacts window, then check the import summary before you finish.

### Steps:

1. Drag your CSV file into the upload area, or click Browse or drag and drop
2. Click Next
3. Review the contact count and any error messages in the import summary
4. *(Optional)* Turn on invitations [confirm: the exact label of the invitation toggle on the import summary]
5. Click Next

Perchwell adds the contacts to the Contacts page. With invitations on, each contact also receives an invitation email.

## Fix bulk upload errors in the import summary

Fix bulk upload errors listed in the import summary by correcting your CSV file. Each error message names one problem, such as:

- Email must be a valid email: An email address in the file is not in a valid format.
- Email can't be blank: A contact in the file has no email address.
- Email already exists: The email address already belongs to one of your contacts.

Save the corrected file in CSV format and upload it again in the Upload Contacts window.

> **Tip:** If the bulk upload still fails, download the CSV template again and paste your contacts into its columns.

## Invite bulk uploaded contacts to Perchwell

If you left invitations off in the import summary, invite bulk uploaded contacts to Perchwell from each contact's profile. [Invite Clients to Perchwell and Resend Invitations](https://support.perchwell.com/baldwin/en/articles/11373895-invite-clients-to-perchwell) explains how to invite a contact from their profile.
