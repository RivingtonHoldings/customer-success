# Customize Your Search Results View

**Description:** Change how listings display in your search results, add and reorder columns, save your columns as a template, and adjust the Search map.

Use this article to change how listings display in your search results, add, remove, and reorder columns, save your columns as a column template, set a default template for new searches, and adjust the map on the Search page.

https://app.arcade.software/share/2ZGJhka6MY0J6paXfupr

## Change how listings display with the View control

The View control above your search results changes how listings display on the Results tab of the Search page. Click View, then click the view you want:

- List: Displays listings in rows with no photos.
- Expanded: Displays listings in rows, with a photo and a listing summary in the Location column.
- Tiles: Displays listings as cards with larger photos and no columns.
- Preview: Opens one listing at a time with its full details and pages through your search results.
- Analytics: Charts the listings that match your search criteria.

Preview also opens when you click a listing in your search results. Click Exit to return to your search results.

## Add, remove, and reorder columns in your search results

Add, remove, and reorder columns from the Columns control above your search results on the Results tab. The Columns control shows the name of the column template you have applied, which is a saved set of columns in a saved order, such as "Columns: SFR Template".

List and Expanded are the two views with columns. To change your columns from Tiles, Preview, or Analytics, click View and switch to List or Expanded first.

### Steps:
1. Click Columns above your search results
2. Click the Columns tab
3. *(Optional)* Type a column name into the search field to find that column quickly
4. Select or clear the checkbox next to a column to add it or remove it
5. Drag the handle to the left of a column name to move the column
6. Click Update

Your search results redraw with the columns you chose, and the Columns control keeps the name of the template you had applied. Location and Status can be moved but not removed, so both columns stay in your search results whichever template you apply.

![The Columns control open on the Columns tab, with a search field, drag handles and checkboxes beside each column, and Update and Save New buttons.](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/trq7czv5/2599904674/6ec1bd50fea07d314b8244f945be/Screenshot+2026-07-04+at+9_55_21%E2%80%AFAM.png)

To reorder columns without opening the Columns control, click and hold a column header in your search results, then drag it to its new position.

![A column header being dragged to a new position in the search results, with the moving column highlighted.](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/trq7czv5/2599904860/b8f94473261ca5a6ddba5dc6cea2/Screenshot+2026-07-04+at+9_55_41%E2%80%AFAM.png)

## Save your columns as a column template

Save your columns as a column template so a later search starts from the same columns instead of rebuilding them. Set your columns first, as covered under Add, remove, and reorder columns in your search results, then save them.

### Steps:
1. Click Columns above your search results
2. Click Save New
3. Enter a name in the Save Column Template modal
4. *(Optional)* Select Make this my default column view on a new search
5. Click Save

Your column template appears under the Templates tab of the Columns control, and the Columns control shows its name.

> **Important:** Update overwrites the column template you have applied. Save New leaves that template as it was and creates a second one.

To apply a template you saved earlier, click Columns, click the Templates tab, then click the template's name.

## Set a default column template for new searches

Star a column template to make it your default, so a new search opens with the columns you want. The starred template shows a filled star in the Templates tab. There are two ways to set the default from the Columns control on the Results tab.

| To set the default | Do this |
|---|---|
| From a template you already saved | Click Columns, click the Templates tab, then click the star next to the template's name |
| While saving a new template | Click Columns, click Save New, then select Make this my default column view on a new search before you click Save |

When you start a new search, Perchwell applies your starred template search, which means the new search opens with that template's filters, columns, map, and display settings already set. If you have not starred a template search, a new search opens with your starred default column template instead. [Customize Your Search Defaults with Template Searches](http://support.perchwell.com/baldwin/en/articles/13903342-customize-your-search-defaults-with-template-searches) walks through creating a template search and starring it.

## Resize, hide, and change layers on the search map

Resize the search map, hide it, and change its layers from either the Filters tab or the Results tab of the Search page.

- Resize the map: Click and drag the handle on the right edge of the map.
- Hide the map: Click the panel icon at the top left of the Filters or Results panel, immediately beside the map. Click the same icon again to bring the map back.
- Change the layers: Click the layers icon at the lower right of the map.

![The Search page with the panel icon that hides the map highlighted, to the left of the View control.](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/trq7czv5/2599900346/96b0aed91400c0cc87bbe2e3f8e6/Screenshot+2026-07-04+at+10_36_15%E2%80%AFAM.png)

The layers panel holds two kinds of layer:

- Roads, Terrain, and Satellite: The base map. Click one to change the map your listings sit on.
- Flood and Parcels: Overlays, each with its own toggle. Turn either one on alongside the base map you chose. `[confirm: what the Flood and Parcels overlays draw on the map]`

The layers panel also links to flood risk definitions.
