# Search for Condos and Condo Complexes

**Description:** Find condo listings with Property Type and Structure Style filters, or search for listings in a specific condo complex or subdivision.

Use this article to search for condos and find listings in specific condo complexes.

> Video placeholder: Show how to search for condos on the Search page: select Residential attached under Residential-Single Family, add Structure Style set to Condo, then select a complex in the Subdivision/Condo filter

## Search for condos by Property Type and subtype

Residential attached, a subtype under the Residential-Single Family property type, includes condos and other homes that share a common wall, such as duplexes and triplexes.

### Steps:

1. Click Property Type in the upper left of the Search page
2. Select Residential-Single Family
3. Click the Property sub type field
4. Select Residential attached

Your search results update to show attached homes, including condos.

![Alt text: The Property Type Quick Filter open with Residential-Single Family checked and Residential attached in its Property sub type list.](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/trq7czv5/2453634741/f4bf72afd287563abb2fd6fc4b25/Group+111.png)

Members who came from a legacy platform may know Property Type as Class and subtypes as Property Type. [Filter Your Search by Property Types](http://support.perchwell.com/baldwin/en/articles/13922158-filter-your-search-by-property-types) explains how to select subtypes and lists the subtypes under each property type.

## Narrow your search to condos with Structure Style

Add the Structure Style filter to narrow your results to listings identified as condos.

### Steps:

1. Click Filters in the upper right of the Search page
2. Enter Structure Style in the Search filters and values field
3. Click the Structure Style field
4. Select Condo

Your search results update to show listings with a Condo structure style. When Residential attached is also selected under Property Type, your results narrow to attached condos.

![Alt text: The Structure Style filter under Property Details on the Filters tab, with its list open and Condo highlighted.](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/trq7czv5/2453635818/6b607a1ffe87b209647ca22e9e1f/Group+109.png)

## Find listings in a specific condo complex or subdivision

Find listings in a specific condo complex or subdivision with the Subdivision/Condo filter.

### Steps:

1. Click Filters in the upper right of the Search page
2. Enter Subdivision/Condo in the Search filters and values field
3. Click the Subdivision/Condo field
4. Enter part of the complex or subdivision name in the search field at the top of the list
5. Select the name from the list of matches

Your search results update to show listings in the selected condo complex or subdivision. Select more than one name to find listings in any of them.

To find listings without a subdivision or condo complex, select None at the top of the list.
