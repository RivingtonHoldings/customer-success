# Filter Your Search by Keyword

**Description:** Search listing descriptions and addresses for a word or phrase with the Text Search filter, and combine multiple keywords with Or or And.

Use this article to filter listings by keywords, exact phrases, or multiple keywords with the Text Search filter.

## Find listings by keyword on the Filters tab

The Text Search filter finds keywords in listing descriptions and addresses. It does not search private remarks or agent remarks.

### Steps:

1. Click the Filters tab
2. Enter Text Search in the Search filters and values field
3. Enter a keyword in the Text Search field under Listing Visibility & Access

Your search results update to show listings that contain the keyword. The keyword appears under Applied Filters as a Text Search filter.

> Screenshot placeholder: the Filters tab with Text Search entered in the Search filters and values field and the Text Search field under Listing Visibility & Access | Alt text: The Filters tab with Text Search entered in the Search filters and values field and the Text Search field under Listing Visibility & Access.

## Search for exact phrases and multiple keywords with Or or And

Put quotation marks around an exact phrase, such as "high ceilings". Without quotation marks, results may include listings where the words appear separately.

To search for multiple keywords or phrases, separate them with commas in the Text Search field, such as "high ceilings", pool. Select how they apply from the dropdown next to the field:

- Or: Shows listings that contain at least one keyword or phrase.
- And: Shows listings that contain every keyword or phrase.

Or is selected by default. For example, enter "high ceilings", pool and select And to find listings that mention both.

The Text Search filter finds listings that mention your keywords. There is no option to exclude listings that mention specific words.

> Screenshot placeholder: the Text Search field with "high ceilings", pool entered and the dropdown open with Or and And | Alt text: The Text Search field with "high ceilings", pool entered and the Or and And options open.

## Filter by keyword from the Text search column

Add the Text search column to filter by keyword directly from your search results. [Customize Your Search View](http://support.perchwell.com/en/articles/11962073-customize-your-search-view) explains how to add columns. Columns are available in List and Expanded views.

### Steps:

1. Click the three-line icon in the Text search column header to open the filter
2. Enter a keyword in the Text Search field
3. *(Optional)* Select Or or And from the dropdown

Your search results update to show listings that contain the keyword. The same Text Search filter also appears under Applied Filters on the Filters tab.
> Screenshot placeholder: the Results tab in Expanded view with the Text search column filter open, including the Text Search field, Or and And dropdown, and tip about commas and quotation marks | Alt text: The Text search column filter open on the Results tab with the Text Search field and Or and And options.

## Remove the keyword filter from your search

Remove the keyword filter from your search in either of these places:

- Filters tab: Click the x on the Text Search filter under Applied Filters.
- Results tab: Click the x at the upper right of the three-line icon in the Text search column header.

Your search results update.
