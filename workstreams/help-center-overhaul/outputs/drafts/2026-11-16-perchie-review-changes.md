### Can I search for listings with an assumable mortgage?

Yes. Find listings with an assumable mortgage with the Financing filter on the Filters tab. Enter Financing in the Search filters and values field, then select Assumable.

The Financing filter also lists other ways a seller will accept payment, such as Cash, Conventional, FHA, VA, USDA, and Exchange.

### Find a Perchwell how-to guide, manual, or PDF

Perchwell has no printed manual. The Baldwin help center is the how-to guide, and every article in it prints or saves as a PDF.

To print an article or save it as a PDF:

1. Open the article in the help center
2. Press Ctrl+P on Windows or Command+P on Mac
3. Select your printer, or select Save as PDF to keep a copy

### Fix a gray Save draft button

Save draft stays gray until every field from the top of the Add/Edit form through County is filled in, including the address, City, and County. Fill them in, then click Save draft.

If your city or county is not in the list, ask Baldwin MLS staff to add it at [mls@baldwinrealtors.com](mailto:mls@baldwinrealtors.com) or 251-270-0328. While you wait, select any available city and county to save the draft, then edit the draft once MLS staff add yours.

> **Important:** Change the city and county to the correct ones before you click Submit listing.

### Change a listing's status, such as Active under contract to Pending

Change a listing's status, such as from Active under contract to Pending, in the Add/Edit form.

1. Click the pencil icon at the right of the listing's row in Manage Listings
2. Select the new status in the Property status field [confirm: field name and where it sits in the form]

4. Click Submit updates

The new status goes live as soon as the update is submitted. [What Each Listing Status Means](https://support.perchwell.com/baldwin/en/articles/14709241-what-each-listing-status-means) explains each status.
