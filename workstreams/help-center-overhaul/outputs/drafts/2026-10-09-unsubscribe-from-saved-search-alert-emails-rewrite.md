# Unsubscribe from Saved Search Alert Emails

**Description:** Unsubscribe from the saved search alert emails your agent set up, and get alerts again when your home search starts back up.

Use this article, as an invited client, to unsubscribe from saved search alert emails and get saved search alerts again if you restart your home search.

## Unsubscribe from saved search alert emails

Unsubscribe from saved search alert emails with the link at the bottom of any alert email Perchwell sends you.

### Steps:
1. Open a saved search alert email from Perchwell
2. Scroll to the bottom of the email
3. *(Optional)* If the footer is hidden, expand the email
4. Click the Click here to unsubscribe link in the footer
5. On the Unsubscribe from notifications page, enter your email address in the Email address field
6. Click Unsubscribe

Perchwell stops sending saved search alert emails from your agent for all of your saved searches, not only the search named in that email. [confirm: what the client sees after clicking Unsubscribe, such as a confirmation message]

## Get saved search alerts again after unsubscribing

Get saved search alerts again by asking your agent to re-share the saved search or send you a new Perchwell invitation. Alerts resume when your agent shares the search, or after you accept the invitation.

[Accept Your Agent's Perchwell Invitation](http://support.perchwell.com/baldwin/en/articles/15521326-accept-your-agent-s-perchwell-invitation) explains how to respond to an invitation.

## Things to Know

- Unsubscribing from saved search alerts does not stop other Perchwell emails, such as message notifications from your agent.
- Tag alerts go to agents only. Invited clients do not receive them.
- To have your Perchwell account removed, contact your agent.
