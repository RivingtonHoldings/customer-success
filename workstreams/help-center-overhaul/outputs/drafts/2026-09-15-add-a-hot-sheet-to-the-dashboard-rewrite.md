# Add a Hot Sheet to the Dashboard

**Description:** Add a Hot Sheets widget to your Perchwell Dashboard and set it up to track listing activity within a Saved Search or across the MLS.

Use this article to add a Hot Sheets widget to your Dashboard, set up either a saved search hot sheet from one Saved Search or a quick hot sheet for the whole MLS, and open listings from a Hot Sheet. Each Hot Sheet counts new listings, price changes, and status changes over the timeframes you choose.

## When to use a Hot Sheet

Open a Hot Sheet when you want a standing view of listing activity rather than a search you run again each time. A Hot Sheet updates automatically from current MLS data, which means the widget reflects new activity without anyone rerunning it.

- Watch activity across the whole MLS
- Watch a single city or property type
- Watch for listings similar to one in your office coming on the market, to understand the effect on pricing
- Check recent activity on the Saved Searches you built for active clients
- Give a broker a view of activity across more than one office

## Add a Hot Sheets widget to the Dashboard

Add a Hot Sheets widget to the Dashboard from Manage widgets, in the top right corner of the Dashboard.

### Steps:

1. Click Dashboard in the top navigation bar
2. Click Manage widgets in the top right corner
3. Click Add hot sheet

> Screenshot placeholder: Show the Manage widgets panel open on the Dashboard, with Add hot sheet highlighted | Alt text: The Manage widgets panel open on the Perchwell Dashboard with the Add hot sheet option highlighted.

The new Hot Sheets widget is added to the Dashboard and opens on a choice of two: From saved search, or New hot sheet.

## Set up a saved search hot sheet

A saved search hot sheet tracks listing activity within the criteria of one Saved Search. Start with the search you want to monitor, such as a client's criteria or all listings for a specific brokerage or office, and save it as a Saved Search before you set up the Hot Sheet.

Once added, the widget is labeled Saved search hot sheet.

https://www.loom.com/share/ddedacbfffc24c4794b74754b6d038cd

### Steps:

1. In the new Hot Sheets widget, click From saved search
2. In the Edit hot sheet modal, choose the Saved Search to monitor
3. Set each timeframe from 0 to 998 days
4. Remove any statuses you do not want to track
5. Click Confirm

The Hot Sheet fills in on the Dashboard with listing activity from that Saved Search.

> **Tip:** A more specific Saved Search makes a more useful Hot Sheet.

Learn how to build a search in [Create a Search with Filters in Perchwell](http://support.perchwell.com/baldwin/en/articles/13903380-create-a-search-with-filters-in-perchwell), and how to edit and manage it in [Manage Your Saved Searches](http://support.perchwell.com/en/articles/8955646-manage-your-saved-searches).

## Set up a quick hot sheet

A quick hot sheet tracks listing activity across your whole MLS, narrowed by property type and status. Once built, its widget is labeled Quick hot sheet.

https://app.arcade.software/share/SINhoyObS2qHu9aF1HE0

### Steps:

1. In the new Hot Sheets widget, click New hot sheet
2. In the Edit hot sheet modal, enter a name for the Hot Sheet
3. Choose the Property Types to monitor
4. Set each timeframe from 0 to 998 days
5. Remove any statuses you do not want to track
6. Click Confirm

The Hot Sheet fills in on the Dashboard with listing activity matching your criteria.

> **Note:** A new Hot Sheet opens with every status selected and with timeframes of 1, 7, and 30 days. Adjust either before clicking Confirm.

## Open listings from a Hot Sheet

Click a bar or the status name beside it to open the listings included in that count in a new tab.

Hot Sheets count activity that occurred during the selected timeframe. A listing's status may have changed since then, so some of the listings that open may show a different status today.

## Things to Know

- The Dashboard and Hot Sheets are not available in the Perchwell mobile app. Both open on desktop only
- For common Hot Sheet questions, such as how many fit on a Dashboard and what counts as off-market activity, see [Dashboard FAQ](http://support.perchwell.com/en/articles/15216626-dashboard-faq)
- To change which widgets sit on the Dashboard, see [Customize Your Dashboard](http://support.perchwell.com/en/articles/13623904-customize-your-dashboard)
