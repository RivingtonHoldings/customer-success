# Find Your Partial Listings in Perchwell

**Description:** Partial listings you started before July 20, 2026 are now draft listings. See where to find them and what to do if one is missing.

Use this article to see which partial listings moved to Perchwell, find them as draft listings in Manage Listings, and re-create or update a partial that did not fully move over.

## Which partial listings moved to Perchwell

Every partial listing created before July 20, 2026 moved to Perchwell as a draft listing in a one-time import on that date. Members who came from a legacy platform may know draft listings as partials. A draft listing is saved in the Add/Edit form but not yet submitted to the MLS, which means it does not appear in search results.

A partial started after July 20, 2026, or a change made to one after that date, did not move to Perchwell. An imported draft looks the same as a draft created in Perchwell. Your published listings are all in Perchwell too.

## Find your partial listings in Manage Listings

Find your partial listings with the Publication status filter on the Manage Listings page, where they are saved as draft listings.

### Steps:

1. Click Filters in the upper right
2. Select Drafts under Publication status

The list narrows to draft listings, including the partial listings that moved over. Agents see their own drafts, and brokerage admins also see drafts for the agents in their brokerage. [Add and Edit Listings](http://support.perchwell.com/baldwin/en/articles/13627189-add-and-edit-listings) explains how to complete the required fields and submit the listing, and [Manage Listings Page Overview](http://support.perchwell.com/en/articles/13623016-manage-listings-page-overview) covers the other filters for finding a listing.

## Re-create or update a partial listing missing from Perchwell

Re-create a partial listing created after July 20, 2026 as a new draft in Perchwell. Click Add New Listing in the upper right of the Manage Listings page to open the Add/Edit form.

If a draft moved over but is missing changes you made after July 20, 2026, click the pencil icon at the right of its row on the Manage Listings page. Add those changes in the Add/Edit form.
