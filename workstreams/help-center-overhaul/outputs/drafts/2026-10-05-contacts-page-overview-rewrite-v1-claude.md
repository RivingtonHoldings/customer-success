# Contacts Page Overview

**Description:** Find your way around the Contacts page: read and filter contacts, add a contact, and look up any member of your MLS in My MLS.

Use this article to find your way around the Contacts page in Perchwell: read, find, add, and group contacts, message a contact or open their profile, export the contact list, and look up any member of your MLS on the My MLS tab.

https://app.arcade.software/share/hYdohaFqPRWoEKbZzQ9P

## Open the Contacts page

The Contacts page holds your own clients and leads, and the roster of everyone in your MLS. Click Contacts in the top navigation to open it, then click a tab:

- My Contacts: The clients and leads you saved.
- My MLS: Every member of your MLS.

Invited clients do not see the My MLS tab.

## Read your contact list on the My Contacts tab

The My Contacts tab lists one row per contact. Three columns tell you where each contact stands:

- Group: The one group the contact belongs to, such as Actively Searching or Referral.
- Last Shared: When you last shared with the contact. Last Email means you shared by email, and Last Chat means you shared in Messages.
- Status: Whether the contact has access to Perchwell. Uninvited means you have not invited the contact, Invite sent means the invitation has gone out, and Last Seen shows the date and time the contact last signed in.

## Find a contact on the My Contacts tab

Finding a contact on the My Contacts tab works three ways:

- Search contacts: Type in the search field on the upper right to find a contact.
- Column headings: Click a column heading, such as Last name or Status, to sort the list by that column.
- Filters: Click the Filters icon, the last icon on the upper right, to show only the contacts in one group or with one status.

## Add a contact on the My Contacts tab

Adding a contact starts from the Add contact icon, the pencil on the upper right of the My Contacts tab. It opens the Create New Contact modal, where you fill in the contact's details and choose what happens next:

- Basic Details: First Name, Last Name, and Email are required. Phone Number is optional.
- Additional Emails: Click Add to include up to five more email addresses. Notifications sent to the contact also go to these addresses, in the same email thread.
- Invite to Perchwell: Turn on to invite the contact to Perchwell, which gives them access to Search, Tags, Contacts, and Messages to collaborate with you. Leave it off to invite the contact later.
- Create Tag: Turn on to create a Client Tag for the contact automatically. A Client Tag gathers the listings you send the contact, and the results of any Saved Search you send through the Tag, in one place where the client can like or dislike them. Set up alerts for the contact or send the Tag later from Tags. Click Learn more under Create Tag for a short explainer.
- Bulk upload: Click Bulk upload, on the lower left of the modal, to add many contacts at once from a CSV (comma-separated values) file.

Click Create to save the contact. To assign a group or add more details, such as a job title, company, or address, edit the contact from their profile once it is saved.

### Add a contact while you share listings

Adding a contact while you share listings starts from a new client Tag in the share flow.

### Steps:

1. Select the listings you want to share
2. Click Actions
3. Click Share
4. Click the Select a Tag dropdown
5. Click + Create New Tag
6. Click + New Contact

The Create New Contact modal opens with the same fields and options as the one on the My Contacts tab.

## Organize contacts into groups on the My Contacts tab

Contact groups sort your clients and leads by status, relationship, or stage, such as Actively Searching, Referral, or Open House. Each contact belongs to one group at a time.

Click the Edit groups icon, the people icon on the upper right of the My Contacts tab, to change your list of groups. To put a contact in a group, open the contact's profile, click Edit profile, and set Grouping. [Create and Manage Your Contacts in Perchwell](https://support.perchwell.com/en/articles/14790755-create-and-manage-your-contacts-in-perchwell) covers adding, renaming, and assigning groups.

## Message a contact or open their profile from the My Contacts tab

Each row on the My Contacts tab leads to the contact's messages and profile:

- Chat icon: Click the chat icon at the end of a contact's row to open Messages with that contact.
- Contact name: Click a contact's name to open their profile.

The contact's profile has four tabs on the left:

- Contact Details: Everything saved about the contact. Click Edit profile on the upper right to add details such as a job title, company, more phone numbers, or an address.
- Shared Listings: The listings you shared with the contact.
- Shared Searches: The searches you shared with the contact.
- Shared Reports: The reports you shared with the contact.

The Reverse Prospecting toggle on the Contact Details tab controls whether agents with a matching listing discover this contact's Saved Searches and reach out to you. Turn it off so those agents cannot discover any of this contact's Saved Searches. [Reverse Prospecting](https://support.perchwell.com/baldwin/en/articles/16201667-reverse-prospecting) explains how agents find matching searches.

## Export your contact list from the My Contacts tab

Exporting your contact list downloads your My Contacts list as a CSV file. Click the Export contacts icon, the arrow on the upper right of the My Contacts tab, to start the download. The export covers the My Contacts tab only; the My MLS roster does not export.

[Export Your Contact List](https://support.perchwell.com/en/articles/12896093-export-your-contact-list) covers where the file lands.

## Look up any member of your MLS on the My MLS tab

The My MLS tab is your MLS roster, sometimes called the agent roster: every member of your MLS, with their brokerage, office, team, email, license number, cell phone, and office phone. Look up an agent there to find their contact details or license number.

### Steps:

1. On the Contacts page, click My MLS
2. Type the member's name in Search my MLS

The roster shows the members whose names match. Search my MLS searches by name only.
