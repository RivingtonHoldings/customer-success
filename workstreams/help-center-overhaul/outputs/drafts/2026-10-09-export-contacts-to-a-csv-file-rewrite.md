# Export Contacts to a CSV File

**Description:** Export your contacts from the Contacts page to a CSV file, and see which contacts and fields the file includes for a backup or import.

Use this article to export your contacts from the Contacts page to a CSV (comma-separated values) file and see which contacts and fields the file includes.

## When to export contacts to a CSV file

Export contacts to a CSV file when you need a copy of them, such as to:

- Share your contact list outside Perchwell
- Keep a backup of your contacts
- Import your contacts into another platform, such as your CRM (customer relationship management system)

## Export contacts from the Contacts page

Export contacts to a CSV file from the My Contacts tab of the Contacts page. Contact export works in a web browser, not in the Perchwell mobile app.

### Steps:

1. Click Contacts in the top navigation
2. Click the Export contacts arrow icon on the upper right of the My Contacts tab

The CSV file downloads to your browser's default download location, such as your Downloads folder or desktop.

## What the contacts CSV file includes

The contacts CSV file includes only the contacts you added to Perchwell yourself, not your entire contact list. [confirm: which contacts on the My Contacts tab the export leaves out, such as contacts carried over from a legacy platform] Members of your MLS on the My MLS tab are not included.

Each contact in the CSV file has these fields:

- First Name
- Last Name
- Email
- Cell phone
- Group
- Status

[Bulk Upload Contacts](https://support.perchwell.com/en/articles/10732227-bulk-upload-contacts) explains how to add multiple contacts to Perchwell from a CSV file.
