# Login and Troubleshooting FAQ

**Description:** Log in to Perchwell from your Baldwin portal and fix common problems with logging in, pages loading, browsers, and pop-ups.

Use this article, as a Baldwin Realtors member, to log in to Perchwell, get past login and loading problems, and report a problem to Baldwin MLS staff.

## Log in to Perchwell

### How do I log in to Perchwell on a computer?

Log in to Perchwell on a computer from your Baldwin portal, Relevate.

1. Log in to your Baldwin portal with your username and password
2. Click Perchwell under Quick Links

Perchwell opens, already logged in to your account.

> Screenshot placeholder: Show the Quick Links section of the Baldwin portal with the Perchwell button in the upper left | Alt text: The Quick Links section of the Baldwin portal, with the Perchwell button in the upper left.

### How do I log in to the Perchwell mobile app?

Log in to the Perchwell mobile app with the email address in your Baldwin portal. Then sign in with your Baldwin portal username and password. [How to Log in to the Perchwell Mobile App](http://support.perchwell.com/baldwin/en/articles/15280170-how-to-log-in-to-the-perchwell-mobile-app) walks through each screen.

### Can I log in to Perchwell without the Baldwin portal?

No. Log in to Perchwell through your Baldwin portal every time. Perchwell uses single sign-on (SSO), which means your Baldwin portal username and password log you in to Perchwell. There is no separate Perchwell password.

## Fix problems logging in to Perchwell

### What should I do if I can't log in to Perchwell?

Work through these checks when you can't log in to Perchwell:

- Check your Baldwin portal username and password, and make sure Caps Lock is off
- Enter your username and password by hand instead of with your browser's autofill
- Open your Baldwin portal in a private or incognito browser window
- Try another supported browser, Chrome or Safari

If you still can't log in, or Perchwell doesn't open from your Baldwin portal, contact Baldwin MLS staff.

### What should I do if Perchwell asks me to complete training?

Complete the required Baldwin Realtors training, then log in to Perchwell again. [How to Complete Required Perchwell Training](http://support.perchwell.com/baldwin/en/articles/16170923-how-to-complete-required-perchwell-training) explains the prerecorded course and how to register for a live session.

### What should I do if I see a permission or access error in Perchwell?

Log out of Perchwell, then log back in through your Baldwin portal to refresh your session. Check that you logged in with your own Baldwin portal account. If the error continues, contact Baldwin MLS staff to check your account's permissions.

Personal and office assistants who see only the Dashboard need permission from their agent or broker first. [User Settings FAQs](http://support.perchwell.com/baldwin/en/articles/16180793-user-settings-faqs) explains why.

## Fix loading, display, and browser problems in Perchwell

### Which browser works best with Perchwell?

Google Chrome works best with Perchwell, and Safari is also supported. Keep your browser updated to its latest version.

### Can I zoom in on Perchwell on a computer?

Yes. Keep your browser zoom at or below 120% so every Perchwell feature stays visible on the page.

### How do I hard refresh Perchwell?

Press your browser's hard refresh shortcut to reload Perchwell with fresh files instead of saved copies:

- Chrome on Windows: Press Ctrl + Shift + R
- Chrome on a Mac: Press Cmd + Shift + R
- Safari on a Mac: Press Cmd + Option + R

### How do I clear my cache and cookies for Perchwell?

Clear your browser's cache and cookies to remove saved website data that may be outdated or damaged. Your browser then loads fresh data from Perchwell.

- Chrome on Windows: Press Ctrl + Shift + Delete, select cache and cookies, then clear them
- Chrome on a Mac: Press Cmd + Shift + Delete, select cache and cookies, then clear them
- Safari on a Mac: Open Safari Settings, click Privacy, then click Manage Website Data. Select perchwell.com and click Remove [confirm: the Remove button in the Manage Website Data window]

Reload the page after clearing your cache and cookies. If Perchwell asks you to log in, log in through your Baldwin portal.

### What should I do if Perchwell won't load or a page is blank?

When Perchwell won't load, gets stuck loading, or shows a blank or partly loaded page, try these in order:

1. Hard refresh the page with your browser's hard refresh shortcut
2. Clear your browser's cache and cookies, then reload the page
3. Check your internet connection, or turn your Wi-Fi off and back on
4. Open Perchwell in another supported browser or on another device

If the page still won't load, contact Baldwin MLS staff.

### What should I do if Perchwell is slow?

Try these steps when Perchwell is slow or lags:

- Close browser tabs and apps you aren't working in
- Restart your browser
- Check your internet speed
- Turn off browser extensions
- Restart your computer

### What should I do if buttons, filters, or the map don't work in Perchwell?

Work through these checks when buttons, filters, or the map don't respond, or Perchwell works in only one browser:

- Update your browser to its latest version
- Turn off browser extensions, especially ad blockers
- Check that JavaScript is turned on in your browser settings
- Open Perchwell in a private or incognito browser window
- Clear your browser's cache and cookies, then reload the page

### What should I do if my browser blocks Print or Export CSV in Perchwell?

Allow pop-ups for perchwell.com in your browser settings when your browser blocks Print or Export CSV. Safari also needs downloads allowed for Export CSV.

To allow pop-ups in Chrome:

1. Open Chrome Settings
2. Click Privacy and security
3. Click Site settings
4. Click Pop-ups and redirects
5. Click Add next to Allowed to send pop-ups and use redirects
6. Enter perchwell.com in the Site field
7. Click Add

To allow pop-ups and downloads in Safari, open Perchwell in Safari first, since Safari lists only open websites:

1. Open Safari Settings
2. Click Websites
3. Click Pop-up Windows
4. Select Allow next to perchwell.com
5. Click Downloads
6. Select Allow next to perchwell.com [confirm: the Allow option in Safari's Downloads list]

Refresh the Perchwell page, then click Print or Export CSV again.

## Report a problem in Perchwell

### Who do I contact for help with Perchwell?

Contact Baldwin MLS staff with any Perchwell question or problem, including login, account, and permission issues. Call (251) 270-0328 or email [mls@baldwinrealtors.com](mailto:mls@baldwinrealtors.com).

### What should I include when I report a bug in Perchwell?

Include these details when you report a Perchwell bug to Baldwin MLS staff:

- Your device, PC or Mac
- Your browser, such as Chrome or Safari
- A short summary of the problem
- Screenshots of the problem
- Screenshots of the Console and Network tabs, which show the errors and page requests behind the problem

To capture the Console and Network tabs in Chrome:

1. Right-click the Perchwell page
2. Select Inspect
3. Click the Console tab and take a screenshot
4. Click the Network tab and take a screenshot

To capture the Console and Network tabs in Safari:

1. Open Safari Settings
2. Click the Advanced tab
3. Select Show features for web developers
4. Press Cmd + Option + C to open the Console
5. Take a screenshot of the Console tab
6. Click the Network tab and take a screenshot

Send these screenshots with your report to Baldwin MLS staff.
