# Find and Update Contacts Moved from Matrix

**Description:** Find your contacts moved from Matrix on the Contacts page, add any missing email addresses, and invite them to Perchwell.

Use this article to find the contacts moved from Matrix and make sure each one has an email address before you invite them to Perchwell.

## Find your contacts moved from Matrix on the Contacts page

Open the Contacts page to find your contacts moved from Matrix on the My Contacts tab. Contacts moved with their name, email address, phone number, and any additional email addresses.

Contacts moved in a one-time transfer at the start of your Perchwell member training. Changes made in Matrix afterward are not in Perchwell.

## Add an email address to a contact moved from Matrix

Add an email address to any contact that moved from Matrix without one. On the My Contacts tab, those contacts have an empty Email column. Perchwell requires an email address to create a contact, but some contacts moved from Matrix without one. Without one, a contact is left out of the contact list when you:
- Share a listing by email
- Share a saved search
- Share a report
- Send a message

### Steps:
1. Click the contact's name on the My Contacts tab
2. Click Edit profile
3. Enter the contact's email address
4. Click Save profile

The email address shows in the Email column. The contact appears in the contact list the next time you share from Perchwell or send a message.

## Invite contacts moved from Matrix to Perchwell

Contacts moved from Matrix arrived uninvited, which means they do not have access to Perchwell until you invite them. Invite a contact to give them Messages and their own searches. Contacts you have not invited can still open listings you share by link, email, or text, with no login.

Invite one contact from their profile, or select up to 100 contacts on the Contacts page, click Actions, and select Invite to Perchwell. To invite contacts as you add them, bulk upload a CSV (comma-separated values) file of up to 100 contacts at a time.

Check each contact's email address before you send an invitation. After you invite a contact, their first name, last name, email address, and phone number are locked. To change a locked field, contact Perchwell Support.
