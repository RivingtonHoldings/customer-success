# Communicate with Your Agent in Perchwell

**Description:** Talk with your agent in Perchwell Messages and let them know which shared listings are a strong match, worth discussing, or not a fit.

Use this article, as an invited client, to chat with your agent in Messages, react to shared listings with Reject, Maybe, or Like, and reply to your agent by email.

https://www.loom.com/embed/1db1267f87c04d45a106e2c2e2606c1b

## Chat with your agent in Messages

Chat with your agent from Messages in your client portal, your workspace for collaborating with your agent.

### Steps:

1. Click Messages in the top navigation
2. Hover over the left edge of the page to open the Messages panel
3. Select your agent's name to open your conversation
4. Enter your message in the Send a message field
5. Click the paper-plane icon to send

Your agent sees your message in your conversation and receives an email notification.

> Screenshot placeholder: a client's Messages page with the Messages panel open and the agent's conversation selected, captured from a test client account | Alt text: The Messages page with the Messages panel open on the left and a conversation with an agent.

To send your agent a listing in Messages, follow the steps in [Share Listings with Your Agent](http://support.perchwell.com/baldwin/en/articles/11373849-share-listings-with-your-agent).

## React to shared listings with Reject, Maybe, or Like

React to the listings your agent shares with you in Shared properties, inside your conversation in Messages. Each reaction tells your agent what you think of a listing:

- Reject: Not a fit.
- Maybe: Worth discussing.
- Like: A strong match.

### Steps:

1. Click Messages in the top navigation
2. Hover over the left edge of the page to open the Messages panel
3. Select your agent's name to open your conversation
4. Click the Reject X, Maybe question mark, or Like heart icon on a listing in Shared properties

Your agent sees the reaction on the listing in Shared properties right away. To change a reaction, click a different icon on the listing.

> Screenshot placeholder: Shared properties in a client's conversation with the Reject, Maybe, and Like icons on a listing, captured from a test client account | Alt text: Shared properties in a Messages conversation with the Reject X, Maybe question mark, and Like heart icons on a listing.

> **Note:** Perchwell does not notify your agent when you react to a listing. Send your agent a message when you want them to follow up.

## Reply to your agent's messages by email

Reply to your agent from your email inbox. Each time your agent writes to you in Messages, Perchwell emails you from [no-reply@perchwell.com](mailto:no-reply@perchwell.com) with your agent's name as the sender.

### Steps:

1. Open the email from Perchwell
2. Reply to the email with your message

Your reply appears in your conversation in Messages and in your agent's email inbox.

## Things to Know

- Messages and reactions work the same way in the Perchwell mobile app.
- Unsubscribing from saved search alert emails does not stop message emails from your agent.
