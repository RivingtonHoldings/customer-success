# Actions Menu Options on the Search Page

**Description:** Select listings on the Search page and open the Actions menu to share, export, print, schedule showings, get directions, and more.

Use this article to select listings in your search results and choose an option from the Actions menu.

> Video placeholder: Select three listings on the Results tab, open the Actions menu, and show each group of options.

## Select listings and open the Actions menu on the Search page

Select listings on the Results tab of the Search page to access all options in the Actions menu.

### Steps:

1. Select the checkboxes next to the listings you want, or the column header checkbox to choose every loaded listing
2. Click Actions in the upper right of the search results

The count next to Actions shows how many listings are selected, such as 3 of 29. The column header checkbox selects only the listings on the page so far, and results load 30 at a time as you scroll.

> Screenshot placeholder: the Results tab in Expanded view with three listings selected and the Actions menu open | Alt text: The Search page with three listings selected and the Actions menu open, with 3 of 29 shown next to Actions.

With no listings selected, only Export CSV is available. Choose at least one listing to turn on the other options.

> Screenshot placeholder: the Actions menu open with no listings selected | Alt text: The Actions menu with no listings selected, with only Export CSV available and the other options greyed out.

## Share listings and email listing agents from the Actions menu

Share selected listings with clients or email their listing agents from the Actions menu:

- Share: Opens the Share window to copy a public link, email or text the listings, or add them to a Tag.
- Share via Message: Opens a separate window to send the listings to a contact with a message. Invited clients receive them in Messages and by email, and contacts you have not invited by email only.
- Share via Text: Opens the Text tab of the Share window to text the listings to a contact.
- Contact Agent: Opens the Contact Agent window to email the listing agents for the selected listings.

[Share Listings with Clients from Search](https://app.notion.com/p/3f28b9e01438816ba3e0d164dae2c01a) explains how to share listings by public link, email, or text. [Message Clients in Perchwell](http://support.perchwell.com/en/articles/12541968-message-clients-in-perchwell) explains how messages are delivered to invited and uninvited clients.

### Email listing agents with Contact Agent

Email the listing agents for multiple listings at once from the Contact Agent window. Each listing agent receives a separate email for each of their listings. The window shows every selected listing with its agents' names and email addresses.

### Steps:

1. Click Actions
2. Select Contact Agent
3. *(Optional)* Select a template under Select a template
4. Enter a subject in the Subject field
5. *(Optional)* Edit the message in the Message field
6. *(Optional)* Enter an email address in the Additional recipient email field
7. *(Optional)* Click Add Email to cc (carbon copy) that address, up to 5 recipients
8. Click Send Email

The message "Your email has been sent successfully." appears after the email is sent.

> **Tip:** Enter {listing} in the subject or message to include each listing's address in its email.

## Create reports, export, or print listings from the Actions menu

Create a report, export a CSV (comma-separated values) file, or print search results from the Actions menu:

- Create a Report: Opens a list of reports for the selected listings, such as a CMA (Comparative Market Analysis), a Listing Report, or a One Pager.
- Export CSV: Downloads the selected listings as a CSV file. With no listings selected, the file includes every listing in the search results, even those you have not scrolled to yet.
- Print: Opens your browser's print window with every listing in the search results, regardless of which listings are selected. Print is available in List and Expanded views.

For more information:

- [Create One Page Reports](http://support.perchwell.com/en/articles/12816780-create-one-page-reports) covers choosing the client or agent version and the built-in QR code.
- [Export Listings to a CSV File](https://app.notion.com/p/3f28b9e0143881e7ae22f09afa774206) covers choosing export fields and creating export templates.
- [Print or Save Search Results as a PDF](https://app.notion.com/p/3f48b9e0143881c18b61d09ddd6add77) covers page layout, printed columns, and saving a PDF.

## Schedule showings and get driving directions for selected listings

Schedule showings or get driving directions to selected listings from the Actions menu:

- Schedule a Showing: Opens the ShowingTime integration to schedule showings.
- Driving Directions: Opens Google Maps in a new tab with a route from your current location to each selected listing. Select up to 9 listings.

If Driving Directions stays greyed out after you select listings, turn on location sharing in your browser's privacy and security settings.

[Schedule Showings for Listings](http://support.perchwell.com/baldwin/en/articles/16392791-schedule-showings-for-listings) shows how to book showing times with listing agents in a short video.

## Keep, hide, or open listings in a new tab from the Actions menu

Keep or hide selected listings, or open them together in a new tab from the Actions menu:

- Keep: Shows only the selected listings and hides the rest.
- Hide: Hides the selected listings.
- Open in New Tab: Opens one new tab with the selected listings in a carousel.

> **Important:** Keep and Hide apply only while you remain in the search. Save the search so those listings stay out of your results. If you reload or leave without saving, the hidden listings return.

[Search Page Overview](https://app.notion.com/p/3e58b9e0143881dd8234e6ec3dfc3397) explains how to view and restore hidden listings.
