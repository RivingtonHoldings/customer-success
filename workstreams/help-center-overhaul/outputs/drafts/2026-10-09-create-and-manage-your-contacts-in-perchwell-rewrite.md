# Create and Manage Your Contacts in Perchwell

**Description:** Create a contact on the Contacts page, add or edit contact groups, assign a group, and edit or delete a contact from their profile.

Use this article to create a contact on the Contacts page, add, rename, reorder, or delete contact groups, assign a contact to a group, and edit or delete a contact from their profile.

> Video placeholder: a new Loom that creates a contact on the Contacts page, edits contact groups in the Edit groups window, assigns a group, and edits and deletes a contact from their profile

## Create a contact on the Contacts page

Create a contact in the Create New Contact window on the Contacts page. A first name, last name, and email address are required.

### Steps:

1. Click the Add contact pencil icon on the My Contacts tab
2. Enter the contact's first name, last name, and email address
3. *(Optional)* Enter a phone number
4. *(Optional)* Click Add under Additional Emails to include up to five more email addresses
5. *(Optional)* Turn on Invite to Perchwell
6. *(Optional)* Turn on Create Tag
7. Click Create

The contact is saved to your My Contacts list, and the Contacts page opens. Perchwell notifications sent to the contact also go to any additional email addresses. With Invite to Perchwell on, the contact receives an invitation email, and with Create Tag on, Perchwell creates a Client Tag for the contact.

To add a company, job title, or group, edit the contact's profile after you save them. See Assign a contact to a group and Edit a contact's details.

> Screenshot placeholder: the Create New Contact window, empty, with both toggles off | Alt text: The Create New Contact window with first name, last name, and email fields, Additional Emails, and the Invite to Perchwell and Create Tag toggles.

To create a contact while you share a listing, click Listing Actions on the listing, choose to share by text, then click Add contact. The Add Contact window that opens there also has Company, Title, and Group fields, and saves with Add contact.

- [Bulk Upload Contacts from a CSV File](https://support.perchwell.com/baldwin/en/articles/10732227-bulk-upload-contacts) explains how to add multiple contacts at once from a CSV (comma-separated values) file.

## Add, rename, reorder, or delete contact groups

Add, rename, reorder, or delete contact groups in the Edit groups window on the Contacts page. Contact groups organize your clients and leads by status, relationship, or stage. The default groups are Actively Searching, Passively Searching, Inactive, and New Contacts.

Click the Edit groups people icon on the My Contacts tab to open the Edit groups window, then:

- Click Add Group to create a group, such as Investor clients or Open house leads. Enter a name in the Group name field, then click Save.
- Select a group to rename it. Change the Group name field, then click Save.
- Select a group, then click Delete group to delete it.
- Drag a group by the six-dot handle on its left to reorder your groups.
- Enter a group name in the Search groups field to find a group.

Deleting a group does not delete its contacts. Those contacts are no longer in any group until you assign them to a new one.

## Assign a contact to a group

Assign a contact to a group from their profile on the Contacts page. Each contact belongs to one group at a time, which means selecting a new group replaces the current one.

### Steps:

1. Click the contact's name on the My Contacts tab
2. Click Edit profile
3. Select a group under Grouping
4. Click Save profile

The contact's group shows in the Group column on the My Contacts tab.

> **Tip:** To see every contact in a group, click the Filters slider icon on the My Contacts tab and filter by that group.

## Edit a contact's details

Edit a contact's details, such as their phone number, additional email addresses, company, job title, or address, from their profile on the Contacts page.

### Steps:

1. Click the contact's name on the My Contacts tab
2. Click Edit profile
3. Update the contact's details
4. Click Save profile

The contact's profile shows the updated details.

> **Important:** After you invite a contact to Perchwell, their first name, last name, email, and phone number are locked. To change a locked field, contact Perchwell Support.

## Delete a contact

Delete a contact from their profile on the Contacts page.

> **Important:** Deleting a contact is permanent and cannot be undone.

### Steps:

1. Click the contact's name on the My Contacts tab
2. Click Delete contact at the bottom of the Contact Details tab
3. Click Delete in the Delete contact window

The contact is removed from your My Contacts list.
