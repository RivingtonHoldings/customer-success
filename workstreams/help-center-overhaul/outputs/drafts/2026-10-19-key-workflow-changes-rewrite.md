# What Changed When You Moved to Perchwell

**Description:** See where legacy workflows such as Quick Search, CollabLink, and the Market Monitor moved in Perchwell, and why they changed.

Use this article to find the Perchwell way to do tasks you know from a legacy platform in Search, listing entry, client collaboration, the Dashboard, reports, and financial calculators, and to understand why each one changed.

## Search changes in Perchwell

These Search changes in Perchwell all happen on the Search page.

### Template Search replaces the Quick Search widget

Set a saved search as your Template Search, and every new search starts with its filters, sort order, columns, view, and map settings. Members who came from a legacy platform may know this as the customizable Quick Search widget on the Home tab.

The Quick Search widget held one set of highlighted filters. Any saved search can become your Template Search, one at a time. Star a different saved search when you want a different starting point. [Create a Template Search](http://support.perchwell.com/baldwin/en/articles/13903342-customize-your-search-defaults-with-template-searches) explains how to set one up.

### Select property types at any point in a search

Select one or more property types from the Property Type Quick Filter at any point in your search. Members who came from a legacy platform may know Property Type as Class and subtypes as Property Type. Class had to be selected before searching.

Start with any filter and add a property type only when it matters. One search can also cover more than one property type, such as sales and rentals together. [Filter Your Search by Property Type and Subtype](http://support.perchwell.com/baldwin/en/articles/13922158-filter-your-search-by-property-types) covers each type and its subtypes.

### Select listings to turn on Actions menu options

Select one or more listings in your search results to turn on the Actions menu options, such as Share and Print. With no listings selected, only Export CSV (comma-separated values) is available. On a legacy platform, actions such as email, print, and export were visible at all times.

Keeping these options off until you select listings leaves the page uncluttered and puts every action for your selection in one menu. [Actions Menu Options on the Search Page](http://support.perchwell.com/baldwin/en/articles/13623271-multi-listing-actions-on-the-search-page) describes each option.

### Column templates replace Spreadsheet views

Save the columns in your search results as a column template, then switch templates without leaving your results. Members who came from a legacy platform may know column templates as custom Spreadsheet views.

[Customize Your Search Results View](http://support.perchwell.com/baldwin/en/articles/11962073-customize-your-search-view) explains how to save and apply a column template.

### Each feature has its own search filter

Filter by features with each feature category's own filter on the Filters tab, such as Lot Description. Members who came from a legacy platform may know a single Features field that joined every feature field. It found listings matching any selected feature.

Perchwell does not combine separate feature filters with Or, because that logic across fields gave results that were hard to predict. A listing has to match every feature filter you set. [Create a Search with Filters](http://support.perchwell.com/baldwin/en/articles/13903380-create-a-search-with-filters-in-perchwell) explains how Or, And, and Not work within one filter.

## Listing entry changes in Perchwell

These listing entry changes in Perchwell happen in the Add/Edit form.

### One Add/Edit form covers every property type

Enter every listing in one Add/Edit form, where the fields change with the property type you select. Change the property type after you start filling in the form if you need to. Members who came from a legacy platform may know a separate form for each property type. The type could not change once the form was started.

Perchwell checks your entries against MLS and brokerage rules as you fill in the form, which means you see errors before you submit. [Add and Edit Listings](http://support.perchwell.com/baldwin/en/articles/13627189-add-and-edit-listings) walks through the form.

### AI Scribe writes a first draft of your listing description

Turn on AI Scribe in the Add/Edit form to write a first draft of the public remarks from the details you entered. On a legacy platform, agents wrote every listing description by hand.

Fair Housing Act compliance is built into AI Scribe, and you still review the draft for compliance and accuracy before you submit. [Write a Listing Description with AI Scribe](http://support.perchwell.com/baldwin/en/articles/11801815-ai-scribe) covers each AI Scribe setting.

## Client collaboration changes in Perchwell

Client collaboration in Perchwell centers on Messages and the Contacts page.

### Messages replaces CollabLink

Share listings, chat in real time, and see client reactions in Messages, Perchwell's place for client conversations. Members who came from a legacy platform may know CollabLink, a public link clients opened without logging in.

Clients sign in to Perchwell to open Messages, which keeps each conversation private. Messages is available only for clients you invite to Perchwell. [Message Clients in Perchwell](http://support.perchwell.com/baldwin/en/articles/12541968-message-clients-in-perchwell) explains how to start a conversation.

To send listings to someone without a Perchwell login, share them by public link, email, or text from the Search page. [Share Listings with Clients from Search](http://support.perchwell.com/baldwin/en/articles/13903326-share-multiple-listings-in-perchwell) covers each option.

### Invited clients manage their own email address and phone number

After you invite a client to Perchwell, only the client can change their email address and phone number, from their own account. An email address is required for every contact. On a legacy platform, contact email and phone were always editable, and email was optional.

A client signs in to Perchwell with their email address, so changing it without telling them would cut off their access. For contacts you have not invited, edit these details from the contact's profile. [Create and Manage Your Contacts in Perchwell](http://support.perchwell.com/baldwin/en/articles/14790755-create-and-manage-your-contacts-in-perchwell) explains how.

### Find emails sent to clients in your email inbox

Search your email inbox for emails Perchwell sent to your clients. Members who came from a legacy platform may know a searchable history of the emails sent to each client inside the platform.

Email services such as Gmail and Outlook already search, filter, and organize your email, so Perchwell keeps that history there. Your inbox gets a copy of each email you send from Messages and each client reply. Replies also appear in your conversation in Messages.

Saved search alerts reach your inbox only when you are a recipient. Select a Recipient option that includes you in the Edit Search window to keep a copy of each alert. [Set Up Email Alerts for Saved Searches](http://support.perchwell.com/baldwin/en/articles/11112420-set-up-email-alerts-for-saved-searches) describes each Recipient option.

## Dashboard changes in Perchwell

The Dashboard is the page Perchwell opens to. Members who came from a legacy platform may know it as the Home screen.

### Hot Sheets replace the Market Monitor

Add Hot Sheets to your Dashboard to track new and updated listings. Keep more than one side by side for different market segments. Link a saved search to a Hot Sheet first to track activity for that search, and set each timeframe in days, up to 998.

Members who came from a legacy platform may know Hot Sheets as the Market Monitor. It showed up to 30 days of activity for the MLS, firm, office, or team, filtered by Class, Type, Area, or City. Multiple Hot Sheets let you tailor each one to your market or your clients. [Add a Hot Sheet to the Dashboard](http://support.perchwell.com/baldwin/en/articles/13893409-add-a-hot-sheet-to-the-dashboard) walks through both kinds.

### BR Announcements opens from the Integrations panel

Open BR Announcements, the Baldwin REALTORS® updates site, from the Integrations panel on the right side of the Dashboard. Members who came from a legacy platform may know BR Announcements as part of the Home screen, alongside the Message Center bulletin board.

Baldwin REALTORS® keeps its announcements and bulletin board on its own site, outside the MLS platform, and BR Announcements opens it in one click. The Dashboard stays focused on activity inside Perchwell. [Open Your MLS Integrations from the Dashboard](http://support.perchwell.com/baldwin/en/articles/13903183-integration-tools-on-the-dashboard) lists every integration.

## Reports follow set layouts in Perchwell

Perchwell reports follow set layouts designed by Baldwin MLS staff and Perchwell, such as the Quick CMA (Comparative Market Analysis) and the Full Report. Choose what goes in each report on the Customize Report step of the report window.

Members who came from a legacy platform may know customizable reports, such as Agent All Fields Customizable and CMA Summary. They were set from the Preferences menu. Set layouts keep formatting consistent. The Full Report includes every detail of a listing, while reports such as the One Page Report are built for one purpose. [Reports FAQ](http://support.perchwell.com/baldwin/en/articles/15201097-reports-faq) answers common layout questions.

## Mortgage & Tax replaces the Financials calculators

Estimate monthly and lifetime mortgage payments for a listing in the Mortgage & Tax section of its Listing Detail Page. For other calculations, open a free calculator from a site such as Fannie Mae, Bankrate, or SoFi.

Members who came from a legacy platform may know the calculators under Financials, including the Financial Amortizer. Free calculators for most other scenarios are widely available online. [Listing Detail Page Overview](http://support.perchwell.com/baldwin/en/articles/14074994-listing-detail-page-overview) covers the Mortgage & Tax section.
