# Contacts Page Overview

**Description:** Manage your contacts and groups on the Contacts page, and find members of your MLS and their contact information on the My MLS tab.

Use this article to open the Contacts page in Perchwell and read the Group, Last Shared, and Status columns. Find, add, organize, and message contacts, review contact profiles, export your contact list, and find members of your MLS from the My MLS tab.

https://app.arcade.software/share/hYdohaFqPRWoEKbZzQ9P

## Open the Contacts page

Click Contacts in the top navigation to open the Contacts page. The page has two tabs:

- My Contacts: View and manage your clients and leads.
- My MLS: Find members of your MLS and view their contact information.

Invited clients do not see the My MLS tab.

## What the Group, Last Shared, and Status columns show

The Group, Last Shared, and Status columns on the My Contacts tab show key details about each contact:

- Group: The group assigned to the contact, such as Actively Searching or Referral.
- Last Shared: The most recent time you shared with the contact. Last Email means you shared by email, and Last Chat means you shared through Messages.
- Status: The contact's access to Perchwell. Uninvited means an invitation has not been sent, Invite sent means an invitation was sent, and Last Seen shows the date and time the contact last signed in.

## Find, sort, and filter contacts

Find or narrow your contact list from the My Contacts tab:

- Search: Enter a name in the Search contacts field to find a contact.
- Sort: Click a column heading, such as Last name or Status, to sort the contact list by that column.
- Filter: Click the Filters slider icon to filter contacts by group or status.

## Add a contact

Click the Add contact pencil icon on the My Contacts tab to open the Create New Contact window. Enter the contact's information and select any additional options:

- Basic Details: Enter the contact's first name, last name, and email address. These fields are required. Phone number is optional.
- Additional Emails: Click Add to include up to five additional email addresses. Notifications sent to the contact also go to these addresses in the same email thread.
- Invite to Perchwell: Turn on to invite the contact to Perchwell when you create them. Leave it off to send an invitation later.
- Create Tag: Turn on to automatically create a Client Tag for the contact. The Tag keeps listings shared with the contact in one place and can also include listings from a Saved Search.
- Bulk upload: Click Bulk upload to add multiple contacts from a CSV (comma-separated values) file.

Click Create to save the contact. To assign a group or add details such as a job title, company, or address, open the saved contact's profile and edit their information.

### Add a contact while sharing listings

Add a new contact directly from the listing share flow.

### Steps:

1. Select the listings you want to share
2. Click Actions
3. Click Share
4. Click the Select a Tag dropdown
5. Click + Create New Tag
6. Click + New Contact

The Create New Contact window opens with the same fields and options available from the My Contacts tab.

## Organize contacts into groups

Contact groups organize your clients and leads by status, relationship, or stage, such as Actively Searching, Referral, or Open House. Each contact can belong to one group at a time.

Click the Edit groups people icon to add, rename, reorder, or delete groups. To assign a contact to a group, open the contact's profile, click Edit profile, and select a group under Grouping.

[Create and Manage Your Contacts in Perchwell](https://support.perchwell.com/en/articles/14790755-create-and-manage-your-contacts-in-perchwell) walks through creating and managing contact groups.

## Message a contact or view their profile

From the My Contacts tab:

- Chat icon: Click the chat bubble icon labeled Chat in Messages next to a contact. Messages opens in a new tab with that contact selected.
- Contact name: Click a contact's name to open their profile.

The contact profile includes four tabs:

- Contact Details: View the contact's information. Click Edit profile to add or update details such as job title, company, phone numbers, or address.
- Shared Listings: View listings shared with the contact.
- Shared Searches: View searches shared with the contact.
- Shared Reports: View reports shared with the contact.

The Reverse Prospecting toggle on the Contact Details tab controls whether the contact's Saved Searches are included in Reverse Prospecting. Turn it off to exclude the contact's Saved Searches.

## Export your contact list

Click the Export contacts arrow icon on the My Contacts tab to download your contact list as a CSV file.

The export includes contacts from My Contacts only. The My MLS roster is not included.

[Export Your Contact List](https://support.perchwell.com/en/articles/12896093-export-your-contact-list) has more on exporting contacts.

## Find an MLS member on the My MLS tab

The My MLS tab is your MLS roster: it lists members of your MLS and their brokerage, office, team, email, license number, cell phone, and office phone.

### Steps:

1. Click My MLS on the Contacts page
2. Enter the member's name in Search my MLS

The roster displays members with matching names. Search my MLS searches by name only.
