# Update Your Brokerage Logo and Colors

**Description:** As a broker admin, replace your brokerage logo and set the colors for charts, analytics, and reports from My brokerage in Perchwell.

Use this article, as a broker admin, to replace your brokerage logo and change your brokerage colors.

## Replace your brokerage logo

Replace your brokerage logo from My brokerage in the profile menu. Only broker admins have access to My brokerage.

![The profile menu open in the upper right of Perchwell with My brokerage highlighted.](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/trq7czv5/2158894365/f2dccb065bb06f57ba64ad7ed473/image.png)

### Steps:

1. Hover over your profile icon in the upper right
2. Click My brokerage
3. Click the logo at the top of the left panel
4. Select an image file in the window that opens
5. Click Open or Confirm, depending on your browser

The new logo replaces your brokerage's previous logo. [confirm: whether the new logo saves right away or only after clicking Save settings]

## Change your brokerage colors for charts and reports

Change your brokerage colors from Branding in My brokerage. Color changes apply to all users in your brokerage. Branding has two sets of colors:

- Visualization colors: Sets the 8 colors for your brokerage's charts and analytics.
- Reports customization: Sets the 6 colors for reports. Click Preview CMA or Preview show sheet to see them on a report.

A hex code is the # followed by six letters and numbers that identify a color, such as #1d364d.

### Steps:

1. Hover over your profile icon in the upper right
2. Click My brokerage
3. Click Branding in the left panel
4. Click Edit settings in the upper right
5. Click the color you want to change under Visualization colors or Reports customization
6. Select a new color in the color picker, or enter a hex code
7. Click Save settings in the upper right

Your charts, analytics, and new reports show the updated colors. Reports created before the change keep their original colors.
