# Mobile App FAQ

**Description:** Questions about the Perchwell mobile app: using Perchwell on an iPad, where listing notes show, and assuming an agent's identity.

Use this article to open Perchwell on an iPad, find the listing notes you add in the Perchwell mobile app, and check where brokers and admins assume an agent's identity.

## Is there a Perchwell app for iPad?

No. There is no Perchwell app for iPad. On an iPad, open Perchwell in a web browser. Chrome is recommended. On a phone, download the Perchwell mobile app for iPhone or Android.

[Log In to the Perchwell Mobile App](http://support.perchwell.com/baldwin/en/articles/15280170-how-to-log-in-to-the-perchwell-mobile-app) explains how to download the app and log in.

## Do listing notes from the Perchwell mobile app show on my computer?

No. Listing notes you add in the Perchwell mobile app stay in the app. They do not show when you open Perchwell on your computer. Only you see your notes. To review a note, open the listing in the mobile app.

[Listing Detail Page in the Perchwell Mobile App](http://support.perchwell.com/baldwin/en/articles/14784837-listing-detail-page-overview) shows where Add Note sits on a listing.

## Can I assume an agent's identity in the Perchwell mobile app?

No. Brokers and admins assume an agent's identity only from a web browser on a computer. Assume Identity is not available in the mobile app or on a phone.

[Log In as an Agent with Assume Identity](https://support.perchwell.com/baldwin/en/articles/16053168-assume-identity) explains who can assume an agent's identity and walks through each step.
