# Universal Search Bar

**Description:** Find a listing, agent, or contact from any page in Perchwell by typing an address, MLS number, name, or email into the Universal Search Bar.

Use this article to open the Universal Search Bar from any page in Perchwell, look up a listing by address or MLS number, find an agent or contact by name, and open the matching record from the results. The Universal Search Bar is available to every member role in Perchwell. [confirm: whether invited clients see the Universal Search Bar, and whether they get agent and contact results]

## When to use the Universal Search Bar

Open the Universal Search Bar when you already know which listing, agent, or contact you want and need to reach it fast, without building a search.

- Open one listing by its address or MLS number
- Open an agent's profile by name
- Open a contact's record by name or email

For a search that filters many listings by criteria, or one you want to save and receive alerts from, build it on the **Search** page instead. [Create a Search with Filters in Perchwell](http://support.perchwell.com/baldwin/en/articles/13903380-create-a-search-with-filters-in-perchwell) walks through that.

## Open the Universal Search Bar and start a search

The Universal Search Bar is the **Search** field in the upper right of the top navigation, and it appears on every page in Perchwell, which means you do not need to go to the **Search** page first.

### Steps:

1. Click the **Search** field in the upper right of the top navigation
2. Type one of the following:
   - An address
   - An MLS number (the listing's MLS ID)
   - An agent name
   - A contact name or email
3. Click a result

Results appear in a panel below the field as you type, and the result you click opens as the listing page, the agent profile, or the contact record.

## What the Universal Search Bar returns

The Universal Search Bar returns matching records of three types: listings, agents, and contacts. The panel shows a results count at the top and groups the matches by type.

- **Listings.** Each listing card shows the address, property type, city, MLS number, price, bedrooms, bathrooms, and a status badge with its date, such as **Active Sale** or **Closed**. Listings in every status appear, including **Active**, **Pending**, **Closed**, and **Expired**. ## Limitations of the Universal Search Bar

The Universal Search Bar is built for quick lookups of one record at a time. It does not replace the **Search** page.

- **One MLS number at a time.** To look up several MLS numbers at once, open the **Search** page, find the **MLS ID** filter under **Listing Visibility & Access**, and enter the numbers separated by commas. [Search FAQ](http://support.perchwell.com/baldwin/en/articles/15200328-search-faq) covers this in detail.
- **No filters.** The Universal Search Bar does not filter by price, property type, status, or location. Build that search on the **Search** page; [Create a Search with Filters in Perchwell](http://support.perchwell.com/baldwin/en/articles/13903380-create-a-search-with-filters-in-perchwell) walks through it.
- **Cannot be saved.** A lookup in the Universal Search Bar is not saved and does not send alerts. For a search you want to keep or receive alerts from, see [Manage Your Saved Searches](http://support.perchwell.com/en/articles/8955646-manage-your-saved-searches).
