Delete a Draft Listing

Use the Listing Management form to delete a listing that is still in Draft status.

Steps

  1. Go to Manage Listings: https://www.perchwell.com/listings

  2. Locate the draft listing you want to delete.

  3. Click the pencil icon on the right side of the listing row.

  4. In the lower-left corner of the Listing Management form, click Delete.

⚠️ This process applies only to draft listings.

📖 Learn more about navigating your listing inventory in Manage Listings Page Overview.
