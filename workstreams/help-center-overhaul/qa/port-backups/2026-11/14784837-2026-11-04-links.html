Use this article to review a listing's details and photos, contact the listing agent, share, tag, or edit the listing from the three-dots menu, and check its location, mortgage estimates, and history on the Listing Detail Page in the Perchwell mobile app.

Review listing details, photos, and agent contacts on the Listing Detail Page

Review a listing's photos, asking price, association fee, DOM (days on market), and CDOM (cumulative days on market) at the top of the Listing Detail Page in the mobile app. Tap any photo to open the photo gallery.

Below the summary, the listing's details are grouped into sections. Tap a section's name to expand or collapse it. The sections are:

  • Description

  • Listing Details

  • Property Details

  • Features

  • Rooms

  • Map

  • Mortgage & Tax

  • History

Listing Details is for agents only, which means clients and anyone viewing the listing from a public link do not see it.

To contact the listing agent, tap Contact Agent at the bottom of the Listing Detail Page, then choose to text or email them.

Listing Detail Page Overview covers the same page in the browser.

Share, tag, report on, or edit a listing from the three-dots menu

Tap the three-dots icon in the upper right of the Listing Detail Page to open the listing's menu, with Actions and Integrations tabs.

Actions tab options on the Listing Detail Page

The Actions tab of the three-dots menu holds nine options:

  • Send via Email: Select a contact to email the listing to.

  • Copy link: Copy the listing's link to paste anywhere.

  • Send via messages: Send the listing to a client in Messages.

  • Share externally: Open your phone's share options, such as Messages, AirDrop, your email app, or WhatsApp.

  • Add Note: Add a private note to the listing. Only you see the note, and it stays in the mobile app.

  • Add Tag: Add the listing to a new or existing Tag.

  • One Pager: Create a one-page PDF of the listing with a QR code that opens its Listing Detail Page.

  • Create Report: Create a CMA, Extended CMA, Listing Report, Show Sheet, or Media Report for the listing.

  • Auto CMA: Open a search for comparable listings, with this listing as the subject property of a CMA (Comparative Market Analysis).

For more information:

  • How to Tag Individual Listings covers adding a listing to a Tag.

  • Create One Page Reports covers the agent and client versions of a one-page PDF.

  • Create a CMA with a Subject Property walks through the comparable listings search.

Open an integration tool from the Integrations tab

Tap Integrations in the three-dots menu, then tap a tool to open it for the listing in your phone's browser. Integrations are services from other companies that your MLS provides, such as property reports and showing scheduling. The Integrations tab includes:

  • RPR (Realtors Property Resource): Open property reports, valuation tools, and market data.

  • ShowingTime: Schedule and manage showings.

  • SentriLock: Manage lockbox access.

  • InfoSparks: View market statistics and charts.

  • CRS tools: Open CRS Comparables, CRS Demographic, CRS Listing Integrity, CRS Parcel Map, or CRS Property Report.

Edit a listing from the Admin tab

Tap Admin in the three-dots menu, then tap Edit Listing to open the listing in Add/Edit. The Admin tab appears on your own listings and for admins. Add and Edit Listings explains how to change a listing.

Check the listing's location in the Map section

Expand the Map section on the Listing Detail Page to view the listing on a map:

  • Map, Satellite, or Terrain: Tap to change the map type.

  • Street View: Tap to view street-level imagery of the property.

  • Directions: Tap to open directions to the listing in Apple Maps on an iPhone, or in Google Maps on an Android phone.

Estimate monthly and lifetime payments in Mortgage & Tax

Expand the Mortgage & Tax section on the Listing Detail Page to estimate the monthly and lifetime cost of buying the listing. Change the interest rate, sale price, down payment, or loan term to update the estimates. Interest rate starts at 7% and Down payment at 20%.

Members who came from a legacy platform may know Mortgage & Tax as the Financial Amortizer.

Review price and status changes in History

Expand the History section on the Listing Detail Page to review the listing's price, status changes, and previous postings. Each change shows its date, its type, the price, DOM, and who made the change.

History is hidden from anyone viewing the listing from a public link.
