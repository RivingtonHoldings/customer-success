Dashboard integrations access

Use the Integrations panel on your Dashboard to launch supported tools in a new window.

Steps:

  1. Click Dashboard in the top navigation

  2. Hover on the right side of the page to reveal the Integrations panel

  3. Click an integration name to open it in a new window

💡 Integrations may vary based on your MLS permissions. Some integrations may require an active account or separate login credentials.

Integration Tools

Each integration supports a specific workflow. Use the list below to choose the right tool.

Public records and property insights

  • Realtors Property Resource (RPR)

    Generate reports and market insights to support pricing conversations.

    • Open property reports

    • Review market analytics and comps (when available)

    • Reference valuation tools and neighborhood insights
      ​

Showings and lockbox access

  • ShowingTime

    Manage showings for eligible listings.

    • Schedule showings

    • Edit or cancel upcoming appointments

    • Track showing activity and updates

    Also accessible from eligible Listing Details pages.
    ​

  • SentriLock

    Manage lockbox access when supported by your MLS.

    • Request or manage lockbox access

    • Review entry history (when available)

    • Coordinate access for agents and teams (when available)

  • Infosparks

    View listing analytics.

    • Customize your results with filters

    • Visualize listing data

    • Compare across different geographic areas
      ​

MLS announcements

  • BR Announcements

    View Baldwin REALTORS® updates inside Perchwell.

    • Read current announcements

    • Check updates and deadlines

    • Stay informed on policy or MLS changes

Listing Details integrations
