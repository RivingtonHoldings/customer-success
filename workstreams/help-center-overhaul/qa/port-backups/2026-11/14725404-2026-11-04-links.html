Use this article to see what moved from Paragon to Perchwell and what did not, review your saved searches and turn their alerts back on, update your contacts, and finish your draft listings.

What moved from Paragon to Perchwell

Your listings, contacts, saved searches, and partial listings moved from Paragon to Perchwell:

  • Listings: Every listing is in Perchwell, which is now the system of record for listings. That means you create and update listings in Perchwell only.

  • Contacts: Every contact moved in a one-time transfer the week before your Perchwell access date. Changes made in Paragon after that date are not in Perchwell.

  • Saved searches: Every saved search Paragon made available moved in the same one-time transfer, with its alerts turned off. If a contact was tied to a save search then that also transferred over.

  • Partial listings: Every partial listing created on July 20th, 2026 moved to Perchwell as a draft listing, which means it is saved but not yet submitted to the MLS.

The data that moved is only as accurate as the data Paragon provided.

What did not move from Paragon to Perchwell

These items from Paragon did not move to Perchwell:

  • The history of listings you shared with each contact

  • Contacts' notes and reactions on listings

  • Market Monitor settings. Add a Hot Sheet to your Dashboard to track new and updated listings, as explained in Add a Hot Sheet to the Dashboard.

  • Listing Carts. Create a Tag to save and share listings with clients, as explained in How to Create a Tag.

Review saved searches moved from Paragon

Review each saved search moved from Paragon, because some of its filters may have been dropped or changed in the move. A missing or changed filter changes which listings the saved search finds. Saved searches that still need review start with [From Paragon: Unreviewed] in their names.

Steps:

  1. Click Saved Searches in the upper left of the Search page

  2. Click the name of a saved search that starts with [From Paragon: Unreviewed]

  3. Check the saved search's filters and the listings in your results

  4. Add or change any filter that is missing or wrong

  5. Click Save

  6. Select Update Current

The saved search keeps your changes. Its name still starts with [From Paragon: Unreviewed] until you rename it, and its alerts stay off until you turn them on.

Check the Contacts column in the My Searches window to confirm each saved search is linked to the right contacts. Your Data: Saved Searches from Paragon lists the Paragon filters most likely to have changed.

Rename a reviewed saved search and turn its alerts back on

Every saved search moved from Paragon has its alerts turned off, which means no one receives alert emails for it until you turn them on. After you review a saved search, remove the [From Paragon: Unreviewed] prefix from its name and turn on its alerts.

Steps:

  1. Click Saved Searches in the upper left of the Search page

  2. Click the pencil icon next to the saved search to open the Edit Search window

  3. Delete [From Paragon: Unreviewed] from the Name this search field

  4. Turn on Alerts

  5. Click Update

A green "Your saved search has been updated." message appears in the lower right, and the saved search's name no longer starts with the prefix. Recipient is set to Only me unless you changed it in step 5. Set Up Email Alerts for Saved Searches explains how often alerts are sent and each Recipient option.

Update contacts moved from Paragon

Update your contacts moved from Paragon before you share with them. They arrived uninvited, and some have no email address. A contact with no email address is left out when you share listings, saved searches, or reports, or send a message.

Find and Update Contacts Moved from Paragon explains how to add a missing email address and invite a contact to Perchwell. To tell your clients about the move, copy an email from Agent-to-Client Transition Email Templates.

Finish draft listings moved from Paragon

Partial listings moved from Paragon are draft listings in Perchwell. Find them with the Publication status filter on the Manage Listings page, then finish each one in the Add/Edit form.
