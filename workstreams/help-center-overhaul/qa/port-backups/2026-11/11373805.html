Saved search alerts overview

Saved search alerts notify you when new listings match the search criteria your agent set up.

  • Get notified when new matches hit the market

  • Open the listing directly from the alert

  • React to a listing or message your agent after you review it

Alert delivery channels

Your agent sets the alert schedule.

  • Receive Email notifications

  • Review new matches in your Perchwell account

Some users also receive mobile push notifications.

Alert email listing access

Steps:

  1. Open the alert email

  2. Click a listing in the email

Perchwell opens the listing so you can review details, react, or message your agent.

In-app new match access

Steps:

  1. Log in to Perchwell

  2. Open your Saved Search

  3. Sort results by Newest First

Alert contents

Each alert includes:

  • A summary of newly matched listings

  • Links to open full listing details

  • A way to message your agent

Tips

  • Alerts start automatically after your agent sets up your saved search.

  • Email alerts and in-platform results reflect the same live data.
