Listing sharing purpose

Share listings to keep your home search organized and give your agent clear direction.

  • Highlight homes you like

  • Ask questions about a property

  • Request a showing

  • Keep a record of what you discussed

Share listings from Search

Share one listing or multiple listings from Search.

Send by Email

Send listings by email when you want to include a longer message or send options in one batch.

Steps:

  1. Open the Search page

  2. Switch to the results view by clicking Search

  3. Select the checkboxes next to the listings you want to share

  4. Click Actions

  5. Choose Share via Email

  6. Select your agent or enter additional email addresses

  7. Update the subject line and message

  8. Click Email

Send by Messages

Send listings in Messages when you want the listing to appear in your conversation thread.

Steps:

  1. Open Search page

  2. Switch to the results view by clicking Search

  3. Select the checkboxes next to the listings you want to share

  4. Click Actions

  5. Choose Share via Messages

  6. Click your agent

  7. Click Share

  8. Type your message

  9. Click Send

Share listings from the Listing Detail Page

Share a listing while you are viewing the property.

Steps:

  1. Open a listing to view the Listing Detail Page

  2. Click Share

  3. Select how you want to share

    • Send via Email

    • Send via Messages

Send by Email

Steps:

  1. Select Send via Email

  2. Select your agent or enter additional email addresses

  3. Update the subject line and message

  4. Click Email

Send by Messages

Steps:

  1. Select Send via Messages

  2. Click the notepad icon

  3. Select your agent

  4. Click Start Conversation

  5. Type your message

  6. Click Send

Create communication templates

Build reusable email templates for the outreach you send over and over, then pull them up anywhere you contact an agent or client. Set them up once in Settings and stop rewriting the same message.
