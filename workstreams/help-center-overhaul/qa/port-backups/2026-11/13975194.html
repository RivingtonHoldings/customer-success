Contacts page overview

Use Contacts as your hub for managing client relationships.

  • Add contacts before you share listings and reports.

  • Invite clients to Perchwell when they need a client portal.

  • Organize contacts with groups.

  • Review shared activity from the Contact Detail Page.

Contacts page access

Steps:

  1. Click Contacts in the top navigation

From Contacts, you can:

  1. Click Add Contact

  2. Import contacts with Bulk Upload

  3. Open a contact profile

  4. Export contacts

  5. Create a group

Contact creation

Create a contact when you onboard a client or before you share listings and reports.

Steps:

  1. Click Add Contact

  2. Enter the required fields

    • First name

    • Last name

    • Email address

  3. Add optional details

  4. Turn on Invite to Perchwell to send an invitation

  5. Click Add Contact

💡 Add a new contact while sharing by clicking Add Contact in the share flow.

Import contacts with Bulk Upload

Upload a CSV to add multiple contacts at once.

  • Click Add Contact

  • Select Bulk Upload

Organize contacts with groups

Use groups to segment contacts by status, relationship, or workflow.

  • Actively searching

  • Sold

  • Open house leads

Group assignment

Steps:

  1. Open Contacts

  2. Click a contact name

  3. Click Edit Profile

  4. In Grouping, assign the contact to one or more groups

  5. Click Save Profile

💡 Use Filters to find contacts in a specific group.

Shared activity review (Contact Detail Page)

Use the contact profile to see what you shared and what the contact engaged with.

Steps:

  1. Open Contacts

  2. Click a contact name to open the Contact Detail Page

From the Contact Detail Page, review:

  • Shared listings

  • Shared searches

  • Shared reports
