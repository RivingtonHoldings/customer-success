Share a Tag with a public link

Generate a link you can send however you'd like: by text, email, or on your own website.

Steps:

  1. Click Tags in the navigation bar.

  2. Find the Tag and click Share.

  3. Click Copy Link.

  4. Send the link to your client.

A success toast confirms the link copied. The client does not need a Perchwell account to open it.

Share a Tag by email

Send the Tag directly from Perchwell instead of copying a link out.

Steps:

  1. Click Tags in the navigation bar.

  2. Find the Tag and click Share.

  3. Enter the client's email in the Share via Email field.

  4. Click Send.

What your client sees

Opening the link brings the client to a branded page showing the Tag's listings. No login required.

  • They can browse each listing's details by clicking the listing.

  • They can like or dislike listings. Liked listings are added back to your Tag automatically, and they can undo a reaction at any time.

Share again

Send the same Tag's link to a client again, or notify a newly-added client after the fact.

Steps:

  1. Click Tags in the navigation bar.

  2. Find the Tag and click Re-Share.

  3. Copy the link, or send it again by email.
