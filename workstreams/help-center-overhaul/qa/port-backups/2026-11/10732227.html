Bulk Upload use cases

Your contacts from Paragon will already be in the platform, but use Bulk Upload when you need to import multiple contacts at once in the future.

  • Move contacts from your selected CRM into Perchwell

  • Add a large client list from any outside resource during onboarding

Bulk Upload access (Contacts)

Steps:

  1. Open Contacts

  2. Click Add Contact

  3. Select Bulk Upload

CSV preparation

Use the template to make sure your file matches the required format.

Steps:

  1. In the Bulk Upload window, click CSV Template

  2. Enter your contacts in the spreadsheet

    • First Name

    • Last Name

    • Email Address

  3. Save the file as a CSV

Requirements and limits

  • Include First Name, Last Name, and Email Address for each contact.

  • Use a unique email address for each contact. Duplicate emails are not allowed.

  • Keep the template column headers exactly as-is. Do not rename or reorder them.

Upload and confirmation

Steps:

  1. Drag and drop your CSV into the upload area, or click the upload area to browse for your file

  2. Click Next

  3. Review the import summary

    • If you see errors, update your spreadsheet, then upload the CSV again

  4. Click Next to finalize

After the upload finishes, Perchwell adds the contacts to Contacts

Tips

  • When exporting a CSV from Apple Pages, deselect Include header names.

  • If the upload fails, start by re-downloading the CSV Template and pasting your data into the correct columns.
