Use this article to find your saved searches moved from Paragon and their contacts, review and fix their filters, mark each search reviewed, and turn its alerts back on.

Find saved searches moved from Paragon

Click Saved Searches, or the name of the open saved search, in the upper left of the Search page to open the My Searches window. Each saved search moved from Paragon starts with [From Paragon: Unreviewed] in its name.

Saved searches moved in a one-time transfer the week before your Perchwell access date. A saved search created in Paragon after that date did not move, so recreate it in Perchwell.

Find the contacts linked to saved searches moved from Paragon

Saved searches moved from Paragon stay linked to the contacts they had in Paragon. Find those contacts in two places:

  • Contacts column: Lists each saved search's contacts in the My Searches window, which opens from Saved Searches on the Search page.

  • Shared Searches tab: Lists every saved search linked to one contact, on the left side of that contact's page.

A link moved only when Paragon sent both the contact and the saved search with the link between them. To add a missing contact, click the pencil icon next to the search in the My Searches window. Select the contact from the Contacts dropdown in the Edit Search window, then click Update. Manage Your Saved Searches explains sharing a saved search with a contact.

Why saved searches moved from Paragon need review

Saved searches moved from Paragon may be missing filters or carry filters that changed in Perchwell, which means a search may find different listings than it did in Paragon.

Paragon dropped some filters when it sent saved searches to Perchwell, with no consistent pattern. For example, a search for condos with 2 or more bedrooms in Gulf Shores may have arrived without its Bedrooms filter.

Baldwin REALTORS® MLS also changed some fields to follow RESO (Real Estate Standards Organization) standards, so a saved search that used one of them needs a new filter. The table below lists the Paragon filters that most often need replacing in a saved search, and what to select in Perchwell instead.

Paragon filter

What to select in Perchwell

Active New Construction status

The Active status, with Construction Status set to Under Construction

Other property type, with the Boat Slip, Fractional Ownership, or Garage subtype

The Residential-Single Family property type, with the Boat Slip, Fractional Ownership, or Garage subtype

More than 2 Pets Allowed?

The More than 2 pets allowed option in Community Amenities

All Features, with Or between features in different groups

The filter for each feature on the Filters tab, such as Community Amenities or Pool Features

Paragon's Class is Property Type in Perchwell, and Paragon's Property Type is the subtype. Listing Field Changes in Perchwell lists every field that changed.

Review and update a saved search moved from Paragon

Check the filters of each saved search moved from Paragon on the Search page, then save your changes to the same search.

Steps:

  1. Click Saved Searches, or the name of the open saved search, in the upper left of the Search page

  2. Click the name of a saved search that starts with [From Paragon: Unreviewed]

  3. Click Filters in the upper right of the Search page

  4. Compare Applied Filters with what the search is meant to find

  5. Add any filter the search is missing

  6. Replace any filter listed in the table under Why saved searches moved from Paragon need review

  7. Click Save

  8. Click Update Current in the Save Search window

The saved search keeps your changes, and its name still starts with [From Paragon: Unreviewed] until you rename it.

Mark a saved search moved from Paragon as reviewed

Remove [From Paragon: Unreviewed] from a saved search's name once you have reviewed it, so the prefix marks only the searches left to check.

Steps:

  1. Click Saved Searches, or the name of the open saved search, in the upper left of the Search page

  2. Click the pencil icon next to the saved search to edit it

  3. Delete [From Paragon: Unreviewed] from the Name this search field

  4. Click Update

A green "Your saved search has been updated." message appears in the lower right, and the name in the My Searches window no longer carries the prefix.

Turn on alerts for a saved search moved from Paragon

Alerts were turned off on saved searches moved from Paragon, which means no one receives emails for a search until you turn them back on. Click Saved Searches, or the name of the open saved search, in the upper left of the Search page to open the My Searches window. Click the bell icon next to the saved search to turn its alerts on. The icon turns green.

To send alerts to the search's contacts too, click the pencil icon next to the search and select a Recipient option that includes them, then click Update. Set Up Email Alerts for Saved Searches explains how often alerts send and who receives them.
