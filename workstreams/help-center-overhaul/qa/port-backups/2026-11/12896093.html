Export use cases

Export contacts when you need a CSV copy of your database.

  • Share your contact list externally

  • Create a data backup

  • Import contacts into another platform

CSV export

Steps:

  1. Click Contacts in the top navigation

  2. Click the Export icon

Perchwell downloads a CSV file to your browser’s default download location.

Tips

  • The file will download to your browser’s default location, such as your Downloads folder or desktop.

  • To delete a contact, open the contact’s Contact Detail Page and select Delete.

  • The export includes all visible contacts in your database.
