Contacts Page Overview

Use Contacts as your hub for managing client relationships.

  • Add contacts before you share listings and reports.

  • Invite clients to Perchwell when they need a client portal.

  • Organize contacts with groups.

  • Review shared activity from the Contact Detail Page.

Access Contacts

Steps:

  1. Tap Home in the bottom navigation

  2. Select Contacts

From Contacts, you can:

  • Tap a contact name to open their profile, or

  • Tap Actions to add a new contact or edit groups.

Create a Contact

Create a contact when you onboard a new client or before you share listings and reports.

Steps:

  1. Tap Home in the bottom navigation

  2. Choose Contacts

  3. Tap Actions

  4. Enter the required fields:

    1. First name

    2. Last name

    3. Email address

  5. Add any optional details

  6. Toggle on Invite to Perchwell to send the client an invitation to their portal

  7. Tap Add Contact

Organize Contacts with Groups

Use groups to segment contacts by status, relationship, or stage in your workflow. Examples include:

  • Actively searching

  • Sold

  • Open house leads

Assign a Contact to a Group

Steps:

  1. Tap Home in the bottom navigation

  2. Choose Contacts

  3. Tap a contact name to open their profile

  4. Select Edit

  5. In the Grouping dropdown, assign the contact to a group

  6. Save your changes

💡 Tip: Use Filters on the Contacts page to find all contacts assigned to a specific group.

Review Shared Activity

Use the Contact Detail Page to see what you have shared with a client and how they have engaged with it.

Steps:

  1. Tap Home in the bottom navigation

  2. Choose Contacts

  3. Tap a contact name to open the Contact Detail Page

From the Contact Detail Page, you can review:

  • Shared searches

  • Shared reports

Tips

  • After you invite a contact to Perchwell, you cannot update their email address.

  • You can add up to five additional email addresses to a single contact.
