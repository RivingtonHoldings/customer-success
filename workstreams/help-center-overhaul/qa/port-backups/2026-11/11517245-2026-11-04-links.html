Use this article to filter listings by open house date with the Open house filter.

Find open houses by date on the Filters tab

The Open house filter under Listing Details finds listings with an open house on the dates you select.

Steps:

  1. Click the Filters tab

  2. Enter Open house in the Search filters and values field

  3. Click the Select date range field

  4. Click a date on the calendar

  5. (Optional) Click a later date to select a date range

  6. Click Submit

Your search results update to show listings with an open house on that date or within that range. To remove a selected date, click it again.

Find upcoming open houses within a set number of days

Click 30, 60, 180, or 365 days on the Open house calendar to select a range starting today. For example, 30 days covers today through the next 30 days.

Filter by open house date from the Open house column

Add the Open house column to filter by date from your search results and see when each open house is scheduled. Customize Your Search View explains how to add columns. Columns are available in List and Expanded views.

Steps:

  1. Click the three-line icon in the Open house column header to open the filter

  2. Click a date on the calendar

  3. (Optional) Click a later date to select a date range

Your search results update as soon as you click a date. The same Open house filter appears under Applied Filters on the Filters tab.

Remove the Open house filter from your search

Remove the Open house filter from either the Filters or Results tab:

  • Filters tab: Click the x next to the selected dates or on the Open house filter under Applied Filters.

  • Results tab: Click the x in the Open house column header.

Your search results update.
