# Golden questions: Baldwin

Run: 2026-08-03-to-2026-08-17 | Window: 2026-08-03 to 2026-08-17 (the two weeks after cutover) | Pool: 585 Baldwin conversations where Fin took part | Sampled: 25 | Built: 2026-09-04

Fin resolution in the sample, Perchwell definition (Fin answered, no teammate message afterward, no same-topic follow-up from the member within 48 hours): 13 of 25 resolved, 12 not. A teammate stepped in on 12 of 25. Intercom's own states for the same 25: assumed resolution 12, confirmed resolution 5, escalated 8. The two are reported side by side, never blended. Every member is "the member" and every Perchwell person is "a teammate." Paraphrases are quoted from the conversations with identifiers removed and typos left in. Where the sample held fewer than three real phrasings, the shortfall is noted; Session 2 should widen the sample to fill them.

## 1. I logged in and only see my profile page. Where is the Dashboard?

- Volume: 2 of 25 sampled (8 percent)
- Paraphrases:
  - "When I log in, it brings me to my Profile page only. How do I find the dashboard to search listings, etc.?"
  - "I can not locate my dashboard. Takes me directly to profile page."
  - "There is no Dashboard icon. I am on desktop, but I need to have the Dashboard icon." (three phrasings from two conversations)
- Correct answer: The Dashboard is desktop only, so it never appears in the mobile app. On desktop, a member who lands on the Profile page with no Dashboard link is usually signed in as a personal or office assistant, which means their account only gains access after a broker or team lead grants them Assume Identity permission; they then use Log in As from the profile menu. A hard refresh fixes the rarer case where the page simply did not load.
- Target Baldwin article: [Dashboard Overview](../../../docs/help-center/baldwin/dashboard-overview.md) and [Login, Access & Troubleshooting Guide and FAQ](../../../docs/help-center/baldwin/login-access-troubleshooting-guide-and-faq.md). Gap: neither article explains that assistant accounts see only the Profile page until Assume Identity is granted.
- CRMLS equivalent: none found in the mirror
- How Fin handled it: answered both times with "Dashboard is desktop only" and browser troubleshooting, cited Dashboard Overview and the login FAQ. One member got no further and was closed by a teammate; the other pushed back and a teammate diagnosed assistant permissions and fixed them. Both count as unresolved under the Perchwell definition in one case and resolved in the other only because the member stopped replying.

## 2. Does what I had in Paragon (carts, shared listings, contacts, saved searches) carry over, and how do I check?

- Volume: 2 of 25 sampled (8 percent)
- Paraphrases:
  - "In Paragon, I have listings saved in Carts for some of my contacts. Will these migrate over to perchwell? How do I access the listings I have in the contacts cart?"
  - "How do I verify my migrated contacts and saved searches?"
  - (only two real phrasings in this sample)
- Correct answer: Contacts and saved searches migrated from Paragon; migrated searches carry a "[From Paragon: Unreviewed]" label until the member reviews the filters and renames them. Paragon carts and listings shared through Paragon did not migrate, which means the Shared Listings tab on a contact shows only what was shared from Perchwell, and the member re-shares from Search if they want them there.
- Target Baldwin article: [Your Data: Contacts from Paragon](../../../docs/help-center/baldwin/your-data-contacts-from-paragon.md), [Your Data: Saved Searches from Paragon](../../../docs/help-center/baldwin/your-data-saved-searches-from-paragon.md), [Manage Your Saved Searches](../../../docs/help-center/baldwin/manage-your-saved-searches.md). Gap: no Baldwin article states plainly that carts do not migrate.
- CRMLS equivalent: [Manage Your Saved Searches](../../../docs/help-center/crmls/manage-your-saved-searches.md) for the saved-search half; migration content will need a CRMLS version once the source platform is confirmed
- How Fin handled it: answered both, cited "Baldwin's Conversion FAQs" (an article that lives in the default help center, not the Baldwin one) plus a Fin snippet. Both resolved with no teammate.

## 3. How do I share the same listings with another client, or send several listings in one link?

- Volume: 2 of 25 sampled (8 percent)
- Paraphrases:
  - "how can i send a list of shared listings that I sent to one client and share them with another"
  - "I have five properties that I have put in messages but would like to send to my client in this format. How do I do that?"
  - (only two real phrasings in this sample)
- Correct answer: From Search, check the listings, click Actions, then Share, and choose Email or Public Link; a public link sends the whole set as one carousel the client can scroll. The same listings can be sent inside Messages with Share via Messages. Re-sharing to a second client is the same steps with the new client selected.
- Target Baldwin article: [Share Multiple Listings in Perchwell](../../../docs/help-center/baldwin/share-multiple-listings-in-perchwell.md)
- CRMLS equivalent: none found in the mirror (CRMLS has "Sharing Listings with Clients" in a different shape)
- How Fin handled it: answered both with the Search, Actions, Share steps and cited the article. One resolved with no teammate; in the other a teammate sent a courtesy check-in, which counts as intervention under the Perchwell definition even though the member had already succeeded.

## 4. Where are my draft listings, and why is Save Draft greyed out?

- Volume: 2 of 25 sampled (8 percent)
- Paraphrases:
  - "Where di I find drafts"
  - "I just want to save a partial listing that doesn't go Active until Monday. Save draft is greyed out."
  - "It will not let me change or address what's in red?" (three phrasings from two conversations)
- Correct answer: Drafts live on the Manage Listings page; open Filter on the right and choose Drafts under Publication Status to see only drafts. Save Draft stays greyed out until the required fields in Listing Information and Listing Details are filled, which means a partial listing still needs those fields before it can be saved.
- Target Baldwin article: [Manage Listings Page Overview](../../../docs/help-center/baldwin/manage-listings-page-overview.md) and [Add and Edit Listings](../../../docs/help-center/baldwin/add-and-edit-listings.md). Gap flagged by a teammate in-thread: the answer needs to start from where the Filter button is, and the required-fields rule for Save Draft is not explicit.
- CRMLS equivalent: none found in the mirror
- How Fin handled it: answered both and cited both articles, but skipped the Filter step in one and the required-fields rule in the other; a teammate corrected both. Unresolved under the Perchwell definition.

## 6. How do I set a buyer up to receive listings?

- Volume: 1 of 25 sampled (4 percent)
- Paraphrases:

  - (only one real phrasing in this sample)

## 15. Why do I get zero results for closed sales over a long window?

- Volume: 1 of 25 sampled (4 percent)
- Paraphrases:
  - "I continue to get zero search results from searches I did many times on Paragon."
  - "I'm trying to do a search for closed 2nd tier on W Beach Blvd 2nd tier houses for the past ten years, and zero properties are coming up."
  - "why isn't my searches coming up" (three phrasings from one conversation)
- Correct answer: Set Property Status to Closed, then set the Activity date range wide enough for the window, for example 3650 days for ten years; a narrow Activity range hides closed listings that did not change in that period. Water-related tiers are a Water property type filter, not a keyword.
- Target Baldwin article: [Understand Status and Activity Filter](../../../docs/help-center/baldwin/understand-status-and-activity-filter.md) and [Search FAQ](../../../docs/help-center/baldwin/search-faq.md). Gap: the Water property type filter is not documented for Baldwin.
- CRMLS equivalent: [Understand Status and Activity Filter](../../../docs/help-center/crmls/understand-status-and-activity-filter.md)
- How Fin handled it: answered six times with progressively different theories and cited six sources; the member stayed frustrated and a teammate gave the exact filters. Unresolved under the Perchwell definition; Intercom marked it assumed resolution.
