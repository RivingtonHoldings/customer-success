# Invite Clients to Perchwell and Resend Invitations

Use this article to see what invited clients can access in Perchwell, invite clients as you add a contact, send or resend an invitation from a contact's profile, invite clients during a bulk upload, and invite multiple contacts at once.

# What invited clients can access in Perchwell

Invited clients get a client portal, their own Perchwell account where you collaborate on their home search.

Invited clients can:
- Search for listings
- View listings and reports you share with them
- Chat with you in Messages

Invited clients cannot:
- View agent-only listing details
- Message other clients
- Message other agents
- See listing agent information
- View listing documents that are not marked Public
- View internal notes or brokerage-specific data

[Accept Your Agent's Perchwell Invitation](https://support.perchwell.com/baldwin/en/articles/15521326-accept-your-agent-s-perchwell-invitation) walks clients through accepting the invitation and creating a password.

# Invite a client when you add a new contact

Invite a client to Perchwell as you add them on the Contacts page. To include a personal message, create the contact first, then invite them from their profile. A saved invitation email template is added automatically when Invite to Perchwell is on.

## Steps:
1. Click Add New in the upper right of the Contacts page
2. Enter the contact's first name, last name, and email address
3. Turn on Invite to Perchwell
4. Click Create

The contact receives an invitation email. Their Status on the My Contacts tab changes to Invite sent.

# Invite a contact or resend an invitation from their profile

Invite a contact to Perchwell, or resend an invitation a client cannot find, from the contact's profile on the Contacts page. If the client already accepted and cannot sign in, ask them to click Forgot Password on the login page instead.

> **Tip:** Ask the client to check their Promotions, Spam, or Junk folders before you resend. The email comes from Perchwell, with the subject You've been invited to join Perchwell.

## Steps:
1. Click the contact's name on the My Contacts tab
2. Under Account status, click Invite contact to Perchwell, or Reinvite contact to Perchwell if the contact was already invited
3. Enter a message for the client
4. Click Send Invite

The contact receives an invitation email.

# Invite clients during a bulk upload

Invite multiple clients to Perchwell as you bulk upload them from a CSV (comma-separated values) file on the Contacts page. Leave the invitation toggle off to add contacts now and invite each one later from their profile.

## Steps:
1. Click Upload in the upper right of the Contacts page
2. Drag your completed CSV file into the upload area, or click Browse or drag and drop
3. Turn on invitations
4. Click Next
5. Review the import summary
6. Click Next

Each contact in the file receives an invitation email.

# Invite multiple contacts at once

Invite up to 100 contacts at once from the Actions menu on the Contacts page. Select each contact, click Actions, then select Invite to Perchwell. A message is required, and every selected contact receives the same message, so invite a client from their profile when you want to write to them personally. [Contacts Page Overview](https://support.perchwell.com/baldwin/en/articles/13975194-contacts-page-overview) covers the other options in the Actions menu.

# Things to Know

> **Important:** After you invite a contact to Perchwell, the contact's first name, last name, email, and phone number are locked. To change a locked field, contact Perchwell Support.
- Perchwell sends each invitation to the contact's email address, which means the client accepts it from that inbox.
- Invitation emails come from [no-reply@perchwell.com](mailto:no-reply@perchwell.com) with your name as the sender, and replies go to you.
