# Broker/Admin Controls & Managing Your Firm in Perchwell

This session is for brokers and office administrators and covers how to manage your firm or office in Perchwell. We’ll review role differences, how to view another user’s account, Manage People tools, brokerage branding, office email templates, and office-level search sharing. We’ll also cover admin mobile capabilities. Ends with live Q&A.
