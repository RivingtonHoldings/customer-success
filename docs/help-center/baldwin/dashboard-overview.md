# Dashboard Overview

# Dashboard overview

The **Dashboard** is your central workspace in Perchwell. It surfaces real-time market activity with hot sheets, listings, saved searches, and contact information.

# Dashboard widgets

Widgets are customizable tiles that display key information.

Select the **Manage widgets** button in the top right corner of your screen to customize your layout.

# Hot Sheets

Hot sheets allow you to track real-time market activity and updates automatically based on current MLS data.

**📖 Visit this article for step-by-step guidance on [adding hot sheets](https://intercom.help/perchwell-help-center-baldwin/en/articles/13893409-adding-hot-sheets-to-the-dashboard) to your Dashboard.**

# Contacts Widget

The Contacts widget allows you to stay connected to your clients by storing their information in one place.

**📖 Visit this article for step-by-step guidance on [creating and managing Contacts](https://intercom.help/perchwell-help-center-baldwin/en/articles/8955819-creating-and-managing-contacts-in-perchwell).**

# Saved Searches Widget

The Saved Searches widget allows you to easily access the searches you have saved.

**📖 Visit this article for step-by-step guidance on [managing saved searches](https://intercom.help/perchwell-help-center-baldwin/en/articles/8955646-managing-saved-searches).**

# Tags Widget

The Tags widget allows you to manage your tags like you have with carts, so you can organize listings, track updates, and share properties with your clients.

**📖 Visit this article for step-by-step guidance on [creating and managing tags](https://intercom.help/perchwell-help-center-baldwin/en/articles/14465546-tags-overview-sharing-alerts).**

# Listings Widget

The Listings widget allows you to view and manage your listings.

**📖 Visit this article for step-by-step guidance on [managing listings](https://intercom.help/perchwell-help-center-baldwin/en/articles/13623016-navigating-the-manage-listings-page).**

# Integrated tools access

The dashboard allows you to launch your real estate products.

Hover over the right side of the screen to access your integrations, such as:
- **ShowingTime:** Schedule and manage property showings
- **SentriLock:** Manage secure lockbox access
- **BR Announcements:** View Baldwin REALTORS**®** updates
- **Inforsparks:** View listing analytics
- **CRS:** View property information system to access public records, tax data, and maps
- **RPR:** Access Realtors Property Resources for additional property data

**📖 Visit this article for step-by-step guidance on the [third-party integrations](https://intercom.help/perchwell-help-center-baldwin/en/articles/13903183-integration-tools-on-the-dashboard).**

# Things to Know
- Dashboard and Hot Sheets are not available on the Perchwell mobile app. You can only view and access these areas on desktop.
