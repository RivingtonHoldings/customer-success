# Analytics FAQ

## Will agents have full access to all analytics reports, or will some be broker/admin-only like in Paragon?

Agents will have full access to all analytics reports in Perchwell.

## How many charts can I have in Search Analytics?

You can display up to **8 charts** at a time.

## Are analytics saved when I save a search?

Yes, analytics are saved along with your search.
