# Your Data: Listings & Partials

Perchwell is now your system of record for all listing activity.

All partial listings from Paragon—known as “drafts” in Perchwell—were migrated to Perchwell on July 20, 2026, as part of a one-time data sync.

Any partials created or updated in Paragon after July 20 were not automatically transferred to Perchwell. Moving forward, all drafts should be created and maintained directly in Perchwell.
