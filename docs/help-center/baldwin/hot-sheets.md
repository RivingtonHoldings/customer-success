# Hot Sheets

## Set up a hot sheet for a city you work in

Run a search for the city you cover, save it, then add it to your dashboard as a hot sheet. Now every login opens with that market activity in front of you, and you can click straight from the bar into the listings.

[Embedded video](https://www.loom.com/embed/e720e58d353f4c83b79270fb4b5e2c23)

## Build a hot sheet from a client saved search

Take a search you already saved for a specific client and turn it into a dashboard hot sheet, so their criteria sit alongside your market view.
