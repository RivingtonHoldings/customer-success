# Run a Basic Search

When working with buyers, Perchwell provides tools to help you quickly find listings that match your client’s needs and keep them informed throughout the home search process. We’ll walk through how to run a search to find listings based on your client’s specific criteria, how to refine those results, and how to take action by sharing searches or individual listings with your clients.

# **Run a Search**

The same searches you run today, shown the Perchwell way. Your client wants a three bedroom, two bath home in a subdivision with a waterfront view, so you build the filters, save the search, and send the listings over. When a filter is not where you expect it, use the Filter Filter to look it up by name instead of hunting through groups.

[Embedded video](https://www.loom.com/embed/9113eeac2c0c41d48bd35c6154330df8)

## Find your way around search results

A quick tour so you always know where you are and how to get back. Use the View control to move between your filters and your results list. Click any column header to sort. Preview a listing inline, or open the full detail page, and use the three dot menu on a row to reach that listing's actions. The View switcher always returns you to your full results.

[Embedded video](https://www.loom.com/embed/ecd0eb629610494e8856124ca4c342af)

# Share a listing without inviting the client, on your computer

Not every client needs a Perchwell account. Select one listing or several, then send them by email or copy a public link you can text over. No invitation required.
