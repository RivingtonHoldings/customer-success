# Communicate with Your Agent in Perchwell

# Messages overview

Use **Messages** to communicate with your agent inside Perchwell.
- Ask questions about a listing
- Share feedback about what you like and do not like
- Keep conversations connected to the properties you are discussing

[Embedded video](https://www.loom.com/embed/1db1267f87c04d45a106e2c2e2606c1b)

## Messages access

### Steps:
1. Click **Messages** in the top navigation
2. In the left conversation panel, select your conversation with your agent
3. Type your message
4. Send your message

## Listing reactions

Use reactions to quickly tell your agent how you feel about a property.
- **Like**: a strong match
- **Maybe**: worth discussing
- **Reject**: not a fit

**💡 Your reactions update instantly and help your agent better understand your preferences.**
