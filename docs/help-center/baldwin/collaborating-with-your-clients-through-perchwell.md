# Collaborating with Your Clients through Perchwell

This session covers how to use Perchwell for client communication and collaboration. You’ll learn how to add and manage contacts, invite clients to the platform, share listings by message, email, and link, and organize listings using tags. We’ll also cover in-platform messaging, responding to client feedback, bulk contact imports, and creating contact groups. Includes live Q&A.
