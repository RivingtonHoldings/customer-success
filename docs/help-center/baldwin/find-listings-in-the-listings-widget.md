# Find Listings in the Listings Widget

Use this article to find the Listings widget on your Dashboard, narrow it to the listings you want, find a listing that is missing, and take action on a listing without leaving the Dashboard.

[Embedded video](https://www.loom.com/embed/4593cca5c13b40e4bfc565b25086ff55)

# Find the Listings widget on the Dashboard

To find the Listings widget, click Dashboard in the top navigation bar from anywhere in Perchwell. The Listings widget sits in the upper right corner of the Dashboard.

> **Note:** The Listings widget cannot be removed or moved to a different position on the Dashboard.

# Narrow the listings in the Listings widget

Narrow the listings in the Listings widget in three ways: choose whose listings you see, filter by status and activity, and search for an address.

> **Note:** The Listings widget starts on MLS Listings. After that it keeps whichever listings and filter you chose last, which means it opens the way you left it each time you return to the Dashboard.

## Choose whose listings you see

Choose whose listings the Listings widget shows. Click the top right corner of the widget, then pick an option under Scope by:
- Select MLS Listings to see listings across your MLS
- Select My Listings to see your own listings
- Select Brokerage Listings to see listings across your brokerage
- Select Office Listings to see listings across your office

The options available to you depend on your MLS permissions.

## Filter by status and activity

The status and activity filters sit below the Search field in the Listings widget. Select a filter to narrow the listings shown:
- Active
- Open Houses
- Coming Soon
- Active Under Contract
- Pending
- Closed
- Price Drop
- Price Increase

Active, Coming Soon, Active Under Contract, Pending, and Closed filter listings by status. See [What Each Listing Status Means](http://support.perchwell.com/baldwin/en/articles/14709241-listing-statuses-in-perchwell) for a definition of each one.

Open Houses, Price Drop, and Price Increase filter listings by recent listing activity rather than status.

Listings appear from most recent to least recent. For example, with Active selected, the most recently listed properties appear first.

If some status and activity filters are hidden, click the arrow at the end of the row to see the next set.

## Search within the Listings widget

Search for a specific listing by entering an address in the Search field at the top of the Listings widget. Matching listings appear as you type, the same way they do in the [Universal Search Bar](http://support.perchwell.com/baldwin/en/articles/11002620-universal-search-bar).

The search looks only within the listings you chose to see and the selected filter, and it always shows the closest matches.

# Find a listing that is missing from the Listings widget

If a listing you expect does not appear in the Listings widget, check the two settings that control which listings appear:
- Scope by: My Listings, Brokerage Listings, and Office Listings show only your listings, your brokerage's listings, or your office's listings. Select MLS Listings to see and search all listings in your MLS
- Status and activity filters: Search results are limited to the filter currently selected in the widget. A listing that does not match that filter will not appear, even when you search for it. Select a filter that matches the listing, such as its current status, then search again

# Take action on a listing from the Listings widget

Take action on a listing directly from the Listings widget. The icons next to each listing depend on whose listings you chose to see:
- Pencil icon: If your MLS uses Perchwell for listing entry and the widget is set to My Listings, click the pencil icon next to a listing to open it in Add/Edit in a new tab. The Dashboard stays open in the original tab
- ShowingTime icon: When the widget is set to My Listings and your MLS connects ShowingTime, click the ShowingTime icon to manage showing details for that listing, including availability, access instructions, appointment requests, and feedback
- Tag icon: When the widget is set to MLS Listings, Brokerage Listings, or Office Listings, click the tag icon next to a listing to add it to a Tag

If your MLS uses Perchwell for listing entry, [Add and Edit Listings](http://support.perchwell.com/baldwin/en/articles/13627189-add-and-edit-listings) covers editing a listing once Add/Edit opens.

# Things to Know
- The Dashboard is not available in the Perchwell mobile app, so the Listings widget opens on desktop only
- For the other widgets on the Dashboard and what each one shows, see [Dashboard Overview](http://support.perchwell.com/baldwin/en/articles/13903062-dashboard-overview)
