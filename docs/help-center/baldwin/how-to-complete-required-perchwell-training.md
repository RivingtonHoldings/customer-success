# How to Complete Required Perchwell Training

## **Complete Your Required Training**

Baldwin REALTORS® requires all members to complete mandatory training before accessing Perchwell. If you see a message asking you to complete training when you try to log in, your training has not yet been recorded as complete.

Choose one of the following options:
​

### **Complete the Prerecorded Training**

Watch the short training videos and complete the quiz through the[Perchwell MLS Training course](https://baldwinorientation.thinkific.com/courses/PerchwellMLSTraining).
​

### **Register for a Training Session**

Register for an upcoming training session through the[Baldwin REALTORS® course registration page](https://mdweb.mmsi2.com/baldwin/edu/scripts/courses.php).

After completing the required training, your access will be updated. If you have already completed training and still cannot log in, contact Perchwell Support for assistance.
