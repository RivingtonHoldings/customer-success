# Masquerade an Agent's Account

# In this article:

You will learn how to sign in to an agent’s account with masquerading so you can troubleshoot issues and confirm what the agent sees in Perchwell.

# What masquerading lets you do

Masquerading signs you in as an agent so you can see the platform from that agent’s perspective.
- Confirm what the agent sees in **Listings**, **Search**, **Reports**, and settings
- Troubleshoot issues reported by an agent
- Verify account permissions and configuration

# Open the agent’s profile from Manage People

Agent accounts are accessed from **Manage People**.

### Steps:
1. Sign in to Perchwell with your credentials or **SSO**
2. Click **Manage People** in the top navigation
3. Enter the agent’s name in the search bar
4. Click the agent’s profile to open the account

# Start masquerading (Log in as)

### Steps:
1. In the agent’s profile, click **Log in as**.
2. Confirm the prompt.

You are now signed in as the agent.

# Stop masquerading and return to your account

Exit masquerading when you finish supporting the agent.

### Steps:
1. Hover over your **profile icon** in the top right
2. Click **Stop Masquerading**

You immediately return to your administrator account.

# Tips
- Masquerading is limited to authorized MLS staff and brokerage administrators.
- Always click **Stop Masquerading** as soon as you finish troubleshooting.
