# July Features

**Market Analytics Report:** Every core metric now has its own page, with historical filters, a Condo or Other structure filter, and an AI summary for each metric.

➡️ **[Learn More](https://support.perchwell.com/baldwin/en/articles/13623210-analytics-tab-overview)**

**Listing Page Upgrades:** A cleaner listing page. The actions you use most are front and center, maps and floor plans surface automatically, and images are easier to view and save at full resolution.

➡️ **[Learn More](https://support.perchwell.com/baldwin/en/articles/14074994-listing-detail-page-overview)**

**Filter Selector Search Bar:** Type a filter value such as a subdivision or an amenity into the search bar at the top of the filters panel and apply it in one step.

➡️ **[Learn More](https://support.perchwell.com/baldwin/en/articles/13903380-create-a-search-with-filters-in-perchwell)**

**Keep:** Select the listings you want in your results and click Keep to hide the rest. Your kept and hidden listings are saved with the search.

➡️ **[Try it out](https://www.perchwell.com/results)**
