# Baldwin’s Conversion FAQs

## When will I be able to login to the Perchwell system?

You are able to log-into Perchwell now!

## How were the Parallel Groups Selected?

The BR MLS membership was divided into three groups for the parallel onboarding process. The first group consists of offices who currently have individuals who are giving their time to serve on the BR Association and BR MLS Board of Directors. Groups two and three were randomly selected through an AI process. Click [here](https://546d8e5d-30d8-4e37-a788-3c49098444fc.filesusr.com/ugd/d752df_b0231c8ee58f4713b72f57968017fff1.pdf) to see which group your office is in.

## Will training be required before I can login to the Perchwell system?

Yes. Every MLS participant and Administrator will need to complete a basic online Perchwell training session before they will be allowed to login to the new Perchwell system.

Additional details will be available as we get closer to the parallel period.

## Will all listing history be brought over to the Perchwell system?

Yes, BR has listing data starting in 2006 and all listing history will be brought over to the new Perchwell system. Please keep in mind that older listings may not contain all the data they do today. The older listings were created in the Navica system where some information might not have transferred over to Paragon, as well as some information was not required or being asked for.

Bottom line is all the listing data will be transferred to the Perchwell system.

## Will the clients I have saved in Paragon transfer over to the Perchwell system?

Yes. Any clients along with their information will be transferred over to the Perchwell system. You will not need to do anything additional for this information to transfer.

If you want complete peace of mind, Paragon allows you to export your clients and their information to store in a file, but please remember this is not necessary or required.

## Will my saved searches transfer to the Perchwell system?

All saved searches made available by Paragon will be imported into Perchwell and linked to contacts. However, the **migrated searches will require review by all Baldwin members** for two reasons:
1. **Arbitrary Filter Elimination: **As of April 20th, 2026, the saved search information made available to Perchwell by Paragon unpredictably drops certain search filters, corrupting the integrity of your saved search data.
2. **Filter Translation: **The Baldwin REALTORS® MLS team is upgrading the structure of your listing database to align with modern best practices adopted across the industry and RESO (Real Estate Standards Organization). As a result, certain filters do not cleanly translate between systems (e.g. grouping “Other” property types under “Residential - Single Family”).

Despite these two challenges, your saved searches will nonetheless appear in Perchwell upon first login, marked for manual review with the prefix of **[From Paragon: Unreviewed]** in the name of the search in Perchwell. You will be able to open, update these saved searches in Perchwell, and remove the label by changing the name as they are reviewed.

## What kind of training will be available for the Perchwell system?

We offer a mix of online, in-person, and virtual training to help you get up and running quickly—from a short certification to hands-on sessions and ongoing learning opportunities. You’ll also have continuous support available through multiple channels.

Check out the [Conversion Center](https://www.perchwell.com/mls/baldwin-conversion-center) to view the full schedule and sign up for sessions.

## How will this change affect my IDX or other data feeds?

Your IDX and other data feeds will remain operational during the transition. We will share updates and provide support to ensure a smooth shift to Perchwell's data services.

## Will my integrated tools be available with Perchwell?

Yes. Most existing integrations will remain available with Perchwell, with vendor coordination in place to support a smooth transition. If any member action is required, we will provide clear guidance throughout the transition.
