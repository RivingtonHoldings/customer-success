# Listings Widget Overview

# Listings Widget location

### Steps:
1. Click **Dashboard** in the top navigation
2. Look for the **Listings Widget** on the right side of the page

# Listings Widget filters

Use the widget filters to narrow your view based on who you want to see listings for and what type of listings you want to work with.

## Choose your listing scope

Start with the scope filter. The options you see depend on your MLS permissions.
- Select **MLS Listings** to view listings across your MLS.
- Select **Brokerage Listings** to view listings across your brokerage.
- Select **My Listings** to view your own listings.
- Select **Office listings** to view listings across your office.

## Narrow by listing type

Refine the results further with the other filters in the widget:
- **Active**
- **Recently Added**
- **Open Houses**
- **Coming soon**
- **Pending**
- **Closed**
- **Price Drop**
- **Price Increase**

# Listings Widget quick actions

Take action directly from the widget so you can manage listings without leaving your Dashboard.
- **Quick Edit** (Listing Agents and Brokerage Admins)
  - Click the **pen icon** next to a listing to open the listing management form.

**💡 The Quick Edit option is available only for listing agents who own the listing. MLS staff and administrators may have broader access.**
- **ShowingTime**
  - Schedule a showing for a listing.
  - Manage upcoming showings when supported by your MLS.
- **Tag**
  - Add a listing to a tag so you can find it again quickly.
  - Track a group of listings over time.
