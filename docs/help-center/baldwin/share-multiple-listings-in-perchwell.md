# Share Multiple Listings in Perchwell

[![image](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/trq7czv5/2465459023/83bb70705dbe9dbf4b24d86019a3/Group+56.png)](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/trq7czv5/2465459023/83bb70705dbe9dbf4b24d86019a3/Group+56.png?expires=1788542100&signature=850548278cfc14a66a5da92337f5607b8c64b88b0d2ab8152f72c1e84e078399&req=diQhE817lIFdWvMW1HO4zUFaXfvd7nDuNFKCIck1vqmHI6zbHhCd6N1Zv9bp%0ABLjhklrxtrzkCucJ1MQ%3D%0A)

## Select multiple listings to share (start in **Search**)

Select the listings first, then choose your share method.
​

### Steps:
1. Open the **Search** page.
2. View search results by clicking Search.
3. Select the checkbox next to each listing you want to share.
4. Click **Actions.**
5. In the drop down, click **Share**.
6. In the pop-up window, choose one of these tabs:
  - **Email**
  - **Text**
  - **Public Link**

## Share multiple listings via **Email**

Use **Email** when you want to email listings to saved contacts directly from Perchwell.

[![image](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/trq7czv5/2465460842/2dc93f571f8999d15fd851136fed/Group+57.png)](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/trq7czv5/2465460842/2dc93f571f8999d15fd851136fed/Group+57.png?expires=1788542100&signature=b6010d38acb8490ee3a65963f970bf22af49a374f9a6a4b7d2dbabc62224d429&req=diQhE814nYlbW%2FMW1HO4zWTwXE7WfFA1eYhPcxu5oPS1kK62WtwyqudvcDge%0Af5UkNAyvlhMetkd5hDY%3D%0A)

In the **Email** tab, you can:
- Choose one or more recipients in **Select emails**.
- Add a message in the message field.
- Edit the email **Subject**.
- Click **Email** to send.

⚠️ **Good to know:** Recipients receive an email with a link to a carousel of client-facing listing detail pages for each listing. The listing agent's contact information is not included.

**📖 **Learn how to save when sharing listings with [email templates](http://support.perchwell.com/baldwin/en/articles/14549167-create-communication-templates).

## Select multiple listings via text

Use **Text** when you want to text listings to a saved directly from Perchwell.

[![image](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/trq7czv5/2585480885/8d01d08a49b7975281562027f8ec/Screenshot+2026-08-04+at+10_35_14%E2%80%AFAM.png)](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/trq7czv5/2585480885/8d01d08a49b7975281562027f8ec/Screenshot+2026-08-04+at+10_35_14%E2%80%AFAM.png?expires=1788542100&signature=eb23161ae2fe10df5394fe96e2724d8aaa7126033e11b6a5d34335e5dfc4f89f&req=diUvE812nYlXXPMW1HO4zX8T%2B%2BqXjQDrpMN3uswP2iY2DV7Uze89xGaGScYw%0ApEY%2FAenvd1vRyD7gALA%3D%0A)

​

In the **Text** tab, you can:
- Choose a contact that has a phone number added to a contact.
- Create a new Contact
- Send a text

⚠️ **Important information:**
- Recipients will need to opt into the text messages
- The text will come from 332-240-3300 and the contact will not be able to reply back to the text
- Due to federal regulation, the text can only be sent out between 8am and 8pm. If you send it outside of those times, the text will be sent at 8am the next morning.

## Share multiple listings with a **Public Link**

Use **Public Link** when you are sending listings through text message or another external messaging app.

[![image](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/trq7czv5/2465460035/15354ffdb5a5fc3c53ed7ec70be6/Group+55.png)](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/trq7czv5/2465460035/15354ffdb5a5fc3c53ed7ec70be6/Group+55.png?expires=1788542100&signature=b3fdf7d6f606e1f51cc617d859973b967050c62e883d70c8a09fcb4dae6464d9&req=diQhE814nYFcXPMW1HO4zQIz3AWjJsBg1OUrYyrHpiEDepYQSx3Hacu4vDKq%0A6XBo9pjEQ2cotBL3ASw%3D%0A)

In the **Public Link** tab, you can:
- Click **Copy** to copy the generated link.
- Paste the link anywhere you want to share it.

**⚠️ Good to know: **Public links do not require clients to log in.

## What clients see when they open a shared set of listings

Shared listings open in a carousel view that makes it easy to browse.
- Scroll through listings in the carousel.
- Open each listing to review details.
- Client's will not see any listing agent information, private documents, or historical listing details.

## Tips
- Select all your listings first, then click **Share** once.
- Use **Email** for saved contacts and **Public Link** for external messaging.
- Update your message and **Subject** to match your client’s search criteria.
- Recipients receive the client-facing listing detail page when sharing listings via email or with a public link.
