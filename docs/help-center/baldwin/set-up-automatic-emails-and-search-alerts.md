# Set-up Automatic Emails and Search Alerts

## **Set up auto emails and search alerts for a client**

Save the search, share it with your client, and let the alerts do the follow up. New listings that match the criteria go out automatically, so your client hears about them the day they hit the market and you stay in the loop too.

[Embedded video](https://www.loom.com/embed/d8c0830f77d3459c8032ff4ca8733a64)

## **Set up auto emails and search alerts for price drops & increases**

Save the search and set alerts for price changes. Your client will automatically receive updates when a matching listing has a price drop or increase, keeping them informed while you stay in the loop. **A more streamlined approach is coming soon!**

[Embedded video](https://www.loom.com/embed/83d3052f198b411aa91e6ec974d83815)
