# Customize Your Dashboard

[Embedded video](https://demo.arcade.software/pgyIXO3DO8xMCsDYNahj?embed)

# Add and Remove Dashboard Widgets

Click **Manage Widgets** at the top right of the **Dashboard** to control which widgets appear. The following widgets are available:
- **Hot Sheets**
- **Contacts**
- **Saved Searches**
- **Tags**

Check or uncheck each widget to add or remove it from your **Dashboard**.

> 💡 Add, remove, and reorder widgets at any time. The **Listings** widget cannot be removed or reordered.

# Reorder and Filter Dashboard Widgets

Drag the **drag handle** (⠿) to move widgets into your preferred order. Click the **filter icon** (≔) on any widget to refine the information it displays. Filter options vary by widget type.

> 📖 Learn how the **Listings** widget works [in this article](https://intercom.help/perchwell-help-center-baldwin/en/articles/13903171-what-is-the-listings-widget-on-the-dashboard).

# Things to Know
- Dashboard and Hot Sheets are not available on the Perchwell mobile app. You can only view and access these areas on desktop.
