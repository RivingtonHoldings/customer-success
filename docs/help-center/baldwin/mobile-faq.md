# Mobile FAQ

## Will the notes I add on the listing details page in the mobile app carry over to the desktop?

Not at this time, but that feedback has been shared with the product team.

## Do you have an iPad app?

No, not at this time. If you previously downloaded the iPad app, please uninstall it and use your default browser on your iPad instead. It works just like the desktop version.

## As an admin, can I assume an agent's identity through the mobile app?

Not at this time, but that feedback has been shared with the product team.
