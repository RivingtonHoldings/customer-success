# User Settings FAQs

## How do I change my name, email address, or phone number in Perchwell?

For all name, email address, phone number changes, please update these via the Baldwin REALTOR® member portal. If you still require assistance, please contact MLS support at 251-270-0328 or email [mls@baldwinrealtors.com](mailto:mls@baldwinrealtors.com) to help you update your information.

## Why Do I Only See the Dashboard When I Log into Perchwell?

As a Personal Assistant or Office Assistant (PA/OA), your broker or agent must grant you permission to log in as them before you can assume their identity. Until this access is granted, your platform access will be limited.

For step-by-step instructions and additional information, please [visit this Help Center article.](https://support.perchwell.com/baldwin/en/articles/16053168-assume-identity)
​

## How Do I Change my Email in My Profile?

Emails are managed by MLS staff in their roster management software (MMSI/Relevate). To update your email so that it displays correctly on Listings, please reach out to your MLS Staff. You can reach out to them at: 251-270-0328 or email [mls@baldwinrealtors.com](mailto:mls@baldwinrealtors.com).
