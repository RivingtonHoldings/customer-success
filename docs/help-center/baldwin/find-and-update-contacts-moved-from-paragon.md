# Find and Update Contacts Moved from Paragon

Use this article to find the contacts that moved from Paragon, add any missing email addresses, and invite those contacts to Perchwell.

# Find your contacts moved from Paragon on the Contacts page

Open the Contacts page to find your contacts moved from Paragon on the My Contacts tab. Each profile includes every field the contact had in Paragon. Members may know their contacts from the Collaboration Center in Paragon.

Contacts moved in a one-time transfer the week before your Perchwell access date. Changes made in Paragon after that date are not in Perchwell.

# Add an email address to a contact moved from Paragon

Add an email address to any contact that moved from Paragon without one. On the My Contacts tab, those contacts have an empty Email column. Perchwell requires an email address for every contact. Without one, a contact is left out when you:
- Share a listing by email
- Share a saved search
- Share a report
- Send a message

## Steps:
1. Click the contact's name on the My Contacts tab
2. Click Edit profile
3. Enter the contact's email address
4. Click Save profile

The email address shows in the Email column, and the contact is included the next time you share from Perchwell or send a message.

# Invite contacts moved from Paragon to Perchwell

Contacts moved from Paragon arrived uninvited, which means they do not have access to Perchwell until you invite them. Check each contact's email address before you send an invitation, since it is locked once the contact is invited.
