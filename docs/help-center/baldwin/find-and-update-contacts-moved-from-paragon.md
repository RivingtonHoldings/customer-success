# Find and Update Contacts Moved from Paragon

Use this article to find the contacts moved from Paragon and make sure each one has an email address before you invite them to Perchwell.

# Find your contacts moved from Paragon on the Contacts page

Open the Contacts page to find your contacts moved from Paragon on the My Contacts tab. Each profile includes every field the contact had in Paragon. In Paragon, your contacts were in the Collaboration Center.

Contacts moved in a one-time transfer the week before your Perchwell access date. Changes made in Paragon after that date are not in Perchwell.

# Add an email address to a contact moved from Paragon

Add an email address to any contact that moved from Paragon without one. On the My Contacts tab, those contacts have an empty Email column. Perchwell requires an email address to create a contact, but some contacts moved from Paragon without one. Without one, a contact is left out of the contact list when you:
- Share a listing by email
- Share a saved search
- Share a report
- Send a message

## Steps:
1. Click the contact's name on the My Contacts tab
2. Click Edit profile
3. Enter the contact's email address
4. Click Save profile

The email address shows in the Email column, and the contact appears in the contact list the next time you share from Perchwell or send a message.

# Invite contacts moved from Paragon to Perchwell

Contacts moved from Paragon arrived uninvited, which means they do not have access to Perchwell until you invite them. Check each contact's email address before you send an invitation, since it is locked once the contact is invited. To change a locked email address, contact Perchwell Support.

- [Create and Manage Your Contacts in Perchwell](https://support.perchwell.com/baldwin/en/articles/14790755-create-and-manage-your-contacts-in-perchwell) covers editing a contact and which details lock after an invitation.
