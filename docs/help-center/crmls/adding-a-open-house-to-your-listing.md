# Adding a Open House to your Listing

## Schedule an Open House

Open Houses are created from the **Manage Listings** page. You can add one to a single listing or apply the same Open House details to multiple listings at once.

## **Option 1: Add to a Single Listing**
1. Go to **Manage Listings**
2. Locate the listing you want to schedule an Open House for *(Use the Filters menu if needed to narrow your list.)*
3. Click the **calendar icon** to the right of the listing

An **Open Houses** form will open.

## **Option 2: Add to Multiple Listings**
1. Go to **Manage Listings**
2. Check the **selection boxes** on the listings you want to update *(Use the Filters menu if needed to narrow your list.)*
3. A footer menu will appear at the bottom
4. Click **Add Open House**

The **Open House** form will open and apply to all selected listings.

> 💡 When adding an Open House to multiple listings, all listings must share the same details, including type, date, start time, and end time.

## Enter Open House Details

After opening the Open Houses form, enter the scheduling information.
1. **Choose Open House Type**
  1. Public
  2. Broker Only
  3. By Appointment
2. **Select Date** — Pick the open house date from the calendar
3. **Set Start Time** — Enter the start time
4. **Set End Time** — Enter the end time

## Finalize the Open House
1. Click **Add** to save the open house
2. To review scheduled open houses, click the **calendar icon** again on the listing → The panel will display all scheduled and past open houses
3. To **delete** an open house, reopen the calendar panel and click the **trash can icon** next to the event

## Things to Know
- Open Houses are managed from the Manage Listings page
- The calendar panel shows both upcoming and past events
- Multiple listings can share one Open House entry when added together
- Editing or deleting is done from the same calendar panel
