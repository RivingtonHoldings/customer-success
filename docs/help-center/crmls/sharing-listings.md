# Sharing Listings

## Sharing Listings in Perchwell

You can easily share listings with clients from the **Search Page**, a **Listing Page**, or your **Tags** tab. Here’s how:

## ✅ From the Search Page

Spotted the right listings? Share them with your client in just a few clicks directly from Search.

![image](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/trq7czv5/1521110176/fd1f2f3ebf989d28932bdacd89ae/Screenshot+2025-05-12+at+9_28_37%E2%80%AFPM.png)
1. **Select the listings** you want to share.
2. Click **“Share”** in the action bar at the bottom.
3. **Choose a client** from your contact list.
4. **Customize your message**, then send.

💡 *Invited clients will receive the message in their Perchwell Messages tab. All clients will also get an email with your message and the listings.*

## 🏠 From a Listing Page

See a home your client might love? Open a chat and share it right from the Listing page.

![image](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/trq7czv5/1521110321/0a089589acea5dd6f44be7fbb31b/Screenshot+2025-05-12+at+9_29_06%E2%80%AFPM.png)
1. In the **action window at the top right**, click **“Share listing or copy link”**.
2. If sharing via email, **select your client** and **add a quick message**.
3. Send the listing directly or copy the link to share manually.

💡 *Ideal for when you want to share one listing with quick context.*

## 🏷️ From the Tags Page

Have a few properties saved for later? Share them straight from the Tags page.

![image](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/trq7czv5/1521110443/a560fb4ecefb995b8bd94eacf1a4/Screenshot+2025-05-12+at+9_29_42%E2%80%AFPM.png)
1. Open a **tag group** from the Tags tab.
2. Click **“Share”** in the top-right corner.
3. **Select a client** and **add a custom message**.

💡 *Great for sharing a curated group of listings in one go.*

> **Pro Tip: **Showcase your branding by sharing the customized tag dashboard. Learn More about [tags](https://intercom.help/perchwell/en/articles/8955809-10-ways-to-use-tags) here.

## 📬 What Your Client Will See
- A branded view of the listings you shared
- Your personalized message
- Instant access via email and, for invited clients, in Perchwell Messaging
