# Engaging with Multiple Agents

## Viewing Saved Searches

All saved searches shared by your agent’s will appear in your Saved Search drop down menu. You can check out the ***Owned By*** section of the saved search to see which agent has sent you the saved search. This makes it easy to identify who has sent you which search.

## Handling Agents in Multiple Regions

Perchwell is available across the nation and if you’re working with multiple agents in different locations, your portal will automatically adjust to accommodate multiple locations.
- **Desktop**
  - Login to your Perchwell account
  - Click on the region in the top right corner
- **Mobile**
  - Log into your Perchwell mobile app
  - Click on Filters
  - Select your Region

> 🚨 *Please note, you wont be able to access specific saved searches or listings if you’re not in the designated region.*

## Communicating with Multiple Agents

It’s easy to communicate with multiple agents in Perchwell. All messages from your agents are grouped under their contact. So easily identify who sent who by just viewing your messages.
- Navigate to Messages
- Click on the Agent who you want to chat with or view listings from.

## Best Practices
- **Keep your Saved Searches Organized**: Discard of saved searches you’re no longer using. Also ensure your agents are removing you from saved searches that are no longer active to remove unwanted alerts/notifications.
