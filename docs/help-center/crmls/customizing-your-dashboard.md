# Customizing your Dashboard

#  **Navigating to your Dashboard**

The Dashboard displays your widgets and integrations in one centralized workspace.
1. Click **Dashboard** in the top navigation menu

## **Adding Widgets to the Dashboard**

To see quick snapshots of information that matters most, you can manage the widgets on your Dashboard.
1. Click **Manage Widgets** at the top right of the page.
  1. Available widgets include:
    1. Hot Sheets
    2. Listings
    3. Contacts
    4. Saved Searches
    5. Tags
    6. Presentations
    7. Days on Market
2. Check or uncheck widgets to control what appears on your Dashboard.

>  💡 The **Listing Widget** cannot be removed.

## **Organizing the Widgets on your Dashboard**

[![image](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/trq7czv5/2124879960/e7926431a8a8ca1e1c50dd3bcd1e/Untitled%2Bdesign%2B-282-29.gif)](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/trq7czv5/2124879960/e7926431a8a8ca1e1c50dd3bcd1e/Untitled%2Bdesign%2B-282-29.gif?expires=1788542100&signature=b0eb598c7146649b0a57e36319c0cf04c7927a6f7665e207413f8ec9ed85bbde&req=diElEsF5lIhZWfMW1HO4zQIRZnUd1Kfswy5GNhgvepqYksei9Tf%2BzQ%2Fvo9wG%0AQXLZMhTLQt3pFyZSZ24%3D%0A)

Reorganize widgets in an order that best fits your needs.
1. Use the drag handle **(𓃎)** to reorder widgets.
2. Apply filters (**≔**) within individual widgets to refine the information displayed. Filter options may vary by the type of widget.

> 💡 The Listing widget cannot be removed.

> 📖 Check out this article to learn more about the [listing widget](https://intercom.help/perchwell/en/articles/13917900-filtering-listing-by-office-mls-or-your-listings-on-the-dashboard).
