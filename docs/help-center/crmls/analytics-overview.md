# Analytics Overview

Analytics in Perchwell gives you a clear, real time view of the market so you can make informed decisions with confidence. With customizable charts, historical trends, and up to date data, **Analytics** helps you understand market activity and strengthen your business strategy.

## **What You Can Do With Analytics**

**Analytics provides tools to help you interpret the market and track performance.**

**Real time market data**

View current and historical trends based on your search filters.

**Customizable charts**
Use premade charts such as Days on Market, Sales Price, and Listing Volume. Charts update as you adjust filters.

## **How to Use Analytics**

### **1. Set Filters for Tailored Insights**

Analytics reflects the filters you select on the Search page.
- Set your criteria on Search
- The charts in Analytics update based on those filters
- Saved searches and charts always stay current as the market shifts

### **2. Create and Customize Charts**
- Apply filters on the Search page
- Navigate to the **Analytics** tab

Select or view the charts generated from your criteria
Customizations help you focus on the trends that matter most to your business.

## **Where Analytics Gets Its Data**

Analytics pulls data directly from the market based on the filters you set. This ensures your charts stay relevant and reflect live market conditions.

## **Types of Charts and when to Use Them**
- **Listings Charts**
Ideal for agents reviewing their listing activity and market performance. **Brokerage Charts**
Available for admin users. Helps brokerages track overall performance and spot market opportunities.

## **Sharing Analytics Insights**

From the **Analytics** tab, click **Share** in the top right corner to:
- Email charts to clients
- Download a PDF for presentations or reports

This helps you communicate data clearly and professionally.

## **Best Practices**
- Review and update filters often to match current market conditions.
- Explore different chart types to get a full view of market activity.
- Use saved searches with Analytics to track trends automatically.
