# Frequently Asked Questions

# **Search and Property Tools**
1. **How to I search for properties efficiently?**
  1. Our advanced search tools let you filter properties by specific criteria like price range, square footage, and amenities. Use the map view to explore properties in particular neighborhoods or draw custom search areas.
2. **Can I save my favorite searches?**
  1. Yes! Save your most-used search criteria as templates to quickly access them later. This helps streamline your workflow when working with multiple clients.
3. **Where does permit data come from?**
  1. CRMLS permit data is sourced directly from local building departments and county records. This information is regularly updated to ensure accuracy and includes details about building permits, modifications, and property improvements.
4. **How far back does CRMLS property data go?**
  1. CRMLS property data includes historical records going back approximately 20 years for most properties. This includes past sales history, price changes, and listing details. However, the availability and completeness of historical data may vary by property, region, and what information is provided to us from the MLS database.

# Manage Listings
1. **How do I add a New Listing?**
  1. New listings can be added through proplist. Please reach out to the CRMLS team for more information.

# Client Collaboration
1. **How do I share listings with my clients?**
  1. Share curated property collections with your clients through our secure platform. They'll receive professionally branded presentations that maintain your brokerage's identity while showcasing properties effectively.
2. **Can I track my clients' activity?**
  1. Monitor which properties your clients are viewing and their favorited listings through our intuitive dashboard. This helps you better understand their preferences and provide more targeted recommendations.
3. **Can clients see Closed or Off-Market listings in their Client Portals?**
  1. Yes, clients can view Sold/Rented properties. At this time, they're unable to set specific timeframes to view this data.

# Market Analysis
1. **How can I create market reports?**
  1. Generate comprehensive market analysis reports with just a few clicks. Our platform automatically compiles relevant data, recent sales, and market trends into professional, branded reports perfect for client presentations.
1. **Where can I find comparative market analysis (CMA) tools?**
  1. Access our CMA tools through the Analysis tab. Compare properties, analyze price trends, and create detailed property valuations using our extensive database of market data.

# Technical Support
1. **What if I need help using the platform?**
  1. Our support team is available Monday through Friday, 9 AM to 6 PM PT. Access live chat support through the help icon or email [support@perchwell.com](mailto:support@perchwell.com) for assistance.
2. **How do I reset my password?**
  1. Click the "Forgot Password" link on the login screen. You'll receive an email with instructions to securely reset your password.
3. **Is Perchwell compatible with iPads or tablets?**
  1. No, Perchwell does not have a iPad designated app. We recommend accessing the platforming using the Chrome browser on these devices.
4. **Is the CRMLS disclaimer required on all communications?**
  1. Yes, the CRMLS disclaimer must be included on all emails, reports, and exported materials containing CRMLS data. This is a mandatory requirement to ensure compliance with CRMLS data usage policies.

# Mobile Access
1. **Can I access Perchwell on my mobile device?**
  1. Yes! Our mobile app provides full platform functionality, allowing you to access property information, share listings, and communicate with clients while on the go.
1. **How do I sync my mobile and desktop accounts?**
  1. Your account automatically syncs across all devices when you sign in with your credentials. Any changes made on one device will be reflected across all your devices.
