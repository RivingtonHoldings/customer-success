# Share Listings

Listings can be shared from **Search**, a **listing details page**, or **Tags**.

### Option 1: Sharing Multiple Listings from Search (Bulk Sharing)

Use the multi-select tool to share one or multiple listings at once.
1. On the **Search** page, check the **selection boxes** on the listings you want to share
2. A footer menu will appear at the bottom
3. Click **Share**

The Share modal will open and default to **Email**.

### Email Sharing
1. Select the client you want to email
2. Choose a template from the dropdown

You can choose from three templates, each with a different preset subject line and all templates can be edited before sending.
1. Update the subject or message if needed
2. Click **Email**

### Public Link + Social Sharing
1. At the top of the Share modal, click **Public Link**
2. Choose to post to **Facebook** or **Twitter**
3. Select **Copy Public Link** to share the URL anywhere
4. Click **Close** to exit the modal

## Option 2: Sharing While Viewing a Single Listing

You can share a listing directly while viewing its details.
1. Locate the listing using **Universal Search** or by clicking it from your **Search** results
2. View the listing
3. Open the actions panel on the right (hover to reveal or click the **three-dot menu** at the top right)
4. Click **Share listing**
5. Choose how you want to share:
  1. Send in Messages
  2. Send via Email
  3. Share as social media post to Facebook or Twitter
  4. Copy public link

## Option 3: Share from a Tag Folder

You can share a curated group of listings directly from a tag folder through Perchwell Messaging. Invited clients can reply inside Messages, while uninvited clients receive the listings by email.
1. Go to the **Tags** page
2. Click the bar that says **“Click to view and filter tags”**
3. Open the tag containing the listings you want to share
4. Click **Share** in the footer
5. Select the contact you want to send the listings to and click **Share**
6. Enter your message
7. Click **Send**

You’ll be redirected to your **Messages** tab after sending.

> 💡 When sharing from a tag folder, all listings in that tag are included by default. You can use the selection boxes to choose specific listings before sending.

## Share via Messaging (Bulk Action from Search)

Use the multi-select tool in **Search** to send listings directly through Perchwell Messaging.
1. On the **Search** page, check the **selection boxes** on the listings you want to share
2. A footer menu will appear at the bottom
3. Click **Share via Messaging**
4. Select the contact you want to send the listings to and click **Share**
5. Enter your message
6. Click **Send**

## Things to Know
- You can share listings by email, public link, social post, or Messaging
- Clients receive listings instantly
- Invited clients can reply inside Perchwell Messages
- Uninvited clients receive email-only access
- Shared listings stay updated if listing details change
