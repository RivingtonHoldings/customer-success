# Export to Excel

## **Introduction**

You can export all the information related to a listing onto an Excel spreadsheet.

## **Select a Report**
1. **From the** Search page, you can select the listings you want in the report.
2. A pop-up window will appear at the bottom of the webpage.
3. **Click** on ‘Create a Report’
4. **Select** ‘Export to Excel’

## **Customize Report**

[![image](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/964641585/ebcd55412fd443927f4bbbdd/image+%2887%29.png)](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/964641585/ebcd55412fd443927f4bbbdd/image+%2887%29.png?expires=1788542100&signature=f7040a8d4fc42dc7f1ffb8ac5769a8df23d456a7c2eda3693f58d0d4258b108e&req=fSYjEM1%2FmIlaFb4f3HP0gClgvByYsaGbBvVtWZYB19rtAMJwJPDTzCSqfGVa%0AvIw4XAHjk2isqZJoMw%3D%3D%0A)
1. **Drag and Drop** to Rearrange the listings in your desired order. Select NEXT
2. **Select where links should open.**
  - Open Links in Perchwell - the client must log into Perchwell
  - Open Links Externally with your Branding - Links will open outside of Perchwell with your Brokerage’s Branding.
3. **Select the information** you want to be included in your Listing Report
  - There are Optional Fields. You have the option to SELECT ALL or DESELECT ALL.

## **Generate Report**
1. You can share the report via email, print, or download.
2. **For Emails**
  - **Email addresses** - Add one or more emails to the clients whom you want to receive the report
  - **Email Subject** - Subject Line for the email ( Both you and your client will receive a copy of the report)
  - **Message** - We already populate a message for the email. However, this can be modified. (You can also create Base Template messages that can be shared amongst your entire Brokerage)
