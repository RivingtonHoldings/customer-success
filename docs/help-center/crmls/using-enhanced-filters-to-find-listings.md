# **Filtering Your Search in Perchwell**

## **Filter Columns**

Fine-tune your search parameters in real-time using the filter column. Each filter has intuitive controls - toggle switches, input fields, or range selectors to help you find exactly what you're looking for.

> 💡 **Pro Tip**: Save time on future searches by arranging your filter columns in your preferred order and saving them as a template.
- **Sort**: Drag and drop filters to customize your view
- **Delete Filters**: Remove filters with a single click

## **Filters Button**

Access frequently used filters like Locations, Bedrooms, and Bathrooms to begin your search. or Searchwell to kick off your search with one click.

Searchwell is our AI search that allows you to kick off your search by entering you or your clients criteria with human language.
- Enter a quick search like, ‘ *2+ bedrooms, 2+ bathrooms, Residential homes in Beverly Hills*’ and watch the filters apply in real-time. You can then add additional filters to narrow your search further.

# **Additional Filters**

Find those unique properties that perfectly match your client's wishlist, from outdoor spaces to park views and everything in between.

## **Option 1: From the Filters Menu**
1. Click the **Filters** button at the top of the page
2. Select ‘**Add more filters**’ at the bottom of the panel
3. Check the filters you want to add

## **Option 2: Using the + Icon**

Click the **+** icon at the far right of the filters row on the **Search** page to quickly add new filters

# **Understanding Basic Filters**

## **Location**

Choose from multiple options to pinpoint listings that match your client's criteria:
- **Draw**: Create custom shapes on the map to define precise search areas
- **Commute**: Find properties within your client's preferred travel time and mode of transportation
- **Street**: Target specific streets and avenues for a hyper-local search

## **Bedrooms & Bathrooms**
- Specify exact matches or minimum requirements to narrow your search

## **Price & Price per Sq Ft**
- Set your price parameters to match your client's budget

## **Status & Activity**
- **Current Status**: View the latest MLS status
- **Listing Activity**: Track property history and status changes over time

> 💡 **Pro** **Tip:** Looking for properties that recently came back on the market? Set **Current Status** to 'Active' and **Listing Activity** to 'Went into contract' in the last 90 days.
