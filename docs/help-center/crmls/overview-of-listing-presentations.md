# Overview of Listing Presentations

##

[Embedded video](https://www.loom.com/embed/f7d4424b8dd9485bbd22295754cda7cf)

# **How to Access Listing Presentations**
1. **Navigate to Listing Presentations**: From the top of your Perchwell web page, click on the **Listing Presentation** button to view your dashboard.
2. **Overview of Your Page**: Here, you’ll see a complete list of your existing presentations and any shared listings as a co-collaborator. Toggle between grid and list views for easy navigation.
3. **Add New Presentation**: Click on **Add New Presentation** to start a new presentation.

# **Creating a New Presentation**

## Adding Listings
1. **Import Listings from Tags**: To add listings, select **Add Listings from Tags** to view and select listings organized by tags. Simply check the boxes for the listings you’d like to include.
2. **Custom Listings**: For properties not yet available in the MLS, you can create a custom listing by entering an address. This listing will only be available for presentation use, and you can add additional information to ensure it appears polished.

## Organizing Listings
1. **Arrange Order**: Rearrange listings by dragging them into your preferred order. Listings are categorized by status but can be manually adjusted.
2. **Set Subject Property**: Choose a primary property by clicking the star icon, and marking it as the subject property.

# **Presentation Customization**

## Adding Content and Slides
1. **Cover Page**: Customize the cover with an image, title, and agent details.
2. **Introduction & Conclusion Slides**: Add preloaded slides such as client letters, agent bios, or other pages uploaded by brokerage admins.
3. **Comparables**: Add dynamic Comparative Market Analysis (CMA) views with pre-selected fields. Choose between status comparisons and side-by-side views.
4. **Insights and Analytics**: Add charts and insights comparing listings in the presentation or from a saved search. Add captions for clarity.

## Configuring Access Settings
1. **Access Settings**: Set security preferences, including password protection and email requirements for viewing access.
2. **Sharing Options**: Share presentations via email, download, or link sharing.

## **Collaboration and Client Engagement**
1. **Client Access**: Add clients using their email to allow them secure access to the presentation.
2. **Co-Collaborators**: Invite additional agents from your brokerage to co-manage and contribute to the presentation.
