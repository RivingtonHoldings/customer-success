# Reporting Listings

## **How to Report a Listing**
1. Navigate to any **Property Detail Page** in Perchwell.
2. Locate a listing that contains incorrect or misleading information.
3. Click the **"Report a Listing"** button in the right-hand sidebar.
4. A new tab will open, directing you to the CRMLS reporting portal to complete your submission.

## **Tip:**

Use this feature in real time as you're reviewing properties. For example, when preparing client searches or reviewing market comps, flag inaccurate listings to avoid sharing incorrect data.

## **Things to Know**
1. You must be logged into **REcore** to submit a report.
2. Submitting a report helps protect MLS data integrity, so if you locate any incorrect information report it.
3. Reporting a listing does not immediately correct the issue; CRMLS will review and take action accordingly.
