# Using MLS Areas in the Location Filter

## Summary

Whether you're focusing on a specific neighborhood or exploring multiple areas at once, understanding how location filters work helps you find the most relevant listings and avoid missing out.

[Embedded video](https://www.loom.com/embed/a604b2fb127f46b8aeafc03d6530a282)

## **How to Use It:**

### 📍 **Searching multiple areas at the same level (broader results)**

When you select more than one County, City, or MLS Area, Perchwell shows listings in **any** of the selected locations.
- **Example:** Searching for *Los Angeles* and *San Diego* will return listings in Los Angeles **and** San Diego.

### 📍 **Combining broader and more specific areas (narrower results)**

If you choose a larger area (like a County) and **add** multiple smaller area within it (like Cities or MLS Areas), Perchwell will only show listings that match **both** cities selected.
- **Example:** Searching for the County Los Angeles and cities like Pasadena and Glendale, Perchwell will show you listings in Pasadena **or **Glendale.

## **Things to Know**
- You can search by **County**, **City**, or **MLS Area. **Choose what makes sense for how your client is searching.
- Choosing multiple Cities will **expand** your results.
- If you’ve created a template search with a specific County selection, no need to remove the County to narrow your selections.
