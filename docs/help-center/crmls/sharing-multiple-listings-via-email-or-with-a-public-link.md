# Sharing Multiple Listings via Email or with a Public Link

[![image](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/trq7czv5/2025168903/28e618ab5abdd3d3efa181c5bb1d/Group+4.png)](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/trq7czv5/2025168903/28e618ab5abdd3d3efa181c5bb1d/Group+4.png?expires=1788542100&signature=5ba3417a1e4dd2611c8be4f60bbd55b86f2f7d847a0c9f6c8cace394f0c792a0&req=diAlE8h4lYhfWvMW1HO4zZ8cilQR1%2BXECj2YbTo1ySKD7mDM2nUYGmDtRJmF%0AgJS979hiinaSvCH6PIA%3D%0A)

**💡 Check out this [video](https://www.loom.com/share/b39567cba22e48ae8944d27de72930c5) to watch how to share listings in real time.**

## **How to Share Multiple Listings**

You can share multiple listings directly from the **Search** page in just a few clicks.

## Steps
1. From **Search**, select the checkbox next to each listing you want to share
2. Use the action bar that appears at the bottom of the screen and click **Share**
3. In the pop-up window, choose how you want to share the listings:
  - Email
  - Public Link

## **Share Multiple Listings via Email**

Use email to send listings directly to clients saved in your contacts.

## Steps
1. In the **Select emails** dropdown, choose one or more contacts
2. Add a message and update the subject line if needed
3. Click **Email** to send

**💡 Save time by using email templates. Templates help you reuse messaging and send listings faster for common client scenarios. Check out our article on [Creating Email Templates](https://intercom.help/perchwell/en/articles/11379741-creating-email-templates)to learn more.**

## **Share Multiple Listings Using a Public Link**

Use a public link when sharing listings outside of email, such as by text message or external messaging apps.

## Steps
1. In the pop-up window, select the **Public Link** tab
2. Copy the generated link
3. Paste the link into an email, text, or message to share with clients

## **Things to Know**
- Listings display in a carousel view that makes browsing easy for clients
- You can share multiple listings with multiple clients at the same time
- Public links do not require clients to log in to view listings
