# Search for Open Houses

# Find Open Houses using the **Filters** menu

Add the **Open House** filter from the **Filters** menu to narrow results to a specific date range.

### Steps:
1. Navigate to the **Search** page
2. Click **Filters**
3. In the filter search bar, type **Open House**
4. In the **Open House** filter, select your date range
5. Click **Submit**, to save

**💡 Use the date range to focus on Open Houses happening today, this weekend, or within a specific tour window.**

# Filter by Open House directly on search results (add the **Open House** column)

Add the **Open House** column to set a date range from the results grid.
1. From the **Search** view, click the the column drop down
2. In the column search bar, type **Open House**
3. Click the checkbox next to the filter
4. Rearrange the order of the Open House Filter here or locate it at the end of your results columns
5. Click on the filter, and adjust your date range

## Tips

Use these best practices to get more accurate results and share what you find.
- Add **Open House** from either **Filters** or the **Search** view. Both options control the same Open House criteria.
- Combine **Open House** with other filters such as price, location, beds, and baths.
- Promote your own Open Houses by tagging those listings, then share the **shareable tag link** with social channels or client groups.
