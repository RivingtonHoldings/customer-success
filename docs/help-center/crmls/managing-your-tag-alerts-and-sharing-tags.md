# Managing your Tag Alerts and Sharing Tags.

## Setting Up and Manage Tag Alerts

[Embedded video](https://www.loom.com/embed/62226e68fe7e4288912be2dc88ccda81)

### 1. Open the Tags Page
- From anywhere in Perchwell, click the Tags tab in the navigation bar.

### 2. Find the Tag You Want to Manage
- Use the search bar to locate a tag quickly.

### 3. Manage Alert Settings
- Click the pencil icon next to the tag.
- Choose the updates you want to receive, such as **new listings**, **price changes**, or **status changes**.
- You can also turn alerts off completely.

### 4. Save Your Preferences
- Click **Save** to confirm your updates.

### 5. Quickly Toggle Alerts
- Click the bell icon next to a tag to turn alerts on or off without opening the full settings.

### 6. Share Tags with Clients
- Select the shared tag icon to generate a link.
- Copy the link to send via text or email.

### 7. What Clients Will See
- Clients who open your shared link will see a custom site with your brokerage branding and contact information.
- They can click any listing tile to view full property details in the client portal.

## Tips and Best Practices
- Review your tag alerts regularly to stay focused on your current clients and priorities.
- Use quick toggle to manage notifications without leaving the Tags page.
- Create tags that align with client needs, geographic areas, or price ranges to streamline updates.
