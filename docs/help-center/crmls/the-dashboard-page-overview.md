# The Dashboard Page Overview

# Navigating to the Dashboard

You can navigate to the Dashboard from anywhere in the platform.
1. Click **Dashboard** in the top navigation bar.
2. Click **Manage Widgets** to customize your layout.

# **Dashboard Widgets**

Widgets are tiles that can be customized on your dashboard.

## Hot Sheets

Track real-time market activity based on Saved Searches or property types.
- Click a status change to generate a filtered results view.
- Review listings tied to activity such as **Price Drop** or **Coming Soon**.

Hot Sheets update automatically based on current MLS data.
​

## **Presentations Widget**

Quickly access and manage your **Listing Presentations**:
- **Review Past Work**: View recent presentations with one click.
- **Create with Confidence**: Edit an existing presentation directly from the dashboard.

## **Contacts Widget**

Stay connected to your clients with an organized view of your **Contacts**:
- **Sort with Ease**: Filter by **Recently Created** or **New** to stay proactive.
- **Find Fast**: Use the search bar to instantly locate a contact.

## **Saved Searches Widget**

Keep your most important searches at your fingertips:
- **Stay Organized**: Sort by **Name** or **Recently Updated**.

## **Listings Widget**

Track all active listings across your MLS:
- **All-in-One View**: Quickly see an overview of listings in your region.
- **Sort Listings Your Way**: Filter by address or quick options like **Price Drop** and **Open Houses**.

## DOM Widget

Quickly view the median Days on Market over the span of the last three years
- **Quickly Review Data**: Hover your mouse over the chart to view the median days on market per month.

## Access Integrated Tools

Launch trusted real estate tools without leaving your workflow. Hover over the right side of the screen to access integrations, such as: You may be prompted to log in with credentials.
- **RPR** – Dive deeper into property data with Real Property Resource.
- **ShowingTime** – Schedule and manage showings without leaving Perchwell.
- **SentriLock** – Control secure property access with built-in SentriLock integration

## **Listings Widget**

Access listing management tools directly from the Listings Widget. Click the **pencil icon** next to an eligible listing to open the **Add/Edit** form.
