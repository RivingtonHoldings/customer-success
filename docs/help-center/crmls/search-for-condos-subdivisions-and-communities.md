# Search for Condos, Subdivisions, and Communities

Use this article to search for condos and find listings in a specific condo complex, subdivision, or community.

# Search for condos by Property Type and subtype

Residential attached, a subtype under the Residential-Single Family property type, includes condos and other homes that share a common wall.

## Steps:
1. Click Property Type in the upper left of the Search page
2. Select Residential-Single Family
3. Click the Property sub type field
4. Select Condominium

Your search results update to show attached homes, including condos.

# Find listings in a specific condo complex, subdivision, or community

Find listings in a specific condo complex, subdivision, or community with the Subdivision/Condo filter.

## Steps:
1. Click Filters in the upper right of the Search page
2. Enter Subdivision/Condo in the Search filters and values field
3. Click the Subdivision/Condo field
4. Enter part of the complex or subdivision name in the search field at the top of the list
5. Select the name from the list of matches
