# Log In to the Perchwell Mobile App

Use this article, as a CRMLS member, to download the Perchwell mobile app, log in with your ReCore account, and change your login email address.

# Download the Perchwell mobile app

Download the Perchwell mobile app from the App Store on an iPhone or from Google Play on an Android phone. Search for Perchwell in either store.

# Log in to the Perchwell mobile app with your RECore account

Log in to the Perchwell mobile app with your REcore account. Perchwell logs you in with single sign-on (SSO), which means your REcore username and password are your Perchwell login. There is no separate Perchwell password.

## Steps:
1. Open the Perchwell mobile app
2. Enter the email address from your REcore portal in the Email field
3. Tap Continue
4. Log in on the REcore portal login page with your username and password

The Perchwell mobile app opens to the Search page.

# Change your Perchwell login email address

Change your Perchwell login email address in your RECore portal before you log in to the mobile app. Your Perchwell account is tied to that email address. If you have trouble updating it, contact CRMLS MLS staff.

# Get help logging in to the Perchwell mobile app

Contact CRMLS staff if you can't log in to the Perchwell mobile app. [Login and Troubleshooting FAQ](https://support.perchwell.com/baldwin/en/articles/14710265-login-access-troubleshooting-guide-and-faq) covers more fixes for login problems.

Invited clients log in with the password they create from their agent's invitation. [Accept Your Agent's Perchwell Invitation](https://support.perchwell.com/crmls/en/articles/18231917-accept-your-agent-s-perchwell-invitation) explains how.
