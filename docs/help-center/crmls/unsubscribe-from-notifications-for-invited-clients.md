# Unsubscribe from Notifications (For Invited Clients)

Unsubscribe when your home search ends or you no longer want Perchwell notifications.

## Email alert unsubscribe

Use the **Unsubscribe** link in any Perchwell search alert email.

### Steps:
1. Open a recent search alert email
2. Scroll to the bottom of the message
  - If you do not see the footer, expand the email
3. Click **Unsubscribe**

**💡 After you unsubscribe, Perchwell stops sending email notifications to your inbox.**

# Tag alert toggle

Turn off alerts for specific tags from **Tags**.

### Steps:
1. Log in to Perchwell
2. Click **Tags**
3. Click the bell icon next to a tag to turn alerts off

## Tips
- Contact your agent if you want your account removed.
- Ask your agent to send a new invitation if you restart your search later.
