# Filtering Listing by Office, MLS, or Your Listings on the Dashboard

# **Navigating to the Listings Widget**

The Listings widget gives you a real-time snapshot of active market activity—perfect for quick check-ins between client conversations.
1. Click **Dashboard** in the top navigation menu.
2. Locate the **Listings** widget on your Dashboard.

> 💡 The Listings widget is included on your Dashboard by default and cannot be removed.

## **Adjusting Your Listings View**

Use the controls at the top of the widget to tailor what you’re seeing.
1. Use the **List Date** dropdown to switch between **Sales** and **Rentals**.
2. Adjust the **Scope** dropdown to view activity by:
  - **MLS**
  - **Brokerage**
  - **Office**

> 💡 Available scopes may vary based on your listing ownership and brokerage permissions.

## **Filtering Listings by Status**

Narrow your view using listing status tabs within the widget.
- Filter listings by **Active**, **Recently Added**, or **Open House**.
- Listings refresh automatically based on your selected filter.
- Results display in chronological order, with the most recent activity shown first.

## **Quick Actions from the Listings Widget**

Take action without leaving your Dashboard.
- Click any listing to open the **Listing Detail Page** for deeper insights and next steps.
- Quickly use the **tag icon** to save listings you want to review later.

## **Tips**
- The Listings widget updates in real time as market data changes.
- Filters and scopes can be adjusted at any time.
- Use the Listing widget for quick market awareness and fast access to your own listings.
