# Export Listings in Perchwell

# Export panel access

Export options live in the upper-right corner of **Search**.

### Steps:
1. Go to **Search**
2. Apply filters to define the listings you want to export
3. Click **Export**

The **Export** panel opens.

# Quick Export

Use Quick Export when you need a one-time file.
- Review the export options in the **Export** panel.
- Click **Export** to generate the CSV.

A confirmation message appears, and the CSV is sent to the email address associated with your account.

# Export templates

Templates control which fields appear as columns in your CSV.

[![image](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/trq7czv5/2242551411/691081aebefd10ad76a404076d29/export+templates.png)](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/trq7czv5/2242551411/691081aebefd10ad76a404076d29/export+templates.png?expires=1788542100&signature=57b3a8c7bb1c24935137ba3c0d5fed73f71c5ec63dc1915396b245462c5cb8db&req=diIjFMx7nIVeWPMW1HO4zeaWs7ThxB7RN1%2FVpQRBe%2B4bgLRXE%2FLG9r%2FFbak7%0A50QSswm67XagfVGxyhg%3D%0A)

# Template creation

### Steps:
1. In the **Export** page, click the **+** icon next to **Templates**
2. Enter a name for your template
3. Select the listing fields to include in the export
4. Drag and drop fields to reorder the columns
5. Click **Save Template**

To make the template your default, turn on **Set as default list**.

# Template export

### Steps:
1. Open the **Export** panel
2. Select your saved template
3. Click **Export**

A confirmation message appears, and the CSV is sent to the email address associated with your account.

## Tips
- Include any available listing field in a template.
- Create as many templates as you need for different workflows.
