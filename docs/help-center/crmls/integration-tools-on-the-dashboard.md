# Integration Tools on the Dashboard

**Description**

Access third-party tools connected to your MLS directly from Perchwell. Available integrations can be launched from the Dashboard or from relevant listing pages.

# Access Integrations from the Dashboard

The Integrations panel is available on the Dashboard.

## Steps
1. Click **Dashboard** in the top navigation bar.
2. Hover over the right side of the screen to reveal the **Integrations** panel.
3. Select an integration to open it in a new browser tab.

> 💡 Access depends on your MLS permissions and may require a separate log in credentials.

# Available Integrations

The following integrations may be available based on active subscriptions:

## Realist

Provides access to public records, tax data, and property history. You may be redirected through your MLS portal to access Realist.

## ShowingTime

Used to schedule, manage, and track property showings. For active listings, ShowingTime may also be accessible directly from the listing detail page.

## SentriLock

Provides access to lockbox and access management tools.

## RPR

Access Realtor Property Resource for property data, reports, and market information.

## Down Payment Resource

Provides information about down payment assistance programs available for eligible properties.

## Cloud CMA

Launch Cloud CMA to create comparative market analyses and listing presentations.

# Tips
- Available integrations depend on your MLS and user permissions.
- Some tools may require a separate login.
- Integrations open in a new browser tab.
- Access to certain tools may be managed through your MLS portal.
