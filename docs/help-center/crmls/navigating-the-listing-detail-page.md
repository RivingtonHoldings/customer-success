## **Listing Header**

Located at the top of the page, the listing header provides essential property information at a quick glance, including:
- Full Listing Address
- Bedrooms
- Bathrooms
- Square Footage & Price per Sq. Ft.
- Acreage

## **Listing Summary**

The **Listing Summary** appears on the right-hand side of the page. Use this section to navigate quickly between key property details and tools.

## **Action Panel**

Hover over the **Action Panel** on the right side to access additional actions and integrations designed to streamline your workflow.

## Listing Tools
- **Contact Agent** Instantly connect with the listing agent.
- **Share**
  - **Start Message Thread** – Share a property directly with clients. It will appear in their *Messages* tab once they’ve been invited or have Perchwell access.
  - **Share Listing / Copy Link** – Send listings via email or copy the link to share externally (e.g., text).
  - **Share via Facebook or Twitter** – Post listings directly to your social media pages.
- **Add Tag** Tag a listing to receive alerts for price or status changes. [Learn more about Tags here.](https://intercom.help/perchwell/en/articles/8955804-how-to-create-and-edit-tags)
- **Print** Print the full listing page in its current format.
- **One Pager** Add listings to a one-page report for quick sharing.
- **Add to Report** Start creating a report directly from the listing page. You can add more listings using tags.
- **Report Listing** If you notice anything inaccurate or suspicious, report it to our team for review.
- **Add to Listing Presentation** Add this property to a new or existing Listing Presentation.
- **Auto CMA** Instantly generate a CMA report using this property as the subject.
- **Edit Listing** If you’re the listing agent, open the listing form directly to make edits or updates.

## **Listing Information**

Property details are displayed in **collapsible tiles**, allowing you to expand, hide, or print sections as needed for clients or personal reference.

- Showing details

## **Interior Section**

Find all interior features, including levels, living area details, room breakdowns, and appliance information.

## **Exterior Section**

View exterior and architectural details, such as structure type, pool features, fencing, parking, and style.

## **Land & Lot**

Review land and lot information, including MLS area, county, tax ID, year built, lot features, zoning, and more.

## **Location Section**

Explore the property’s surroundings with interactive map views and street imagery.
- **Map Layers** – Switch between default, satellite, or terrain views.
- **Street View** – View real-time images of the property from street level.
- **Directions** – Get directions instantly via Google Maps.

## **History Section**

Track every change made to a listing during its time on the market. The **History Section** includes two parts:
​

## **Listing History Details**

Displays all major status and price changes.Key prices and statuses may differ slightly from the change log details.

>  💡 **Key prices and statuses may differ slightly from the change log details.**

## **Change Log Details**

Shows additional changes that occurred while the listing was active.

Each entry includes:
1. **Date** – When the change took effect
2. **Type** – The nature of the update (e.g., status change)
3. **Price** – Listing price at that time
4. **DOM** – *Days on Market* when the change was made
5. **Changed By** – The user who applied the change

## **Mortgage & Taxes**

Estimate affordability with built-in **Monthly** and **Lifetime** payment calculators. These tools help you set clear financial expectations with your clients and ensure every property aligns with their goals and budget.
