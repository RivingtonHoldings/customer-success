# Adding a New Chart in Analytics

## **Types of Charts You Can Create**

Perchwell allows you to create several types of charts, including:
- **Market Share**
- **Days on Market**
- **Price**
- **Property Type**

Each chart type is designed to visualize specific kinds of data effectively, with formats like bar, line, or pie charts depending on the data.

## **Data Selection for Charts**

### **Setting Filters**
1. **Main Search Page:** Begin by setting your desired filters on the main search page. These filters dictate the data that will be visualized in your chart.
2. **Multiple Sources:** You can use data from multiple sources or filters within Perchwell for a comprehensive view.

## **Creating a New Chart**

### **Step-by-Step Process**

![image](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/964655574/fe639c74810a49689eed439e/Group+209.png)
1. **Navigate to Analytics:** After setting your filters, click on the ‘Analytics’ tab.
2. **Select Chart Type:** Choose from the available chart types based on the insights you need.
3. **Customization:** While the data displayed is customizable through filters, the color schemes and labels align with the company's branding.

## **Saving and Accessing Charts**
- **Automatic Saving:** Once your search is saved, your charts are automatically saved and updated with the latest market data.
- **Accessing Charts:** Find your charts anytime in the 'Analytics' section.

## Sharing **and Downloading Charts**

![image](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/964658641/36f065dcd1e12e489af423d9/Group+210+%281%29.png)
- **Export Functionality:** Easily share via email and download your charts as a PDF, ready for inclusion in reports or presentations.

## **Avoiding Common Mistakes**
- **Accurate Filters:** Ensure that your filters on the main search page accurately reflect the data you want to visualize.

## **Best Practices for Chart Creation**
- **Regular Filter Updates:** Keep your filters updated to reflect current market trends for the most relevant insights.
- **Diverse Chart Usage:** Utilize different types of charts to get a comprehensive understanding of the market.
