# Filter Your Search by Commute

# Open the **Commute** filter

Access **Commute** from the **Filters** menu or from the **Location** column on the **Search** page.

#### From the **Filters** Tab:
1. Go to the **Search** page
2. On the Map, click the commute filter icon.witch to the **Filters** tab

## Set your commute criteria

Enter the details below to define your search area. The map updates to highlight the area that matches your commute time.

**Complete these fields:**

| **Travel mode** | Choose **Driving**, **Walking**, or **Biking**. |
|---|---|
| **Max commute time** | Enter the longest commute you want. |
| **Origin** | Type the starting location for your commute (for example, your office or school). |
| **Day** | Select the day you plan to commute. |
| **Departure time** | Set the time you typically leave. |

## Tips
- Add multiple commute filters to compare different origin locations.
- Combine **Commute** with other filters such as price, bedrooms, and status.
- Review the highlighted map area to confirm it matches your commute time and travel mode.
