# Share and Export Analytics Charts

# Share and export use cases

Share charts when you need to send market visuals to someone else or add them to a deliverable.
- Add charts to a CMA or listing report
- Send market insights to a client
- Share findings with a colleague or team member
- Reuse charts in presentations and other marketing materials

# Prepare your charts to be shared

Confirm your search results match what you want your chart to represent.

### Steps:
1. Go to **Search**
2. Apply your filters
3. Review your results to confirm the listings match your dataset
4. Click the **Analytics** tab

Share directly from the chart.

### Steps:
1. On the **Analytics** page, find the chart you want to share
2. Click **Share**
3. Select **Portrait** or **Landscape**
4. Choose how you want to share:
  - **Email Report**
  - **Export PDF**

## Email a report

Use **Email Report** when you want to send the chart directly.
- Enter one or more recipient email addresses
- Send the report

## Export a PDF

Use **Export PDF** when you want to download the chart and attach it to another document.
- Save the PDF to your computer
- Add it to a CMA, report, or presentation

# What your recipient receives

When you export a PDF, it includes:
- The **chart visualization**
- Your **brokerage branding**
- Your **contact information**

# Common ways to reuse exported charts

Export PDFs to use outside of Perchwell.
- Attach charts to **CMAs**
- Include charts in **listing reports**
- Add charts to presentations for team discussions or strategy meetings
