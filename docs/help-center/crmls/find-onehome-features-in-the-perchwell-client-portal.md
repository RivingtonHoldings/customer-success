# Find OneHome Features in the Perchwell Client Portal

Use this article to find what your clients get in the Perchwell client portal in place of each OneHome feature, and what to do for each one.

# OneHome features and their Perchwell client portal matches

Clients moving from OneHome look for the same tools. This table lists each OneHome feature with its Perchwell client portal match.

| OneHome feature | Perchwell client portal match | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Clients open OneHome from your email and browse without an account | Clients open shared listings by link, email, or text with no login. Invited clients also get Messages and their own searches | Invite clients from the Contacts page by adding each contact manually, or bulk upload a CSV (comma-separated values) file of up to 100 contacts at a time. |
| Activate my OneHome account | Accept the Perchwell invitation | Ask the client to accept the invitation email. |
| Favorite / Not For Me | Like, Maybe, or Reject on listings you share in Messages | See the client's reactions in Shared properties. |
| Listing set | Tags, or listings shared in Messages | Tag the listings or share them in Messages. |
| Add a Note for the agent | Messages, in real time and also emailed to you | Ask clients to message you about a listing. |
| Agent-saved searches the client cannot edit | A shared saved search the client opens and edits. Client edits sync to your copy | Choose what the client changes. Perchwell does not notify you when a client edits a shared search. |
| Request a Tour | The client shares the listing with you in Messages or by email | Ask clients to message you to book a showing. |
| Recent OneHome visitors and last portal visit | Client activity on the Tags page (views, likes, dislikes) and the Contacts page (logins and shared listings) | Open Tags or Contacts to check client activity. |
| OneHome mobile app | Perchwell mobile app, with Perchwell branding | Ask the client to accept the invitation in a browser first, then log in to the app. |

- [Message Clients in Perchwell](https://support.perchwell.com/crmls/en/articles/18232897-message-clients-in-perchwell) explains how to share listings and read client reactions in Messages.
